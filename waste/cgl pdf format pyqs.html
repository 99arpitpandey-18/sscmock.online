📖 Detailed Explanations (Tricky Ones)
Q2 (Reasoning - Coding):
Pattern: C→F (+3), O→R (+3), L→P (+4), D→E (+1).
Apply to WARM: W→Z (+3), A→D (+3), R→V (+4), M→N (+1).
✅ Answer: ZDVN (Option C)
Q11 (Reasoning - BODMAS):
8 @ 3 # 4 % 6 $ 2 → 8 × 3 - 4 + 6 ÷ 2
= 24 - 4 + 3 = 23 (Option B)
Q51 (Quant - Partnership):
P = 90,000×8 = 7,20,000 ; Q = 1,50,000×5 = 7,50,000.
Ratio = 720:750 = 24:25.
Q's share = (25/49)×50,000 = ₹25,510 (Option D)
Q53 (Quant - Average):
Multiples of 13 between 150-300: 156 (13×12) to 299 (13×23).
Total terms = 12. Average = (156+299)/2 = 455/2 = 227.5 (Option B)
Q58 (Quant - Simplification - IMPORTANT):
Yahan '81 3' ka matlab 8⅓ (8 + 1/3 = 25/3) hai.
Solve inner: (5/6 - 1/6) = 2/3. (1/3 × 2/3) = 2/9.
(2/5 - 2/9) = 8/45. (7/9 ÷ 8/45) = 35/8.
Final: 25/3 - 35/8 = (200 - 105)/24 = 95/24 (Option C)
Q61 (Quant - Profit/Loss):
Effective selling units = 1200. Discounted price = 110×0.85 = ₹93.5.
Offer: 3 free on 10 => Paid units = 1200 × (10/13) = 923.07.
Revenue = 923.07×93.5 = ₹86,307. Loss = 1,20,000 - 86,307 = ₹33,692.
Loss% = (33692/120000)×100 = 28.08% (Option C)
Q67 (Quant - Relative Speed):
M + N = 480/5 = 96. M - N = 480/15 = 32.
Solving: 2M = 128 => M = 64 km/h (Option B)
Q71 (Quant - Discount):
Let CP = x. MP = 1.7x. After 20% = 1.36x. After 15% = 1.156x.
1.156x = 39312 => x = 39312 / 1.156 = ₹34,000 (Option B)
Q77 (English - Passive Voice):
"Have been ignoring" (Present Perfect Continuous) ka Passive:
"Has been being ignored" - though uncommon, grammatically correct.
✅ Option D
Q83 (English - Error Detection):
Subject "That the report overlooked..." (Singular) ke saath verb "were" galat hai.
Sahi hoga "was surprising". Error part (2). ✅ Option B
Q100 (English - Homonym):
