1. Sentence Structure क्या होता है?

Sentence Structure का अर्थ है sentence में words को एक सही grammatical order में arrange करना।

दूसरे शब्दों में, sentence के अंदर Subject, Verb, Object, Complement और Adverb किस क्रम में आएंगे, यही Sentence Structure है।

⭐ सबसे Important Rule

English में केवल सही words जानना पर्याप्त नहीं है। उन्हें सही order में रखना भी जरूरी है।

Rahul plays cricket.

Rahul = Subject

plays = Verb

cricket = Object

❌ Rahul cricket plays.

✅ Rahul plays cricket.

English में normal affirmative sentence में Subject के बाद सामान्यतः Verb आता है।

2. Sentence के मुख्य Elements

Element Meaning Example
Subject कौन काम कर रहा है? Rahul
Verb Action / State plays
Object Action किस पर हो रहा है? cricket
Complement Subject/Object के बारे में information happy
Adverb कब, कहाँ, कैसे आदि quickly

3. Subject क्या है?

Sentence में जो व्यक्ति, चीज या noun action करता है या जिसके बारे में sentence है, उसे Subject कहते हैं।

Rahul plays cricket.

She is reading.

The boy opened the door.

My father works in Delhi.

🔥 SSC Trick

Verb से सवाल पूछो: Who? या What?

जो answer मिले, वह अक्सर Subject होता है।

4. Predicate क्या है?

Subject के बारे में जो कुछ बताया जाता है, उसे Predicate कहते हैं।

Rahul plays cricket.

She is very intelligent.

The boy opened the door.

Sentence = Subject + Predicate

5. Object क्या है?

Verb का action जिस व्यक्ति या चीज पर होता है, उसे Object कहते हैं।

Rahul plays cricket.

She reads a book.

He bought a car.

Direct Object

She opened the door.

He ate an apple.

Indirect Object

He gave me a book.

She sent her brother a letter.

6. Complement क्या है?

Complement वह word या phrase है जो Subject या Object के बारे में आवश्यक information देता है।

Rahul is a doctor.

She became happy.

They elected him captain.

Subject Complement

He is honest.

She became a teacher.

Object Complement

They made him captain.

We found the question difficult.

7. Basic Sentence Structure

⭐ Basic Formula

Subject + Verb + Object

Rahul plays cricket.

She reads books.

They watch movies.

He drives a car.

8. SV Structure – Subject + Verb

कुछ verbs को object की आवश्यकता नहीं होती। ऐसे sentence में Subject के बाद Verb आता है।

Formula:

Subject + Verb

Birds fly.

Children laughed.

The baby cried.

The sun rises.

9. SVC Structure – Subject + Verb + Complement

Formula:

Subject + Linking Verb + Complement

Rahul is happy.

She became a doctor.

The sky looks beautiful.

He seems tired.

🔥 Common Linking Verbs

be, become, seem, look, feel, appear, remain, grow, turn

10. SVO Structure – Subject + Verb + Object

Ram eats rice.

She reads a book.

He opened the door.

They play cricket.

SSC पहचान

अगर Verb के बाद यह पूछा जा सके What? या Whom? और answer मिले, तो वह Object हो सकता है।

11. SVOO Structure – Subject + Verb + Indirect Object + Direct Object

Formula:

Subject + Verb + Indirect Object + Direct Object

He gave me a book.

She sent her friend a message.

Father bought me a bicycle.

यहाँ पहले object से पूछा जा सकता है to whom? या for whom?

12. SVOC Structure – Subject + Verb + Object + Complement

Formula:

Subject + Verb + Object + Object Complement

They made him captain.

We found the book interesting.

The teacher considered him intelligent.

13. Adverbial Structure

Adverb sentence में action के बारे में how, when, where, why आदि information देता है।

He runs quickly.

She came yesterday.

They live in Delhi.

He works very hard.

Common Adverb Positions

Type Question Example
Manner How? He works carefully.
Place Where? He lives in Delhi.
Time When? He came yesterday.
Reason Why? He left because he was tired.

14. Phrase क्या है?

Words का ऐसा group जिसमें subject और finite verb की complete combination नहीं होती, उसे Phrase कहते हैं।

in the morning

after the class

to win the match

a beautiful girl

Phrase = incomplete group of words

15. Clause क्या है?

Words का ऐसा group जिसमें सामान्यतः Subject + Verb होता है, उसे Clause कहते हैं।

when he came

because she was ill

who lives in Delhi

Independent Clause

जो clause अकेले complete meaning दे सकता है।

He went home.

She was tired.

Dependent Clause

जो clause अकेले complete meaning नहीं देता।

because he was tired

when she arrived

16. Simple Sentence

Simple sentence में एक independent clause होता है।

Formula:

One Independent Clause

Rahul plays cricket.

She is happy.

The boy opened the door.

They went to Delhi.

17. Compound Sentence

Compound sentence में दो या अधिक independent clauses coordinating conjunction से जुड़े होते हैं।

Formula:

Independent Clause + Coordinating Conjunction + Independent Clause

Rahul worked hard and he passed the exam.

I wanted to go but it was raining.

Hurry up or you will miss the bus.

🔥 FANBOYS

For – And – Nor – But – Or – Yet – So

18. Complex Sentence

Complex sentence में एक independent clause और कम से कम एक dependent clause होता है।

Formula:

Independent Clause + Dependent Clause

I stayed home because it was raining.

When he arrived, we started the meeting.

She knows that he is honest.

19. Compound-Complex Sentence

इसमें कम से कम दो independent clauses और एक dependent clause होता है।

I went home because I was tired, and my brother stayed there.

She studied hard because she wanted to pass, and she cleared the examination.

⭐ Exam Trick

Independent clauses की संख्या और dependent clause की presence देखकर sentence type identify करें।

20. English में Word Order

सबसे Basic Order:

Subject → Verb → Object

Rahul → reads → books.

She → bought → a dress.

They → played → cricket.

Adjective का Position

Adjective सामान्यतः noun से पहले आता है।

a beautiful girl

an intelligent student

a large house

Adverb का Position

He runs quickly.

She always helps me.

He speaks English fluently.

21. Common Errors – SSC

Incorrect Correct Reason
He cricket plays. He plays cricket. Normal order = Subject + Verb + Object.
She beautiful is. She is beautiful. Linking verb comes before complement.
He quickly runs. He runs quickly. Basic manner adverb placement.
I yesterday met him. I met him yesterday. Natural time-adverb position.
He gave a book me. He gave me a book. Indirect object generally precedes direct object.
She is a girl intelligent. She is an intelligent girl. Adjective generally comes before noun.
Because he was ill. He stayed home. Because he was ill, he stayed home. Dependent clause cannot normally stand alone.
He went market. He went to the market. Appropriate preposition/article needed.
She sings beautiful. She sings beautifully. Verb is modified by an adverb.
He did not went there. He did not go there. After did, use base form of verb.

22. SSC Sentence Structure Tricks

🔥 Trick 1 – Basic Formula

Subject + Verb + Object

🔥 Trick 2 – Subject पहचानो

Verb से Who? या What? पूछो।

🔥 Trick 3 – Object पहचानो

Verb से What? या Whom? पूछो।

🔥 Trick 4 – Linking Verb

be, seem, become, look, feel आदि के बाद अक्सर complement आता है।

🔥 Trick 5 – Adjective

Adjective सामान्यतः noun से पहले आता है।

a beautiful house ✔

🔥 Trick 6 – Adverb

Verb को modify करने के लिए अक्सर adverb का प्रयोग होता है।

He runs quickly. ✔

🔥 Trick 7 – Sentence Type

One independent clause → Simple

Two independent clauses → Compound

Independent + Dependent → Complex

23. SSC PYQ-Based / Exam Pattern Practice

Question 1 SSC Pattern

Choose the correct sentence.

A. He cricket plays.
B. He plays cricket.
C. Cricket he plays.
D. Plays he cricket.
Answer: B. He plays cricket.

Basic English structure = Subject + Verb + Object.

Question 2 SSC Pattern

Identify the Subject in the sentence:

Rahul reads a newspaper.

A. reads
B. a newspaper
C. Rahul
D. newspaper
Answer: C. Rahul

Rahul action करने वाला Subject है।

Question 3 SSC Pattern

Identify the Object:

She bought a new dress.

A. She
B. bought
C. a new dress
D. new
Answer: C. a new dress

Bought का action किस चीज पर हुआ? A new dress.

Question 4 SSC Pattern

Identify the sentence structure:

Birds fly.

A. SVO
B. SVC
C. SV
D. SVOO
Answer: C. SV

Birds = Subject, fly = Verb.

Question 5 SSC Pattern

Identify the sentence structure:

She is happy.

A. SV
B. SVC
C. SVO
D. SVOO
Answer: B. SVC

She = Subject, is = Linking Verb, happy = Subject Complement.

Question 6 SSC Pattern

Identify the sentence type:

Rahul worked hard and he passed the exam.

A. Simple
B. Compound
C. Complex
D. Phrase
Answer: B. Compound

दो independent clauses को “and” ने जोड़ा है।

Question 7 SSC Pattern

Identify the sentence type:

I stayed home because it was raining.

A. Simple
B. Compound
C. Complex
D. Compound-Complex
Answer: C. Complex

एक independent clause और एक dependent clause है।

Question 8 SSC Pattern

Choose the correct sentence.

A. She sings beautiful.
B. She beautiful sings.
C. She sings beautifully.
D. Beautifully she singing.
Answer: C. She sings beautifully.

Sings एक verb है और उसे modify करने के लिए adverb “beautifully” चाहिए।

Question 9 SSC Pattern

Choose the correct sentence.

A. He gave a book me.
B. He me gave a book.
C. He gave me a book.
D. He a book gave me.
Answer: C. He gave me a book.

SVOO structure में indirect object “me” direct object “a book” से पहले आया है।

Question 10 SSC Pattern

Identify the phrase:

The boy in the room is my brother.

A. The boy
B. in the room
C. is
D. my brother
Answer: B. in the room

“in the room” एक prepositional phrase है।

Error Detection Practice

Find the error:

Correct: She sings beautifully.

Find the error:

24. Quick Revision – Sentence Structure

Rule 2 Basic Order = S + V + O
Rule 3 SV = Subject + Verb
Rule 4 SVC = Subject + Verb + Complement
Rule 5 SVO = Subject + Verb + Object
Rule 6 SVOO = Subject + Verb + IO + DO
Rule 7 SVOC = Subject + Verb + Object + Complement
Rule 8 Phrase = No complete Subject + Finite Verb combination
Rule 9 Clause = Subject + Verb
Rule 10 Simple = One Independent Clause
Rule 11 Compound = Independent + Independent
Rule 12 Complex = Independent + Dependent
Rule 13 Adjective → generally before noun
Rule 14 Verb → generally followed by object when transitive
Rule 15 Adverb → modifies verb/adjective/adverb
Rule 16 Linking Verb → often followed by Complement
🎯 SSC Sentence Structure Final Checklist
  • Basic English order = Subject + Verb + Object.
  • Subject = action करने वाला / sentence का main topic.
  • Object = action का receiver.
  • Complement Subject/Object के बारे में information देता है।
  • SV = Subject + Verb.
  • SVC = Subject + Verb + Complement.
  • SVO = Subject + Verb + Object.
  • SVOO = Subject + Verb + Indirect Object + Direct Object.
  • SVOC = Subject + Verb + Object + Complement.
  • Phrase में complete clause structure नहीं होता।
  • Clause में Subject + Verb होता है।
  • One independent clause → Simple.
  • Two independent clauses → Compound.
  • Independent + Dependent → Complex.
  • Adjective generally noun से पहले आता है।
  • Verb को modify करने के लिए Adverb का प्रयोग होता है।
  • Sentence में word order को ध्यान से check करें।
