📊 RRB NTPC (Non-Technical Popular Categories)

🎓 Eligibility12th pass (for undergraduate posts) / Graduate (for graduate posts) as per notification.
📝 Exam Pattern2-stage CBT (1st & 2nd) + Typing/Computer Skill (for some posts).
📚 Core SubjectsGeneral Awareness, Maths, Reasoning, General Science, Computer.

🏥 Medical Standards — NTPC

NTPC posts generally do not have physical efficiency tests but require medical fitness as per Railway medical standards (A1, B1, etc.).

PostMedical CategoryRemarks
Commercial, Ticket Examiner, etc.A1 / B1Good vision, colour vision, general fitness
Stenographer, ClerkC1Normal vision, medical fitness
✅ Medical standards vary by post. Always refer to official notification for exact requirements.

📚 Preparation Strategy

  • Focus on GK (static + current affairs) daily
  • Maths: practice speed and accuracy
  • Reasoning: solve puzzles and logical sets
  • Take regular mocks and analyze weak areas

📖 Important Resources

  • NCERT Science (6-10) for basics
  • Lucent GK / Manorama Yearbook
  • Quantitative Aptitude (R.S. Aggarwal)
  • Previous year papers (PYQs)
Official reference: Indian Railways / RRB recruitment notifications. Always check latest RRB notification for exact details.

🏃 Physical Efficiency Test (PET) — Group D

PET is qualifying in nature. Candidates must clear these standards.

GenderRaceDistanceTime
MaleRunning1000 meters4 minutes 30 seconds
FemaleRunning800 meters4 minutes 30 seconds
Male (Ex-servicemen)Running1000 meters5 minutes
Female (Ex-servicemen)Running800 meters5 minutes
⚠️ PET is qualifying; candidates must meet the time limit. Some posts may also have weight lifting or other physical tests – check notification.

📚 Preparation Strategy

  • Science: focus on 10th level NCERT
  • Maths: practice quick calculations
  • Reasoning: daily practice of puzzles
  • GK: read newspapers and monthly compilations

🏃 Physical Preparation

  • Start running daily 2-3 months before
  • Practice timed runs to achieve qualifying time
  • Maintain general fitness and stamina
Official reference: RRB Group D notifications. Always refer to the latest notification for exact standards.

🏥 Medical Standards — ALP

ALP requires high medical fitness (A1 category).

ParameterRequirement
Vision6/6 in both eyes (without glasses) – colour vision normal
HeightMinimum 165 cm (relaxation for SC/ST as per rules)
ChestMinimum 80 cm (unexpanded) with expansion
GeneralNo physical deformities; good hearing
⚠️ Medical standards are stringent for ALP. Get a thorough check-up before applying.

📚 Technical Preparation

  • Focus on core subjects: Electrical, Mechanical, Electronics as per your trade
  • Use standard textbooks and previous technical papers
  • Practice numericals and conceptual questions

🎯 Aptitude (CBAT)

  • Practice memory and observation tests
  • Develop quick decision making
  • Take mock CBAT online
Official reference: RRB ALP notifications. Always check latest notification for updates.
