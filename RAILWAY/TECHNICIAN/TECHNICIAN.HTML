Exam Overview

RRB Technician क्या है?

Indian Railways में technical maintenance और railway systems से जुड़े Technician posts के लिए recruitment.

🚆 Technician Recruitment

RRB Technician recruitment में अलग-अलग technical categories के लिए candidates का selection Computer Based Test, Document Verification और Medical Examination जैसे stages के माध्यम से किया जाता है।

  • Technician Grade-I Signal
  • Technician Grade-III
  • Technical / Trade-oriented preparation
  • Computer Based Objective Examination
  • Document Verification
  • Railway Medical Examination

📋 Eligibility Snapshot

Technician Grade-III Relevant trade में Matriculation/SSLC + ITI या notification में निर्धारित equivalent qualification.
Technician Grade-I Signal Relevant discipline में prescribed Diploma / Degree / technical qualification के अनुसार eligibility.
Age Age limit recruitment notification/CEN के अनुसार निर्धारित होती है।
Important Exact trade और qualification हमेशा जिस CEN में vacancy निकले, उसी से verify करें।

Technical Preparation

Technician Trade Preparation

अपनी post और notification में निर्धारित relevant trade के technical concepts को priority के साथ तैयार करें।

⚡ Electrician Electrical basics, wiring, machines, current, voltage, resistance और safety concepts.
🔧 Fitter Tools, fitting operations, measurements, workshop practice और mechanical basics.
🔩 Welder Welding processes, tools, safety, joints और workshop fundamentals.
📡 Electronics / Signal Electronic components, circuits, communication और signal-related fundamentals.
🖥️ Electronics Mechanic Components, digital electronics, instruments और troubleshooting basics.
🚗 Mechanic Mechanical systems, tools, maintenance और relevant trade fundamentals.
💡 Wireman Wiring, electrical installation, circuits और safety principles.
⚙️ Other Relevant Trades जिस Technician post के लिए apply करें, उसी CEN में prescribed trade list को follow करें।
