Typing Skill Test (TST) – Qualifying
  • Applicable for: Senior Clerk cum Typist & Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist
  • Qualifying speed: 30 WPM (English) or 25 WPM (Hindi)
  • Fonts: Kruti Dev & Mangal for Hindi typing
  • Exemption: PwBD candidates (blindness / low vision / CP / locomotor disability ≥40%)
  • Selection: Shortlisted = 8× vacancies per category (based on CBT 2 scores)
  • Final merit: Based on CBT 2 scores; TST is qualifying only
