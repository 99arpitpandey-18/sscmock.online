1. Pronoun क्या होता है?

Pronoun वह word है जो किसी noun के स्थान पर प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Pronoun = संज्ञा (Noun) के स्थान पर आने वाला शब्द।

📌 Definition A pronoun is a word used instead of a noun.

Pronoun का मुख्य उद्देश्य noun की repetition को avoid करना है।

Example

Rahul is a good boy. Rahul goes to school every day.

यहाँ Rahul बार-बार आया है।

इसे pronoun की मदद से लिखेंगे:

Correct: Rahul is a good boy. He goes to school every day.

यहाँ He = Rahul

More Examples

  • Riya is intelligent. She is my friend.
  • Mohan has a car. He drives it every day.
  • The book is expensive. It is imported.
  • Ram and Shyam are friends. They play together.

3. Personal Pronouns

Personal pronouns किसी person, animal या thing को refer करते हैं।

Person Subject Object Possessive
First Person Singular I me my / mine
First Person Plural we us our / ours
Second Person you you your / yours
Third Person Male he him his
Third Person Female she her her / hers
Third Person Thing it it its
Third Person Plural they them their / theirs
⭐ SSC Rule Subject pronoun का प्रयोग subject के रूप में होता है।
Object pronoun का प्रयोग object के रूप में होता है।

Examples

  • I am a student.
  • Rahul helped me.
  • She is intelligent.
  • The teacher praised him.
  • They invited us.

4. Possessive Pronouns

Possessive pronouns ownership या possession दिखाते हैं।

Possessive Pronouns: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs

Important Difference

Possessive Adjective Possessive Pronoun
my mine
your yours
our ours
their theirs
her hers
Examples: This is my book.
This book is mine.

That is her car.
That car is hers.
❌ Common Error This book is my.
❌ Wrong

This book is mine.
✅ Correct

5. Reflexive Pronouns

जब subject और object एक ही व्यक्ति हों, तब reflexive pronoun का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Person Reflexive
I myself
we ourselves
you yourself / yourselves
he himself
she herself
it itself
they themselves

Examples

  • I hurt myself.
  • He introduced himself.
  • She blamed herself.
  • They enjoyed themselves.
  • We prepared ourselves for the exam.
⚠ Important Myself का प्रयोग केवल I के साथ reflexive sense में होता है।

❌ Myself Arpit.
✅ I am Arpit.

6. Demonstrative Pronouns

Demonstrative pronouns किसी व्यक्ति या वस्तु की ओर संकेत करते हैं।

Pronoun Number Distance
this Singular Near
these Plural Near
that Singular Far
those Plural Far
Examples:
This is my book.
These are my books.
That is your house.
Those are your houses.
❌ SSC Error Those is my books.
❌ Wrong

Those are my books.
✅ Correct

7. Relative Pronouns

Relative pronouns दो clauses को जोड़ते हैं और पहले आए noun से relation बताते हैं।

Pronoun Use
who Persons as subject
whom Persons as object
whose Possession
which Things / Animals
that Persons / Things in many restrictive clauses

WHO

The boy who won the race is my brother.

यहाँ who का संबंध "boy" से है।

WHOM

The man whom I met was a doctor.

यहाँ "whom" object के रूप में है।

WHOSE

The girl whose father is a doctor is my friend.

WHICH

This is the book which I bought yesterday.

THAT

This is the house that Jack built.

8. Interrogative Pronouns

Questions पूछने के लिए interrogative pronouns का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Pronoun Use Example
Who Person as subject Who called you?
Whom Person as object Whom did you meet?
Whose Possession Whose book is this?
Which Limited choice Which pen do you want?
What General information What do you want?

9. Indefinite Pronouns

जब किसी व्यक्ति या वस्तु की exact identity ज्ञात न हो, indefinite pronouns का प्रयोग होता है।

Pronoun Meaning
someone कोई व्यक्ति
somebody कोई व्यक्ति
anyone कोई भी व्यक्ति
everyone हर व्यक्ति
everybody हर व्यक्ति
nobody कोई नहीं
none एक भी नहीं
either दो में से कोई एक
neither दोनों में से कोई नहीं
⭐ Important SSC Rule Everyone, everybody, someone, somebody, nobody, anyone आदि grammatically singular माने जाते हैं।

Examples

  • Everyone is ready.
  • Somebody has called you.
  • Nobody knows the answer.
  • Everyone was present.
❌ Common Error Everyone are ready.
❌ Wrong

Everyone is ready.
✅ Correct

10. Distributive Pronouns

जब किसी group के members को individually refer किया जाता है, distributive pronouns का प्रयोग होता है।

Each

Each दो या दो से अधिक व्यक्तियों/वस्तुओं में से हर एक के लिए प्रयोग होता है।

Each of the boys is intelligent.

यहाँ each singular है, इसलिए verb भी singular है।

Either

Either = दो में से कोई एक।

Either of the two boys is capable.

Neither

Neither = दो में से कोई भी नहीं।

Neither of the two answers is correct.

11. Reciprocal Pronouns

जब दो या अधिक लोग एक-दूसरे के प्रति समान action करते हैं, reciprocal pronouns का प्रयोग होता है।

Two important forms:
each other
one another

Examples

  • Ram and Shyam helped each other.
  • The two sisters love each other.
  • The students helped one another.

12. Important SSC Rules

Rule 1 — Pronoun must agree with antecedent

Pronoun का number और person उसके antecedent के according होना चाहिए।

Every student must bring his or her identity card.

Modern English में context के अनुसार singular they/their भी कई contexts में मिलता है, लेकिन traditional SSC grammar questions में sentence structure और options के अनुसार answer चुनना चाहिए।

Rule 2 — Each / Every के बाद singular noun

Each student was given a book.
Every student was present.

Rule 3 — One के साथ one's

One should do one's duty.
❌ One should do his duty.
SSC traditional grammar में preferred:
✅ One should do one's duty.

Rule 4 — Between के बाद objective pronoun

This is a secret between you and me.

❌ between you and I
✅ between you and me

Rule 5 — Preposition के बाद objective pronoun

  • for me
  • to him
  • with her
  • between us

Rule 6 — Than / As के बाद context देखें

He is taller than I.

यहाँ पूरा implied clause है:
He is taller than I am.

13. Common Pronoun Errors

❌ Error 1 Myself will attend the meeting.

✅ Correct: I will attend the meeting.
❌ Error 2 Between you and I.

✅ Correct: Between you and me.
❌ Error 3 Everyone have done their work.

Traditional SSC pattern:
Everyone has done his/her work.
❌ Error 4 Each boys was given a prize.

✅ Correct: Each boy was given a prize.
❌ Error 5 He himself did it himself.

Unnecessary repetition of reflexive pronoun should be avoided according to sentence structure.
❌ Error 6 This books are expensive.

✅ Correct: These books are expensive.

14. SSC PYQs & Exam-Level Questions

PYQ 01 SSC Pattern

Between you and ______, this matter should remain secret.

A. I
B. me
C. myself
D. mine
Answer: B. me
Explanation: "Between" is a preposition, so objective pronoun me is used.
PYQ 02 SSC Pattern

Everyone should do ______ duty honestly.

A. his
B. their
C. our
D. your
Answer: A. his
Explanation: Traditional SSC grammar pattern में singular "Everyone" के साथ singular possessive form expected होती है।
PYQ 03 SSC Pattern

Each of the students ______ given a certificate.

A. were
B. have
C. was
D. are
Answer: C. was
Explanation: Each singular माना जाता है, इसलिए singular verb was आएगा।
PYQ 04 SSC Pattern

This is the boy ______ won the competition.

A. which
B. who
C. whom
D. whose
Answer: B. who
Explanation: "Boy" person है और relative pronoun subject के रूप में काम कर रहा है, इसलिए who।
PYQ 05 SSC Pattern

The man ______ I met yesterday was a doctor.

A. who
B. whom
C. whose
D. which
Answer: B. whom
Explanation: "I met whom" — यहाँ pronoun object है, इसलिए traditional grammar में whom।
PYQ 06 SSC Pattern

Neither of the two boys has completed ______ work.

A. their
B. his
C. our
D. your
Answer: B. his
Explanation: Neither singular sense देता है; traditional SSC pattern में singular possessive pronoun/adjective अपेक्षित है।
PYQ 07 SSC Pattern

The two brothers helped ______.

A. themselves
B. each other
C. himself
D. ourselves
Answer: B. each other
Explanation: दो persons एक-दूसरे पर action कर रहे हैं, इसलिए reciprocal pronoun "each other"।
PYQ 08 SSC Pattern

One should respect ______ parents.

A. his
B. one's
C. their
D. your
Answer: B. one's
Explanation: "One" के साथ possessive form "one's" प्रयोग की जाती है।
PYQ 09 SSC Pattern

______ of the two answers is correct.

A. Neither
B. None
C. Nobody
D. No one
Answer: A. Neither
Explanation: दो persons/things में से कोई भी नहीं = neither।
PYQ 10 SSC Pattern

The girl ______ father is a doctor is my friend.

A. who
B. whom
C. whose
D. which
Answer: C. whose
Explanation: Father का relation girl से possession दिखाता है, इसलिए whose।
