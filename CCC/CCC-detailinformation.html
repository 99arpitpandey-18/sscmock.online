CCC Kya Hai? / What is CCC?

Basic introduction

🇮🇳 HINDI

CCC क्या है?

CCC ka full form Course on Computer Concepts hai. Iska main purpose learners ko basic computer concepts, office productivity, internet, email, digital services aur cyber safety ke baare mein practical awareness dena hai.

🇬🇧 ENGLISH

What is CCC?

CCC stands for Course on Computer Concepts. It is designed to develop basic computer literacy and awareness of digital tools, office applications, internet services, digital financial tools and cyber safety.

Passing Marks / पासिंग मार्क्स

CCC qualify karne ke liye kitna score?

🇮🇳 HINDI

Minimum Qualifying Score

CCC mein traditionally 50% marks ko minimum qualifying threshold maana gaya hai. Example: Agar paper 100 marks ka maana jaye, to 50% = 50 marks. Lekin current examination instructions ko application/exam se pehle verify karein.

🇬🇧 ENGLISH

Minimum Qualifying Score

The traditional minimum qualifying threshold for CCC is 50%. For example, if the assessment is considered out of 100 marks, 50% would correspond to 50 marks. Check the latest NIELIT examination instructions for the current rules.
