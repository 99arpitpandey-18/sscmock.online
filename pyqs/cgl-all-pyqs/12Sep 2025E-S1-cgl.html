Q1. A man said, “My father’s wife’s only son is the brother of your mother.” How is the man related to you?
(a) Uncle   (b) Father   (c) Grandfather   (d) Brother
✅ Answer: (a) Uncle
📖 Father’s wife = mother. Mother’s only son = man himself (or his brother). But here it says "brother of your mother" – that is the man’s mother’s son? Actually: man’s father’s wife = his mother. Her only son = the man (if he is the only son) – but then he is the brother of your mother, meaning the man is your mother’s brother → he is your maternal uncle. So answer (a).
Q2. COLD : FRPE :: WARM : ?
(a) ZDUN   (b) ZDVP   (c) ZDVN   (d) YDUP
✅ Answer: (c) ZDVN
📖 COLD → FRPE: C→F (+3), O→R (+3), L→P (+4), D→E (+1). Pattern not consistent. Actually COLD to FRPE: each letter shifted by +3? C+3=F, O+3=R, L+4=P, D+1=E. Try WARM: W+3=Z, A+3=D, R+4=V, M+1=N → ZDVN. So answer (c).
Q3. Select the letter-cluster that replaces ? in: XZYX, XYXX, XXWX, XWVX, ?
(a) XVUX   (b) XTVX   (c) XUVX   (d) XUTX
✅ Answer: (a) XVUX
📖 Positions: 1st letter always X. 2nd: Z→Y→X→W→V (decrease by 1). 3rd: Y→X→W→V→U (decrease by 1). 4th: X fixed. So next is X V U X → XVUX.
