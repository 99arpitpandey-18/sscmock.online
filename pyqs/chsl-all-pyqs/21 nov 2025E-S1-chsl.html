Q1 Choose the correct indirect narration of the following statement from the given alternatives:

Mohan said, “I will come to the library this evening if my friend does not take me to the market.”
(a) Mohan said that he will come to the library that evening if his friend did not take him to the market.
(b) Mohan said that he would come to the library that evening if his friend did not take him to the market.
(c) Mohan said that he would go to the library this evening if his friend does not take him to the market.
(d) Mohan said that he came to the library that evening if his friend had not taken him to the market.
Correct Answer: (b)
Explanation: In indirect speech, “will” changes to “would” after a past reporting verb. “This evening” changes to “that evening”. Therefore, option (b) is correct.
Q2 Select the option that expresses the given sentence in direct voice.

The teacher politely asked the students if they were ready for the test that day.

(b) The teacher said to the students, “Were you ready for the test that day?”
(c) The teacher asked the students, “Were you ready for the test today?”
(d) The teacher asked, “Are you ready for the test that day?”
Correct Answer: (a)
Explanation: In direct speech, “they were” changes back to “you are” and “that day” becomes “today”. Hence option (a).

Q20 Which of the following sentences uses a modal verb to show possibility?

The National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 is among the most important education reforms introduced in India in recent years.
