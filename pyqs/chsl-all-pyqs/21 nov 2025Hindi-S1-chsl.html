Q1. Choose the correct indirect narration of the following statement from the given alternatives:
Mohan said, “I will come to the library this evening if my friend does not take me to the market.”
(a) Mohan said that he will come to the library that evening if his friend did not take him to the market. (b) Mohan said that he would come to the library that evening if his friend did not take him to the market. (c) Mohan said that he would go to the library this evening if his friend does not take him to the market. (d) Mohan said that he came to the library that evening if his friend had not taken him to the market.
Answer: (b)
In indirect speech, “will” changes to “would” and “this evening” changes to “that evening”. Therefore, option (b) is correct.

Q27. A बाईं ओर से 10वें स्थान पर है, B दाईं ओर से 18वें स्थान पर है और उनके बीच 9 छात्र हैं। छात्रों की अधिकतम संख्या ज्ञात कीजिए।
(a) 35 (b) 36 (c) 37 (d) 38
Answer: (c) 37
A की left rank = 10. उनके बीच 9 छात्र हैं, इसलिए B की left rank 20 होगी। B की right rank 18 है। अतः कुल = 20 + 18 − 1 = 37.
