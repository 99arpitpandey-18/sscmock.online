1. Introduction to Digital Financial Tools

Basic concept of digital financial services

What are Digital Financial Tools?

🇬🇧 ENGLISH

Digital financial tools are electronic systems and services used to perform financial activities such as payments, money transfers, banking and transactions using digital devices and networks.

🇮🇳 हिंदी

Digital Financial Tools ऐसे electronic systems और services हैं जिनका उपयोग digital devices और networks की सहायता से payment, money transfer, banking और financial transactions करने के लिए किया जाता है।

📌 Exam Definition

Digital financial tools enable users to perform financial transactions electronically without depending entirely on physical cash.

💳

3. UPI — Unified Payments Interface

Important digital payment concept

What is UPI?

🇬🇧 ENGLISH

UPI stands for Unified Payments Interface. It is a digital payment system that allows users to make bank-account-based payments through supported applications.

🇮🇳 हिंदी

UPI का पूरा नाम Unified Payments Interface है। यह एक digital payment system है जिसके माध्यम से supported applications द्वारा bank account आधारित payments किए जा सकते हैं।

Important UPI Features

  • Fast digital payments
  • Bank account based transactions
  • UPI ID के माध्यम से payment
  • QR code based payment
  • Supported mobile applications द्वारा उपयोग
  • PIN based transaction authentication

⭐ IMPORTANT FOR EXAM

Remember: UPI stands for Unified Payments Interface. It is an important digital payment mechanism used for bank-account-based electronic transactions.

🏦

4. Digital Banking

Internet Banking and Mobile Banking

Internet Banking

🇬🇧 ENGLISH

Internet Banking allows customers to access various banking services through the internet using a bank's online platform.

🇮🇳 हिंदी

Internet Banking के द्वारा customers internet और bank के online platform का उपयोग करके विभिन्न banking services access कर सकते हैं।

  • Transaction history
  • Fund transfer
  • Bill payment
  • Account statement

Mobile Banking

🇬🇧 ENGLISH

Mobile Banking provides banking services through a mobile device and a bank's supported mobile application.

🇮🇳 हिंदी

Mobile Banking mobile device और bank की supported mobile application के माध्यम से banking services प्रदान करता है।

6. Debit Card & Credit Card

Important card-based payment tools

Feature Debit Card Credit Card
Source of payment Generally linked to available bank balance Uses a credit facility provided by issuer
Payment Amount is generally debited from linked account Amount is charged against available credit limit
ATM use Commonly used for cash withdrawal May also support cash-related services subject to issuer terms
🇬🇧 DEBIT CARD

A debit card generally allows the customer to make payments using funds available in the linked bank account.

🇮🇳 डेबिट कार्ड

Debit Card से सामान्यतः linked bank account में उपलब्ध funds का उपयोग करके payment किया जाता है।

🇬🇧 CREDIT CARD

A credit card provides access to a credit facility up to an approved limit, subject to the issuer's terms.

🇮🇳 क्रेडिट कार्ड

Credit Card approved credit limit तक credit facility का उपयोग करके payment करने की सुविधा देता है।

8. QR Code & PoS

Digital payment acceptance tools

🔳

QR Code

QR stands for Quick Response. A QR code can encode information that can be scanned by compatible devices or applications.

🏪

PoS

PoS stands for Point of Sale. It refers to systems or terminals used to accept payments at a point of sale.

📲

Scan & Pay

Customer scans a supported QR code using a payment application and completes the payment.

🔐

9. Digital Financial Security

Safe digital banking and payment practices

Important Security Practices

🇬🇧 ENGLISH
  • Never share OTP with anyone.
  • Never share UPI PIN.
  • Use strong passwords.
  • Avoid suspicious links.
  • Use official banking applications.
  • Check transaction details before payment.
  • Report suspicious transactions promptly.
🇮🇳 हिंदी
  • OTP किसी के साथ share न करें।
  • UPI PIN किसी को न बताएं।
  • Strong password का उपयोग करें।
  • Suspicious links से बचें।
  • Official banking applications का उपयोग करें।
  • Payment से पहले transaction details check करें।
  • Suspicious transaction को तुरंत report करें।

🚨 GOLDEN RULE

Bank, payment service या कोई genuine support representative आपसे आपका OTP, PIN या password बताने के लिए नहीं कहना चाहिए। ऐसे confidential credentials किसी के साथ share न करें।
