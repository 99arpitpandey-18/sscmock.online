1. Introduction

E-Mail, Social Networking and e-Governance

Digital Communication & Services

🇬🇧 ENGLISH

Modern computers and the Internet provide many facilities for communication, social interaction and access to government services. E-Mail, social networking and e-Governance are important examples of such digital services.

🇮🇳 हिंदी

आधुनिक computer और Internet communication, social interaction तथा government services तक पहुँचने की अनेक सुविधाएँ प्रदान करते हैं। E-Mail, social networking और e-Governance इसके महत्वपूर्ण उदाहरण हैं।

📌 Exam Definition

E-Mail is an electronic method of sending and receiving messages through a computer network or the Internet.

📧

2. E-Mail

Electronic Mail and its basic concepts

What is E-Mail?

🇬🇧 ENGLISH

E-Mail stands for Electronic Mail. It is a service used to send and receive digital messages over the Internet. Messages can contain text, images, documents and other attachments.

🇮🇳 हिंदी

E-Mail का पूरा नाम Electronic Mail है। यह Internet के माध्यम से digital messages भेजने और प्राप्त करने की service है। इसमें text, images, documents और अन्य attachments भेजे जा सकते हैं।

⭐ IMPORTANT FOR EXAM

E-Mail is one of the most commonly used Internet-based communication services. It is faster and more convenient than traditional postal communication.

📨

Structure of an E-Mail Address

Field English Meaning हिंदी अर्थ

4. E-Mail Services & Operations

Common email operations

✉️

Compose

Used to create a new email message.

📤

Send

Sends the composed message to the recipient.

📥

Inbox

Stores received email messages.

📤

Sent Mail

Contains messages that have already been sent.

📝

Drafts

Stores messages that are not yet sent.

🗑️

Trash

Contains deleted email messages.

Common E-Mail Protocols

Protocol Full Form Main Use
SMTP Simple Mail Transfer Protocol Sending e-mail
POP3 Post Office Protocol Version 3 Receiving / downloading e-mail
IMAP Internet Message Access Protocol Accessing and synchronizing mail on server

5. Social Networking

Social networking and online communication

What is Social Networking?

🇬🇧 ENGLISH

Social networking refers to using online platforms and services to communicate, interact, share information and build connections with other people.

🇮🇳 हिंदी

Social networking का अर्थ online platforms और services के माध्यम से लोगों से communication करना, information share करना और connections बनाना है।

👤

User Profile

Represents a user's identity and information on a social platform.

👥

Connections

Users can connect with friends, classmates and communities.

💬

Messaging

Allows users to exchange digital messages.

📷

Media Sharing

Users can share images, videos and other media.

🔔

Notifications

Provides alerts about activities and updates.

#️⃣

Hashtags

Help organize and discover content around a particular topic.

Advantages of Social Networking

🇬🇧 ENGLISH
  • Fast communication
  • Information sharing
  • Educational interaction
  • Community building
  • Professional networking
  • Public awareness
🇮🇳 हिंदी
  • तेज communication
  • Information sharing — जानकारी साझा करना
  • Educational interaction — शैक्षणिक संवाद
  • Community building — समुदाय बनाना
  • Professional networking — व्यावसायिक networking
  • Public awareness — जन-जागरूकता

6. Social Networking & Cyber Safety

Safe and responsible use of online platforms

Important Safety Practices

🇬🇧 ENGLISH
  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication where available.
  • Do not share passwords or OTPs.
  • Check privacy settings.
  • Avoid suspicious links.
  • Verify information before sharing it.
  • Report suspicious or abusive activity.
🇮🇳 हिंदी
  • Strong और unique passwords का उपयोग करें।
  • उपलब्ध होने पर two-factor authentication enable करें।
  • Password या OTP किसी से share न करें।
  • Privacy settings को check करें।
  • Suspicious links से बचें।
  • Information share करने से पहले verify करें।
  • Suspicious activity को report करें।

⚠️ EXAM POINT

Never share confidential information such as passwords, PINs or OTPs with unknown persons.

7. e-Governance

Electronic delivery of government services

What is e-Governance?

🇬🇧 ENGLISH

e-Governance refers to the use of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) by government organizations to provide services, information and communication to citizens, businesses and other organizations.

🇮🇳 हिंदी

e-Governance का अर्थ government organizations द्वारा Information and Communication Technology (ICT) का उपयोग करके citizens, businesses और अन्य organizations को services, information तथा communication उपलब्ध कराना है।

📌 Simple Definition

Government services delivered electronically using digital technology are commonly referred to as e-Governance services.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

G2C

Government to Citizen. Services provided by government to citizens.

🏢

G2B

Government to Business. Digital interaction between government and businesses.

🏛️

G2G

Government to Government. Digital interaction between government departments.

👨‍💼

G2E

Government to Employee. Digital services for government employees.

📲

10. Digital Signature

Important concept in digital transactions

Digital Signature

🇬🇧 ENGLISH

A digital signature is a cryptographic mechanism used to verify the authenticity and integrity of a digital message or document.

🇮🇳 हिंदी

Digital signature एक cryptographic mechanism है जिसका उपयोग digital message या document की authenticity और integrity verify करने के लिए किया जाता है।

📌 REMEMBER

Digital signature is different from simply typing or drawing a signature on a document. It uses cryptographic techniques.

🪪

11. Digital Identity

Identity in online environments

🇬🇧 ENGLISH

Digital identity refers to information associated with a person or entity that is used to identify them in a digital environment.

One Page Revision

Chapter 7 in quick format

E-Mail

Electronic Mail is an Internet-based service used to send and receive digital messages, documents and attachments.

E-Mail Fields

To, Cc, Bcc, Subject, Body and Attachment are important parts of an email message.

E-Mail Protocols

SMTP is associated with sending mail. POP3 is associated with downloading mail. IMAP provides server-based access and synchronization.

Social Networking

Online platforms allow users to communicate, share content, build connections and interact with communities.

Cyber Safety

Use strong passwords, protect OTPs, review privacy settings, avoid suspicious links and verify information before sharing.

e-Governance

ICT-enabled delivery of government services and information to citizens, businesses, employees and government departments.

G2C / G2B

G2C means Government to Citizen, while G2B means Government to Business.

G2G / G2E

G2G means Government to Government, while G2E means Government to Employee.
