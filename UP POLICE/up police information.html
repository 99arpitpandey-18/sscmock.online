🏃 Physical Standards — Computer Operator

Computer Operator के लिए सामान्यतः उतनी कठोर physical standards नहीं होती जितनी Constable/SI के लिए। फिर भी, medical fitness और बुनियादी शारीरिक मानक (जैसे न्यूनतम ऊँचाई/वज़न) कुछ notifications में उल्लिखित हो सकते हैं। हमेशा latest notice verify करें।

🖥️ Computer Syllabus

  • Computer fundamentals & generations
  • Hardware, CPU, memory, input/output devices
  • Operating System & file management
  • MS Word, Excel, PowerPoint & office automation
  • Internet, Email, WWW, networking basics
  • LAN/WAN/MAN, data communication
  • DBMS, tables, records, keys & SQL basics
  • Cyber security & digital awareness

🚀 Computer Operator Student Roadmap

  • Step 1: Physics + Maths वाले Intermediate eligibility को check करें
  • Step 2: O Level / accepted equivalent qualification complete करें
  • Step 3: Computer Science topic-wise MCQs करें
  • Step 4: Hindi 25 + English 30 WPM से ऊपर regular typing practice करें
  • Step 5: Written + typing को parallel prepare करें
Official rule reference: UP Police Computer Staff Service Rules. The rule lists Intermediate with Physics & Mathematics plus DOEACC 'O' Level, or the specified diploma route. Current recruitment notice/test instructions are final.
