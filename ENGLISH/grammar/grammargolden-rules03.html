Noun (संज्ञा) – Types & Rules

Noun

संज्ञा (Noun) – किसी व्यक्ति, वस्तु, स्थान, भाव या विचार के नाम को Noun कहते हैं।

5 Types of Nouns:

  • Common Noun: जातिवाचक – boy, city, book
  • Proper Noun: व्यक्तिवाचक – Ram, Delhi, Gita
  • Collective Noun: समूहवाचक – team, flock, jury
  • Abstract Noun: भाववाचक – honesty, childhood, sorrow
  • Material Noun: द्रव्यवाचक – gold, water, wood
Example: “Honesty is the best policy.” → Honesty (Abstract Noun)

Important Rules:

  • Rule 1: कुछ Nouns हमेशा Singular होते हैं – scenery, information, furniture, advice, luggage
  • Rule 2: कुछ Nouns हमेशा Plural होते हैं – cattle, police, people, premises
  • Rule 3: Collective Noun के साथ Verb Singular/Plural दोनों हो सकती है – यदि Group पर जोर हो तो Singular, सदस्यों पर जोर हो तो Plural

Pronoun (सर्वनाम) – Types & Rules

Pronoun

Pronoun – संज्ञा के स्थान पर प्रयुक्त होने वाला शब्द।

Types of Pronouns:

  • Personal: I, we, you, he, she, it, they
  • Possessive: my, our, your, his, her, its, their
  • Reflexive: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, themselves
  • Demonstrative: this, that, these, those
  • Indefinite: some, any, none, all, few, many, everyone
  • Distributive: each, either, neither, every
  • Relative: who, whom, whose, which, that
  • Interrogative: who, whom, whose, which, what
  • Reciprocal: each other, one another
Important Rule:
✔️ “Each of the boys is honest.” (Singular Verb)
✔️ “Either of the two is correct.”
✖️ “Each of the boys are honest.” (❌ Wrong)

Pronoun – Common Errors:

  • Who vs Whom: Who → Subject; Whom → Object
  • Its vs It's: Its → Possessive; It's → It is
  • Their vs There: Their → Possessive; There → Place

Verb (क्रिया) – Types & Rules

Verb

Verb – क्रिया (Action) या अवस्था (State) को व्यक्त करता है।

Main Types:

  • Transitive Verb: जिसका प्रभाव Object पर पड़े – She wrote a letter.
  • Intransitive Verb: जिसका प्रभाव Object पर न पड़े – He slept.
  • Auxiliary / Helping Verbs: is, am, are, was, were, have, has, had, do, does, did, will, shall, can, may, must, etc.
  • Finite Verb: Tense के अनुसार बदलता है – He goes to school.
  • Non-Finite Verb: Infinitive, Gerund, Participle – He likes to read.
Important Rule:
✔️ “The committee has submitted its report.” (Singular)
✔️ “The team are wearing new jerseys.” (Plural – members पर जोर)

Tenses (काल) – Complete Rules

Tense

Tense – समय (Time) को दर्शाता है – Present, Past, Future (तीनों के 4-4 रूप)।

TenseFormulaExample
Present IndefiniteV1 + s/esHe goes to school.
Present Continuousis/am/are + V1+ingHe is going to school.
Present Perfecthas/have + V3He has gone to school.
Present Perfect Continuoushas/have + been + V1+ingHe has been going since morning.
Past IndefiniteV2He went to school.
Past Continuouswas/were + V1+ingHe was going to school.
Past Perfecthad + V3He had gone before I came.
Future Indefinitewill/shall + V1He will go to school.

Important Rules:

  • Rule 1: यदि समय (Time) दिया हो तो Simple Past का प्रयोग करें – I went to Delhi yesterday.
  • Rule 2: यदि दो कार्य Past में हुए, तो पहले वाला Past Perfect, बाद वाला Simple Past – He had left before I arrived.
  • Rule 3: ‘since’ और ‘for’ के साथ Perfect Continuous का प्रयोग – I have been studying since 8 AM.

Preposition (पूर्वसर्ग) – Complete Guide

Preposition

Preposition – वह शब्द जो Noun/Pronoun का संबंध अन्य शब्दों से दिखाए।

Important Prepositions & Rules:

  • In / At / On (Time):
    - In → Months, Years, Seasons – in January, in 2025
    - On → Days, Dates – on Monday, on 5th May
    - At → Exact Time – at 5 PM, at night
  • In / At / On (Place):
    - In → enclosed space – in the room
    - On → surface – on the table
    - At → point – at the door
  • Since / For:
    - Since → point of time – since 8 AM, since Monday
    - For → duration – for 2 hours, for 10 years
  • Between / Among:
    - Between → two persons/things – between Ram and Shyam
    - Among → more than two – among friends
  • Beside / Besides:
    - Beside → next to – beside the river
    - Besides → in addition to – besides English, he knows Hindi
Common Errors:
✖️ “We have been waiting since 2 hours.” (❌ Wrong)
✔️ “We have been waiting for 2 hours.” (✅ Correct)
✖️ “He is in the bus.” (❌ for a person)
✔️ “He is on the bus.” (✅ Correct)

Article (आर्टिकल) – A, An, The Rules

Article

Article – A, An, The (Determiners) – Noun के पहले आते हैं।

Definite Article: THE

  • जब Noun पहले ही ज्ञात हो: – Give me the book.
  • जब Adjective की Superlative Degree हो: – He is the best student.
  • दिशाएँ (Directions): – to the east
  • नदियाँ, सागर, पर्वत श्रृंखलाएँ: – the Ganga, the Himalayas
  • समाचार पत्र, संगठन: – the Times of India, the UN

Indefinite Articles: A / An

  • A → Consonant sound (व्यंजन ध्वनि) – a boy, a university, a European
  • An → Vowel sound (स्वर ध्वनि) – an apple, an honest man, an hour
Important Exceptions:
✔️ “He is an honest man.” (Honest → 'O' sound)
✔️ “She is an MLA.” (M → 'em' sound)
✔️ “He is a university student.” (University → 'you' sound)

Active & Passive Voice – Rules

Voice

Active Voice: Subject करता है कार्य।
Passive Voice: Object पर कार्य होता है।

Formula:
Active: Subject + Verb + Object
Passive: Object + be (according to tense) + V3 + by + Subject

TenseActivePassive
Present IndefiniteHe writes a letter.A letter is written by him.
Present ContinuousHe is writing a letter.A letter is being written by him.
Present PerfectHe has written a letter.A letter has been written by him.
Past IndefiniteHe wrote a letter.A letter was written by him.
Future IndefiniteHe will write a letter.A letter will be written by him.

Important Rules:

  • Rule 1: Intransitive Verbs का Passive नहीं बनता – He sleeps. (✔️ No Passive)
  • Rule 2: जब Active में ‘let’ आए तो Passive में ‘let’ + Object + be + V3 – Let the work be done.
  • Rule 3: ‘by’ की जगह ‘to/at/with’ भी आ सकता है – He is known to me.

Direct & Indirect Speech (Narration)

Narration

Direct Speech: – किसी के कहे गए शब्दों को ज्यों-का-त्यों दिखाना।
Indirect Speech: – उसे अपने शब्दों में बताना।

Rules for changing Tense:

  • Reporting Verb Past में हो → Reported Speech का Tense Past हो जाता है।
  • Present Indefinite → Past Indefinite: “I write” → He said that he wrote.
  • Present Continuous → Past Continuous: “I am writing” → He said that he was writing.
  • Present Perfect → Past Perfect: “I have written” → He said that he had written.

Rules for changing Modals:

  • Can → Could ; May → Might ; Will → Would ; Shall → Should
  • Must → Had to / Must (if universal truth)
Example:
Direct: He said, “I am going to market.”
Indirect: He said that he was going to market.

Direct: She said, “I will help you.”
Indirect: She said that she would help me.

Conjunction (संयोजक) – Complete Rules

Conjunction

Conjunction – दो शब्दों, वाक्यांशों या वाक्यों को जोड़ने वाला शब्द।

Types of Conjunctions:

  • Coordinating Conjunctions: FANBOYS – For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So
  • Subordinating Conjunctions: – Although, Because, Since, Unless, Until, While, Whereas, As soon as
  • Correlative Conjunctions: – Either…or, Neither…nor, Both…and, Not only…but also

Important Rules:

  • Rule 1: Neither…nor – Verb का Agreement निकटतम Subject के साथ – Neither Ram nor his friends are coming.
  • Rule 2: Either…or – Verb का Agreement निकटतम Subject के साथ – Either Sita or her sisters are going.
  • Rule 3: Not only…but also – दोनों तरफ समान संरचना – He is not only intelligent but also hardworking.
  • Rule 4: As soon as के साथ Past Perfect का प्रयोग – As soon as he had arrived, the meeting started.

Modals & Conditional Sentences

Modals

Modals (सहायक क्रियाएँ):

  • Can / Could: Ability, Permission, Possibility – I can swim.
  • May / Might: Possibility, Permission – It may rain today.
  • Must: Necessity, Strong obligation – You must obey the rules.
  • Should / Ought to: Advice, Moral duty – You should help the poor.
  • Will / Shall: Future, Promise, Offer – I will help you.
  • Used to: Past habit – He used to smoke.
  • Need / Dare: Semi-modals – You need not go.

Conditional Sentences (If Clauses):

  • Type 0 (Universal Truth): If + Present, Present – If you heat water, it boils.
  • Type 1 (Possible Condition): If + Present, Future – If it rains, I will stay home.
  • Type 2 (Unlikely Condition): If + Past, would + V1 – If I were a bird, I would fly.
  • Type 3 (Impossible Past): If + Past Perfect, would have + V3 – If he had studied, he would have passed.
Important: ‘If I were’ (not ‘was’) – हमेशा Subjunctive Mood में were का प्रयोग होता है।

Error Detection – 50+ Common Rules

Error Detection

Error Detection – SSC में सबसे महत्वपूर्ण Topic। नीचे कुछ महत्वपूर्ण Rules:

Subject-Verb Agreement:

  • ✔️ “Each of the boys is playing.” (❌ not ‘are’)
  • ✔️ “Neither of them is coming.”
  • ✔️ “The United States is a big country.” (Singular – एक देश)
  • ✔️ “The police are on duty.” (Plural – हमेशा Plural)
  • ✔️ “A number of students are absent.” (Plural – ‘a number of’ → Plural)
  • ✔️ “The number of students is 500.” (Singular – ‘the number’ → Singular)

Tense Errors:

  • ✔️ “I have seen him yesterday.” (❌ ‘have’ के साथ ‘yesterday’ नहीं) → “I saw him yesterday.”
  • ✔️ “He is working here since 2010.” (❌) → “He has been working here since 2010.”
  • ✔️ “If she will come, I will go.” (❌) → “If she comes, I will go.”

Preposition Errors:

  • ✔️ “He is in the bed.” (❌) → “He is on the bed.”
  • ✔️ “She is angry on me.” (❌) → “She is angry with me.”
  • ✔️ “I am married with her.” (❌) → “I am married to her.”

Article Errors:

  • ✔️ “He is a honest man.” (❌) → “He is an honest man.”
  • ✔️ “We are going to the hospital.” (❌ – जब purpose हो) → “We are going to hospital.”
  • ✔️ “The Sun rises in the east.” (✔️ – क्योंकि अनोखा है)
Tip: SSC में Error Detection के 90% Questions इन्हीं Rules से आते हैं – इन्हें अच्छे से याद करें।

Subject-Verb Agreement – Detailed Rules

Agreement

Subject-Verb Agreement – SSC में बहुत महत्वपूर्ण।

Key Rules:

  • Rule 1: Each, Every, Either, Neither के साथ Singular Verb – Each student has a book.
  • Rule 2: Either…or / Neither…nor – Verb, निकटतम Subject से मेल खाती है – Neither Ram nor his friends are here.
  • Rule 3: With / together with / along with / as well as – इनके साथ मुख्य Subject से Verb मिलती है – He as well as his friends is going.
  • Rule 4: Scenery, information, furniture, advice, luggage – ये हमेशा Singular – The furniture is new.
  • Rule 5: People, cattle, police, clergy – ये हमेशा Plural – The police are on duty.
  • Rule 6: A lot of / A number of – Plural (अगर Noun Plural हो) – A number of students are present.
  • Rule 7: The number of – Singular – The number of students is 500.
  • Rule 8: More than one – Singular – More than one student has come.

Degree of Comparison – Rules

Comparison

Degree of Comparison – तुलना करने के 3 रूप:

  • Positive Degree: – She is as tall as her sister.
  • Comparative Degree: – She is taller than her sister.
  • Superlative Degree: – She is the tallest girl in the class.

Important Rules:

  • Rule 1: Senior, Junior, Superior, Inferior, Prior – इनके साथ ‘to’ लगता है, ‘than’ नहीं – He is senior to me. (❌ not ‘than’)
  • Rule 2: Less / Lesser – Less (Uncountable) – less water ; Fewer (Countable) – fewer students
  • Rule 3: Double Comparative – दो Comparative एक साथ नहीं – He is more taller than me. (❌) → He is taller than me.
  • Rule 4: Most / More – Most → Superlative ; More → Comparative
