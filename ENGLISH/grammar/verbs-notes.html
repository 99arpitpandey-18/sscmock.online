1. Verb क्या होता है?

Verb वह word है जो किसी action, state या condition को express करता है।

हिंदी में Verb को क्रिया कहते हैं।

📌 Definition A verb is a word that expresses an action, occurrence, or state of being.

यानी जो word बताए कि subject क्या करता है, क्या हो रहा है या किस अवस्था में है, वह verb हो सकता है।

Examples

  • Ram runs. → राम दौड़ता है।
  • She reads a book. → वह किताब पढ़ती है।
  • They play cricket. → वे क्रिकेट खेलते हैं।
  • He is happy. → वह खुश है।
  • I know him. → मैं उसे जानता हूँ।

2. Verb को कैसे पहचानें?

Sentence में subject के बारे में देखें कि वह क्या कर रहा है, क्या है, या उसके साथ क्या हो रहा है।

Ravi plays football.

Ravi क्या करता है?
→ plays

इसलिए plays = Verb।
She is beautiful.

यहाँ "is" subject की state बताता है।
इसलिए is = Verb।

4. Main Verb और Helping Verb

Main Verb

Main verb sentence का main action या state बताता है।

He plays cricket.

यहाँ "plays" main verb है।

Helping Verb

Helping verb main verb की tense, voice, question, negative आदि बनाने में सहायता करता है।

He is playing cricket.

is = Helping Verb
playing = Main Verb

Primary Helping Verbs

Verb Forms
Be am, is, are, was, were, be, being, been
Have has, have, had
Do do, does, did

6. Transitive Verb

जिस verb को अपना meaning complete करने के लिए object की आवश्यकता होती है, उसे Transitive Verb कहते हैं।

⭐ पहचानने की Trick Verb के बाद पूछें:

What? या Whom?

अगर answer मिलता है, तो verb transitive हो सकता है।
He bought a car.

He bought what?
→ a car

इसलिए "bought" = Transitive Verb।
She helped me.

She helped whom?
→ me

इसलिए "helped" transitive है।

7. Intransitive Verb

जिस verb को अपना meaning complete करने के लिए object की आवश्यकता नहीं होती, उसे Intransitive Verb कहते हैं।

The baby cried.

यहाँ object की आवश्यकता नहीं है।
इसलिए cried = Intransitive Verb।
  • He sleeps.
  • The sun rises.
  • Birds fly.
  • The baby cried.
  • He laughed.
⚠️ Important कुछ verbs context के अनुसार transitive और intransitive दोनों हो सकते हैं।
He runs every morning.
→ Intransitive

He runs a company.
→ Transitive

8. Finite Verb

वह verb जो subject और tense के अनुसार अपना form बदलता है, Finite Verb कहलाता है।

He plays cricket.

They play cricket.

"plays" और "play" subject के अनुसार बदल रहे हैं।

Another Example

  • I am happy.
  • He is happy.
  • They are happy.

9. Non-Finite Verb

वह verb form जो subject या tense के अनुसार अपना form नहीं बदलता, Non-Finite Verb कहलाता है।

इसके मुख्य प्रकार हैं:

  • Infinitive
  • Gerund
  • Participle
To read is a good habit.

यहाँ "to read" subject के अनुसार नहीं बदलता।

10. Infinitive

सामान्यतः to + V1 को infinitive कहा जाता है।

to + V1

to go
to read
to write
to play
to learn

Examples

  • I want to learn English.
  • He likes to play cricket.
  • She decided to study hard.
⚠️ Modal के बाद "to" नहीं

can + V1
may + V1
must + V1

❌ He can to swim.
✅ He can swim.

11. Gerund

जब V-ing form noun की तरह काम करे, तो उसे Gerund कहते हैं।

Swimming is good exercise.

यहाँ Swimming sentence का subject है।
इसलिए यह Gerund है।

More Examples

  • Reading is my hobby.
  • I enjoy reading.
  • Walking is good for health.
  • He is fond of playing cricket.
⭐ Preposition के बाद सामान्यतः Gerund

interested in learning
good at speaking
fond of reading

12. Participles

Participle verb की ऐसी form है जो adjective की तरह काम कर सकती है या verb phrase का हिस्सा बन सकती है।

Present Participle

सामान्यतः V-ing form।

The crying baby is hungry.

यहाँ crying baby को describe कर रहा है, इसलिए यह participle है।

Past Participle

सामान्यतः V3 form।

The broken chair is useless.

यहाँ broken chair को describe कर रहा है।

13. Auxiliary Verbs

Auxiliary verbs मुख्य verb की सहायता करते हैं।

Primary Auxiliaries

Auxiliary Forms Common Use
Be am, is, are, was, were Continuous / Passive
Have has, have, had Perfect Tense
Do do, does, did Questions / Negatives / Emphasis

Examples

  • He is reading.
  • She has finished her work.
  • Do you know him?
  • He does not play cricket.
  • I did not go there.

14. Modal Verbs

Modals ऐसे auxiliary verbs हैं जो ability, permission, possibility, necessity, advice, obligation आदि express करते हैं।

Modal Common Meaning Example
can सकना / ability I can swim.
could सकता था / polite request Could you help me?
may permission / possibility May I come in?
might कम possibility It might rain.
must necessity You must obey the rules.
should advice You should study.
would polite request / conditional Would you help me?
shall future / suggestion Shall we go?
will future I will help you.
⭐ Golden Rule Modal + V1

can go
should study
must obey
may come

15. Important Verb Rules

Rule 1 — Modal + V1

❌ He can goes there.
✅ He can go there.

Rule 2 — Did + V1

❌ He did not went there.
✅ He did not go there.

Rule 3 — Does + V1

❌ He does not goes there.
✅ He does not go there.

Rule 4 — Has/Have/Had + V3

He has gone home.

She has finished her work.

They had left before I arrived.

Rule 5 — Be + V-ing

He is reading.

They are playing.

She was singing.

Rule 6 — Passive Voice में V3

The letter was written by him.

The work has been completed.

Rule 7 — Let + Object + V1

Let me go.

Let him play.

Rule 8 — Make + Object + V1

The teacher made him work.

Rule 9 — Used to + V1

He used to play cricket.

❌ He used to played cricket.

Rule 10 — Had better + V1

You had better study hard.

❌ You had better studied hard.

Rule 11 — Would rather + V1

I would rather stay here.

Rule 12 — After preposition → V-ing

He is interested in learning English.

She is good at speaking English.

16. Common Errors

❌ Error 1 He did not went to school.

✅ Correct: He did not go to school.
❌ Error 2 She can sings well.

✅ Correct: She can sing well.
❌ Error 3 He has went home.

✅ Correct: He has gone home.
❌ Error 4 They are play cricket.

✅ Correct: They are playing cricket.
❌ Error 5 He is good in mathematics.

Exam-oriented correction: He is good at mathematics.
❌ Error 6 I am knowing him.

✅ Correct: I know him.

Know सामान्यतः stative verb है और continuous form सामान्य contexts में नहीं लिया जाता।

17. SSC PYQs & Exam-Level Questions

PYQ 01 SSC Pattern

He did not ______ to school yesterday.

A. went
B. go
C. gone
D. going
Answer: B. go
Explanation: Did के बाद हमेशा V1 आता है।
PYQ 02 SSC Pattern

She has ______ her homework.

A. do
B. did
C. done
D. doing
Answer: C. done
Explanation: Has + V3.
Do → did → done.
PYQ 03 SSC Pattern

He can ______ English fluently.

A. speaks
B. spoke
C. speak
D. speaking
Answer: C. speak
Explanation: Can + V1.
PYQ 04 SSC Pattern

They are ______ cricket now.

A. play
B. played
C. playing
D. plays
Answer: C. playing
Explanation: Present Continuous:
are + V-ing.
PYQ 05 SSC Pattern

The work has been ______.

A. complete
B. completed
C. completing
D. completes
Answer: B. completed
Explanation: Passive construction में V3 का प्रयोग होता है।
PYQ 06 SSC Pattern

He used to ______ cricket.

A. played
B. playing
C. play
D. plays
Answer: C. play
Explanation: Used to + V1.
PYQ 07 SSC Pattern

Let him ______ inside.

A. comes
B. came
C. come
D. coming
Answer: C. come
Explanation: Let + object + V1.
PYQ 08 SSC Pattern

She is interested in ______ English.

A. learn
B. learned
C. learning
D. learns
Answer: C. learning
Explanation: Preposition "in" के बाद V-ing form आएगा।
PYQ 09 SSC Pattern

You had better ______ now.

A. leave
B. left
C. leaving
D. leaves
Answer: A. leave
Explanation: Had better + V1.
PYQ 10 SSC Pattern

I would rather ______ at home.

A. stayed
B. stay
C. staying
D. stays
Answer: B. stay
Explanation: Would rather + V1.
PYQ 11 SSC Pattern

______ is good exercise.

A. Swim
B. Swam
C. Swimming
D. Swims
Answer: C. Swimming
Explanation: यहाँ Swimming noun की तरह subject का काम कर रहा है, इसलिए Gerund है।
PYQ 12 SSC Pattern

The teacher made the students ______.

A. worked
B. working
C. work
D. works
Answer: C. work
Explanation: Make + object + V1.
