1. Direct & Indirect Speech क्या है?

Direct Speech (प्रत्यक्ष कथन) – किसी व्यक्ति के exact words को quotation marks (" ") के अंदर दिखाना।

Indirect Speech (अप्रत्यक्ष कथन) – किसी व्यक्ति के words को अपने शब्दों में बिना quotation marks के कहना। इसे Reported Speech भी कहते हैं।

⭐ सबसे पहले यह याद रखें

Direct Speech में two parts होते हैं:
1. Reporting Verb (said, told, asked, etc.)
2. Reported Speech (quotation marks में)

Direct:
Ram said, "I am going to school."

Indirect:
Ram said that he was going to school.

यहाँ said = Reporting Verb
"I am going to school." = Reported Speech

2. Basic Rules of Narration

⭐ तीन मुख्य Changes
  1. Tense Change – Reported Speech का tense Reporting Verb के tense के अनुसार बदलता है।
  2. Pronoun Change – Reported Speech के pronouns (I, we, you, etc.) बदलते हैं।
  3. Time & Place Words – now, today, here, this आदि बदलते हैं।

3. Tense Changes (Reporting Verb → Past Tense में)

जब Reporting Verb Past Tense (said, told, asked) में हो, तो Reported Speech का tense one step back (past) हो जाता है।

Direct Speech Tense Indirect Speech Tense Example (Direct → Indirect)
Simple Present (V1/s) Simple Past (V2) "I play" → He said that he played.
Present Continuous (is/am/are + Ving) Past Continuous (was/were + Ving) "I am playing" → He said that he was playing.
Present Perfect (has/have + V3) Past Perfect (had + V3) "I have played" → He said that he had played.
Present Perfect Continuous Past Perfect Continuous "I have been playing" → He said that he had been playing.
Simple Past (V2) Past Perfect (had + V3) "I played" → He said that he had played.
Past Continuous (was/were + Ving) Past Perfect Continuous (had been + Ving) "I was playing" → He said that he had been playing.
Past Perfect (had + V3) Past Perfect (had + V3) – No Change "I had played" → He said that he had played.
Future (will/shall + V1) Would/Should + V1 "I will play" → He said that he would play.
Future Continuous Would be + Ving "I will be playing" → He said that he would be playing.

4. Pronoun Changes

⭐ SSC Favourite Rule

Pronouns को SON rule से बदलें:
S – Subject (I, we, you)
O – Object (me, us, you)
N – No change (he, she, it, they, name)

Direct Speech Pronoun Indirect Speech Pronoun Rule
I (First Person) Reporting Verb के Subject के अनुसार Subject → I → He/She
We (First Person) Reporting Verb के Subject के अनुसार Subject → We → They
You (Second Person) Reporting Verb के Object के अनुसार Object → You → Him/Her/Them
He/She/It/They (Third Person) No Change Third person → same remains

Example 1:
Direct: Ram said, "I am happy."
Indirect: Ram said that he was happy.
(I → He, because subject is Ram)

Example 2:
Direct: He said to me, "You are right."
Indirect: He told me that I was right.
(You → I, because object is me)

Example 3:
Direct: She said, "He is my brother."
Indirect: She said that he was her brother.
(He → He, third person no change)

5. Time & Place Words – Changes

Direct Speech Indirect Speech
now then
today that day
tonight that night
yesterday the previous day / the day before
tomorrow the next day / the following day
last week the previous week
next week the following week
here there
this that
these those
ago before
thus so

Direct: He said, "I will come tomorrow."
Indirect: He said that he would come the next day.

Direct: She said, "I am reading this book."
Indirect: She said that she was reading that book.

6. Assertive Sentences (Statements)

Assertive sentences में that का प्रयोग connector के रूप में किया जाता है।

Direct: He said, "I am tired."
Indirect: He said that he was tired.

Direct: She said to me, "You are honest."
Indirect: She told me that I was honest.

Direct: Ram said, "I will help you."
Indirect: Ram said that he would help me.

⭐ Said vs Said to

said to → told (object के साथ)
said → said (object न होने पर)

He said to me → He told me
He said → He said

7. Interrogative Sentences (Questions)

Questions को Indirect Speech में बदलते समय:
1. that नहीं आता, if/whether आता है (Yes/No questions में)।
2. Wh-word (what, who, where, etc.) वही रहता है।
3. Subject के बाद verb आती है (question order नहीं)।

Yes/No Questions (if/whether)

Direct: He said to me, "Are you coming?"
Indirect: He asked me if I was coming.

Direct: She said, "Is he your brother?"
Indirect: She asked whether he was my brother.

Wh-Questions

Direct: He said to me, "What are you doing?"
Indirect: He asked me what I was doing.

Direct: She said, "Where do you live?"
Indirect: She asked where I lived.

Direct: He said, "Who is that man?"
Indirect: He asked who that man was.

⭐ Important SSC Rule

Reporting Verb: said/said to → asked/inquired
(question sentences में)

8. Imperative Sentences (Commands / Requests / Advice)

Imperative sentences (order, request, advice, suggestion) में:
1. to का प्रयोग होता है (infinitive)।
2. Reporting Verb बदलता है:
- order/command → ordered/commanded
- request → requested
- advice → advised
- suggestion → suggested

Direct: He said to me, "Open the door."
Indirect: He ordered me to open the door.

Direct: She said to him, "Please help me."
Indirect: She requested him to help her.

Direct: The teacher said, "Work hard."
Indirect: The teacher advised me to work hard.

Negative Imperative (Don't)

Direct: He said to me, "Don't go there."
Indirect: He ordered me not to go there.

Direct: She said, "Never tell a lie."
Indirect: She advised me never to tell a lie.

9. Exclamatory Sentences

Exclamatory sentences (surprise, joy, sorrow, wonder) में:
1. that का प्रयोग होता है।
2. Reporting Verb बदलता है:
- joy → exclaimed with joy / exclaimed happily
- sorrow → exclaimed with sorrow / exclaimed sadly
- surprise → exclaimed with surprise
- wonder → exclaimed with wonder

Direct: He said, "What a beautiful place!"
Indirect: He exclaimed with wonder that it was a very beautiful place.

Direct: She said, "Alas! I have lost my bag."
Indirect: She exclaimed with sorrow that she had lost her bag.

Direct: He said, "Hurrah! We have won the match."
Indirect: He exclaimed with joy that they had won the match.

10. Optative Sentences (Wish / Prayer)

Optative sentences (wish, prayer, blessing) में:
1. that का प्रयोग होता है।
2. Reporting Verb → wished / prayed
3. may → might (often)

Direct: He said, "May you live long!"
Indirect: He wished that I might live long.

Direct: She said, "May God bless you!"
Indirect: She prayed that God might bless me.

11. Reporting Verbs – Summary

Type of Sentence Reporting Verb (Direct → Indirect)
Assertive (Statement) said → said / told (object के साथ)
Interrogative (Question) said → asked / inquired / demanded
Imperative (Order) said → ordered / commanded
Imperative (Request) said → requested / begged
Imperative (Advice) said → advised / suggested
Imperative (Suggestion) said → suggested / proposed
Exclamatory (Joy/Sorrow/Surprise) said → exclaimed (with joy/sorrow/surprise)
Optative (Wish/Prayer) said → wished / prayed

12. Exceptions – Universal Truth / Habits / Facts

⭐ SSC Favourite Rule

यदि Reported Speech Universal Truth, Habitual Fact, या Scientific Fact हो, तो tense नहीं बदलता – चाहे Reporting Verb Past Tense में ही क्यों न हो।

Direct: The teacher said, "The earth revolves around the sun."
Indirect: The teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun.
(Tense नहीं बदला – Universal Truth)

Direct: He said, "I get up at 6 am daily."
Indirect: He said that he gets up at 6 am daily.
(Tense नहीं बदला – Habitual Fact)

Direct: She said, "Water boils at 100°C."
Indirect: She said that water boils at 100°C.
(Tense नहीं बदला – Scientific Fact)

Other Exceptions – No Tense Change

1. Reporting Verb Present/Future में हो:
He says, "I am happy."
→ He says that he is happy. (No change)

2. Past Perfect already हो:
He said, "I had played."
→ He said that he had played. (No change)

13. Common Errors – SSC

Incorrect Correct Reason
He said that he is tired. He said that he was tired. Reporting Verb Past → tense change
He told that he was happy. He said that he was happy. 'told' के साथ object चाहिए
He asked me that where I was going. He asked me where I was going. Wh-word के साथ 'that' नहीं
He asked me if I am coming. He asked me if I was coming. Tense change (am → was)
He ordered me that I go. He ordered me to go. Imperative → to + V1
He said that he will come. He said that he would come. Future → would
She said that she can swim. She said that she could swim. Can → Could

14. SSC Narration Tricks

🔥 Trick 1 – SON Rule for Pronouns

Subject → I/We → according to subject
Object → You → according to object
No change → He/She/It/They

🔥 Trick 2 – Tense Change = One Step Back

Present → Past
Past → Past Perfect
Future → Would/Should

🔥 Trick 3 – Said to → Told

Said to (with object) → told
Said (no object) → said

🔥 Trick 4 – Question में if/whether या Wh-word

Yes/No question → if/whether
Wh-question → Wh-word + subject + verb

🔥 Trick 5 – Imperative = to + V1

Order/Request/Advice → to + V1
Negative → not to + V1

🔥 Trick 6 – Universal Truth = No Tense Change

Scientific facts, habits, universal truths → tense नहीं बदलता।

15. SSC PYQ-Based / Exam Pattern Practice

Question 1 SSC CGL 2022

Change to Indirect Speech:

He said, "I am going to the market."

A. He said that he was going to the market.
B. He said that I am going to the market.
C. He said that he is going to the market.
D. He said that he went to the market.
Answer: A. He said that he was going to the market.

I → He (subject), am going → was going (tense change)।

Question 2 SSC CHSL 2021

Change to Indirect Speech:

She said to me, "Are you coming with us?"

A. She asked me that I was coming with them.
B. She asked me if I was coming with them.
C. She asked me if I am coming with them.
D. She told me if I was coming with us.
Answer: B. She asked me if I was coming with them.

Question → asked + if/whether, you → I, us → them।

Question 3 SSC CPO 2022

Change to Indirect Speech:

He said to me, "Please help me."

A. He requested me to help him.
B. He ordered me to help him.
C. He said me to help him.
D. He requested that help him.
Answer: A. He requested me to help him.

'Please' → request → requested, me → him।

Question 4 SSC MTS 2022

Change to Indirect Speech:

The teacher said, "The sun rises in the east."

A. The teacher said that the sun rises in the east.
B. The teacher said that the sun rose in the east.
C. The teacher said that the sun had risen in the east.
D. The teacher said that the sun is rising in the east.
Answer: A. The teacher said that the sun rises in the east.

Universal Truth → tense नहीं बदलता।

Question 5 SSC CGL 2023

Change to Indirect Speech:

He said, "What a beautiful painting!"

A. He exclaimed with wonder that it was a beautiful painting.
B. He said what a beautiful painting.
C. He exclaimed that it was a beautiful painting.
D. He said that it is a beautiful painting.
Answer: A. He exclaimed with wonder that it was a beautiful painting.

Exclamatory sentence → exclaimed with wonder।

Question 6 SSC CHSL 2023

Change to Indirect Speech:

He said to me, "Don't waste your time."

A. He ordered me not to waste my time.
B. He advised me not to waste my time.
C. He told me don't waste my time.
D. He said me not to waste my time.
Answer: A. He ordered me not to waste my time.

Negative imperative → ordered + not to + V1, your → my।

Question 7 SSC CPO 2023

Change to Indirect Speech:

She said, "Where do you live?"

A. She asked where I lived.
B. She asked that where I lived.
C. She asked where do I live.
D. She asked if where I lived.
Answer: A. She asked where I lived.

Wh-question → wh-word + subject + verb (you → I, do live → lived)।

Question 8 SSC MTS 2023

Change to Indirect Speech:

He said, "I will meet you tomorrow."

A. He said that he would meet me the next day.
B. He said that he will meet me tomorrow.
C. He said that he would meet me tomorrow.
D. He said that he will meet me the next day.
Answer: A. He said that he would meet me the next day.

will → would, you → me, tomorrow → the next day।

16. Quick Revision – Direct & Indirect Speech

Rule 1 Present → Past
Rule 2 Past → Past Perfect
Rule 3 Future → Would/Should
Rule 4 SON Rule (Pronouns)
Rule 5 Said to → told
Rule 6 Question → if/whether or Wh-word
Rule 7 Imperative → to + V1
Rule 8 Exclamatory → exclaimed
Rule 9 Optative → wished/prayed
Rule 10 Universal Truth → No Change
🎯 SSC Narration Final Checklist
  • Reporting Verb: said → told (with object), said (without object)।
  • Tense Change: Present → Past, Past → Past Perfect, Future → Would/Should।
  • Pronoun Change: SON Rule – Subject, Object, No Change।
  • Time/Place: now→then, today→that day, here→there, this→that।
  • Assertive: that + tense change।
  • Interrogative: if/whether (Yes/No) या Wh-word + subject + verb।
  • Imperative: to + V1 (order/request/advice)।
  • Exclamatory: exclaimed with joy/sorrow/surprise/wonder।
  • Optative: wished/prayed + that + might।
  • Universal Truth: tense नहीं बदलता।
