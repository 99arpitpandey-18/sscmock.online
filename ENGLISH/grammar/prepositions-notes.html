1. Preposition क्या होता है?

Preposition वह word है जो किसी noun या pronoun का relation दूसरे word के साथ बताता है।

आसान भाषा में: Preposition = Relation बताने वाला word.

The book is on the table.

किताब मेज पर है।

यहाँ on book और table के बीच relation बता रहा है।

He is in the room.

वह कमरे में है।

She went to Delhi.

वह Delhi गई।

3. Prepositions of Place

In – में / अंदर

The boy is in the room.

लड़का कमरे में है।

There is water in the glass.

गिलास में पानी है।

On – पर

The book is on the table.

किताब मेज पर है।

There is a picture on the wall.

दीवार पर एक तस्वीर है।

Under – नीचे

The cat is under the table.

बिल्ली मेज के नीचे है।

Behind – पीछे

He is standing behind me.

वह मेरे पीछे खड़ा है।

In front of – सामने

The car is in front of the house.

कार घर के सामने है।

4. Prepositions of Time

At – Exact Time

The train arrives at 6 o'clock.

ट्रेन 6 बजे आती है।

I wake up at 5 a.m..

I sleep at night.

On – Day / Date

I will meet you on Monday.

मैं सोमवार को तुमसे मिलूँगा।

He was born on 15 August.

In – Month / Year / Long Period

I was born in 2008.

He came here in January.

We will finish it in two days.

5. IN / ON / AT – SSC का सबसे Important Topic

Preposition Use Examples
At Exact time at 5 pm, at noon, at night
On Day / Date on Monday, on 15 August
In Month / Year / Long period in July, in 2026, in winter
⭐ SSC Shortcut

AT = छोटा / Exact Time

ON = Day / Date

IN = बड़ा Time Period

6. Since और For

Since – Starting Point

Since का प्रयोग किसी निश्चित starting point के लिए होता है।

I have lived here since 2020.

मैं 2020 से यहाँ रह रहा हूँ।

He has been ill since Monday.

वह सोमवार से बीमार है।

For – Duration

I have lived here for five years.

मैं पाँच साल से यहाँ रह रहा हूँ।

He has been waiting for two hours.

याद रखने की Trick

Since = कब से?

For = कितने समय से?

7. By और Until / Till

By – Deadline

By का अर्थ है किसी समय तक काम पूरा हो जाना चाहिए।

Submit the form by Monday.

सोमवार तक form जमा कर दो।

I will finish the work by 5 p.m..

Until / Till – उस समय तक जारी रहना

Wait here until 5 p.m..

5 बजे तक यहाँ इंतजार करो।

He worked until midnight.

Important Difference

By 5 pm = 5 बजे तक काम complete हो जाएगा।

Until 5 pm = 5 बजे तक काम/स्थिति जारी रहेगी।

8. Between और Among

Between

Traditional rule के अनुसार between का प्रयोग दो persons/things के बीच किया जाता है।

Divide the money between Ram and Shyam.

पैसे राम और श्याम के बीच बाँटो।

Among

सामान्यतः group या तीन या अधिक लोगों/चीजों के बीच के लिए among का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

He was sitting among his friends.

वह अपने दोस्तों के बीच बैठा था।

SSC Note

Modern English में context के अनुसार between तीन या अधिक लोगों/चीजों के साथ भी आ सकता है, लेकिन basic exam rule में:

Between → two

Among → group

9. Beside और Besides

Beside = बगल में

He sat beside me.

वह मेरे बगल में बैठा।

Besides = के अलावा

Besides English, he knows Hindi.

English के अलावा वह Hindi जानता है।

Trick

Beside = Side

Besides = Extra / In addition to

10. In और Into

In = अंदर होना

He is in the room.

वह कमरे में है।

Into = अंदर जाने की movement

He went into the room.

वह कमरे के अंदर गया।

The boy jumped into the river.

Trick

In = Position

Into = Movement

11. On और Onto

On = किसी surface पर होना

The book is on the table.

Onto = surface पर movement

The cat jumped onto the table.

बिल्ली मेज पर कूद गई।

12. Above और Over

Above

जब एक चीज दूसरी चीज से ऊँचाई पर हो तो above का प्रयोग हो सकता है।

The fan is above my head.

The temperature is above 40°C.

Over

Over का प्रयोग ऊपर होने के साथ-साथ covering, movement या greater quantity के अर्थ में भी हो सकता है।

The plane flew over the city.

He put a blanket over the child.

There were over 100 students.

13. Below और Under

Below

The temperature is below zero.

Write your name below the line.

Under

The cat is under the table.

He is standing under the tree.

14. Across और Through

Across = एक side से दूसरी side

He walked across the road.

वह सड़क के एक किनारे से दूसरे किनारे गया।

They swam across the river.

Through = अंदर से होकर

He walked through the forest.

वह जंगल के अंदर से होकर चला।

The train passed through the tunnel.

15. From और To

He came from Delhi.

वह Delhi से आया।

He went to Delhi.

वह Delhi गया।

From ... to

The office is open from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.

He travelled from Delhi to Mumbai.

16. With और By

With = Instrument / Tool

He cut the apple with a knife.

उसने चाकू से apple काटा।

She wrote the letter with a pen.

By = Agent / Means

The letter was written by Ram.

पत्र Ram द्वारा लिखा गया।

I travelled by train.

मैं train से गया।

Shortcut

With = किस चीज से?

By = किसके द्वारा / किस माध्यम से?

17. Of और Off

Of = संबंध / belonging

The colour of the sky is blue.

This is the roof of the house.

Off = अलग / हटकर

The plane took off.

Take your shoes off.

He fell off the bicycle.

22. Common Errors – SSC

Incorrect Correct Rule
He is good in English. He is good at English. good at
I am interested on music. I am interested in music. interested in
He depends of his father. He depends on his father. depend on
Listen me. Listen to me. listen to
He is suffering with fever. He is suffering from fever. suffer from
She is married with him. She is married to him. married to
He insisted to go. He insisted on going. insist on
He entered into the room. He entered the room. Enter generally takes direct object.
Discuss about the matter. Discuss the matter. Discuss does not normally take “about”.
Return back home. Return home. “Back” is unnecessary here.

23. SSC Tricks

🔥 Trick 1 – Since vs For

Since = Starting Point

For = Duration

Since Monday ✔

For two days ✔

🔥 Trick 2 – By vs Until

By = deadline / काम पूरा होने की सीमा

Until = किसी समय तक जारी रहना

🔥 Trick 3 – In vs Into

In = position

Into = movement

🔥 Trick 4 – Beside vs Besides

Beside = बगल में

Besides = के अलावा

🔥 Trick 5 – With vs By

With = instrument

By = agent / means

24. SSC PYQ-Based / Exam Pattern Practice

Question 1 SSC Pattern

He has been living here _____ 2020.

A. for
B. since
C. from
D. by
Answer: B. since

2020 एक starting point है।

Question 2 SSC Pattern

He has been waiting _____ two hours.

A. since
B. from
C. for
D. by
Answer: C. for

Two hours duration है।

Question 3 SSC Pattern

He is good _____ Mathematics.

A. in
B. on
C. at
D. with
Answer: C. at

Correct expression: good at.

Question 4 SSC Pattern

The boy jumped _____ the river.

A. in
B. into
C. on
D. at
Answer: B. into

Jumping अंदर की ओर movement है।

Question 5 SSC Pattern

He sat _____ me.

A. besides
B. beside
C. among
D. between
Answer: B. beside

Beside = बगल में।

Question 6 SSC Pattern

The train passed _____ the tunnel.

A. across
B. through
C. on
D. above
Answer: B. through

Tunnel के अंदर से होकर जाना → through.

Question 7 SSC Pattern

He travelled _____ train.

A. with
B. by
C. on
D. from
Answer: B. by

Means of transport → by train.

Question 8 SSC Pattern

He insisted _____ going there.

A. to
B. for
C. on
D. at
Answer: C. on

Fixed expression: insist on.

Error Detection Practice

Find the error:

He is good in English.

Error: in

Correct: He is good at English.

Find the error:

He depends of his parents.

Error: of

Correct: He depends on his parents.

Find the error:

She is interested on music.

Error: on

Correct: She is interested in music.
