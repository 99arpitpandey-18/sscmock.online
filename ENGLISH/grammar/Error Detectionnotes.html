1. Error Detection क्या होता है?

Error Detection या Error Spotting में एक sentence को अलग-अलग parts में दिया जाता है और हमें grammatical error वाले part को पहचानना होता है।

SSC exams में Error Detection के questions में मुख्य रूप से Subject-Verb Agreement, Tense, Article, Pronoun, Preposition, Conjunction, Comparison और अन्य grammar rules को test किया जाता है।

⭐ सबसे Important बात

Error Detection में केवल sentence को पढ़ना पर्याप्त नहीं है। पहले sentence का grammatical structure पहचानें और फिर rule apply करें।

He go to school every day.

यहाँ go गलत है।

Correct: He goes to school every day.

2. SSC Error Detection Pattern

Error Detection questions में sentence को सामान्यतः चार parts में divide किया जाता है।

(A) He

(B) go

(C) to school

(D) every day.

🎯 Exam Approach

पहले Subject identify करें।

फिर Main Verb check करें।

उसके बाद Tense, Article, Pronoun, Preposition और बाकी grammatical structures check करें।

3. Error Detection – Golden Rules

🔥 Golden Rule 1 – Subject + Verb

Singular subject के साथ singular verb और plural subject के साथ plural verb का सही प्रयोग करें।

He goes to school.

They go to school.

🔥 Golden Rule 2 – Tense Consistency

Sentence में time expression और tense को carefully check करें।

He went to Delhi yesterday.

🔥 Golden Rule 3 – Structure Check

केवल individual words नहीं, पूरे grammatical structure को check करें।

He is senior to me.

❌ He is senior than me.

4. Subject-Verb Agreement Errors

Subject और verb का number और person में agreement होना चाहिए।

❌ He go to school.

✅ He goes to school.

❌ They goes to school.

✅ They go to school.

Each / Every

❌ Each of the boys are present.

✅ Each of the boys is present.

❌ Every student have a book.

✅ Every student has a book.

One of + Plural Noun + Singular Verb

❌ One of my friends are a doctor.

✅ One of my friends is a doctor.

Either / Neither

Neither of the boys is ready.

Either of these books is useful.

5. Tense Errors

Sentence में time expression और verb form के बीच consistency होना बहुत important है।

❌ He goes to Delhi yesterday.

✅ He went to Delhi yesterday.

Since / For

❌ He is living here since 2020.

✅ He has been living here since 2020.

❌ She has been ill since two days.

✅ She has been ill for two days.

Yesterday / Last / Ago

Yesterday, last week, last year, ago आदि past time expressions के साथ सामान्यतः simple past का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

❌ I have seen him yesterday.

✅ I saw him yesterday.

6. Article Errors

A, An और The का गलत प्रयोग Error Detection में बहुत common है।

❌ He is an university student.

✅ He is a university student.

❌ He is a honest man.

✅ He is an honest man.

Superlative

❌ He is best player in the team.

✅ He is the best player in the team.

River Names

❌ Ganga is a holy river.

✅ The Ganga is a holy river.

7. Pronoun Errors

Pronoun का number, person और case noun के according होना चाहिए।

❌ Everyone should do their work carefully.

Exam-oriented traditional grammar में: Everyone should do his or her work carefully.

Between + Objective Case

❌ This secret is between you and I.

✅ This secret is between you and me.

Subjective Case

❌ Him and I went to the market.

✅ He and I went to the market.

8. Preposition Errors

कई verbs और adjectives के साथ fixed prepositions आते हैं।

❌ He is good in English.

✅ He is good at English.

❌ He is senior than me.

✅ He is senior to me.

Common Fixed Prepositions

Word Correct Preposition Example
Good at good at English
Interested in interested in music
Depend on depend on him
Insist on insist on going
Senior to senior to me

9. Adjective Errors

Adjective noun या pronoun की quality, quantity या state बताता है।

Much / Many

❌ There are much students in the class.

✅ There are many students in the class.

Few / Little

Few → countable nouns

Little → uncountable nouns

Few students were present.

There is little water in the bottle.

10. Adverb Errors

Adverb का सही placement और form sentence के meaning को affect करता है।

❌ He runs very fastly.

✅ He runs very fast.

❌ She sings beautiful.

✅ She sings beautifully.

11. Conjunction Errors

Conjunctions को उनके corresponding structures के साथ सही तरीके से use करना चाहिए।

Though / Although

❌ Although he is poor but he is honest.

✅ Although he is poor, he is honest.

Because / Therefore

❌ Because he was ill, therefore he did not come.

✅ Because he was ill, he did not come.

Either...or

Either Ram or Shyam will come.

Neither...nor

Neither Ram nor Shyam was present.

12. Modal Errors

Can, could, may, might, shall, should, will, would, must आदि modal auxiliaries के बाद generally verb की base form आती है।

❌ He can goes there.

✅ He can go there.

❌ You should to work hard.

✅ You should work hard.

13. Infinitive Errors

सामान्यतः infinitive का structure: to + V1 होता है।

❌ He wants to went there.

✅ He wants to go there.

Let / Make / Help

❌ Let me to go.

✅ Let me go.

❌ He made me to laugh.

✅ He made me laugh.

14. Gerund Errors

कुछ verbs और prepositions के बाद gerund यानी V-ing का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

❌ He is fond of play cricket.

✅ He is fond of playing cricket.

❌ I am interested in learn English.

✅ I am interested in learning English.

15. Comparison Errors

Comparative structures SSC में बहुत frequently पूछे जाते हैं।

❌ He is more better than me.

✅ He is better than me.

Senior / Junior / Inferior / Superior

❌ He is senior than me.

✅ He is senior to me.

❌ This quality is superior than that.

✅ This quality is superior to that.

Double Comparative

❌ The more you study, the more better you become.

✅ The more you study, the better you become.

16. Parallelism Errors

जब sentence में दो या अधिक समान grammatical structures दिए हों तो उनका form समान होना चाहिए।

❌ He likes reading, writing and to play.

✅ He likes reading, writing and playing.

❌ She likes to sing, dancing and reading.

✅ She likes singing, dancing and reading.

17. Redundancy Errors

Redundancy का अर्थ है sentence में unnecessary word का दोबारा या गलत तरीके से प्रयोग।

❌ He returned back home.

✅ He returned home.

❌ Discuss about the matter.

✅ Discuss the matter.

❌ Revert back to me.

✅ Revert to me.

18. Determiner Errors

Each / Every

Each boy has a book.

Every student was present.

Much / Many

Much water

Many students

Fewer / Less

❌ There are less students in the class.

✅ There are fewer students in the class.

Fewer → countable

Less → uncountable

19. Common Word Errors

Wrong Usage Correct Usage Example
Discuss about Discuss We discussed the matter.
Order for Order He ordered food.
Enter into Enter He entered the room.
Return back Return He returned home.
Revert back Revert Please revert soon.
Prefer than Prefer to I prefer tea to coffee.
Senior than Senior to He is senior to me.
Different than Different from This is different from that.

20. Common SSC Errors

Incorrect Correct Rule
He go to school. He goes to school. Singular subject → singular verb
She have a car. She has a car. He/She/It → has
He is an university student. He is a university student. University → /yuː/ sound
He is a honest man. He is an honest man. Silent H
He is senior than me. He is senior to me. Senior + to
He discussed about the issue. He discussed the issue. Discuss does not take about
She is good in English. She is good at English. Good at
Although he is poor but he is honest. Although he is poor, he is honest. Although + no but
He can goes there. He can go there. Modal + V1
Let me to go. Let me go. Let + object + V1
He returned back home. He returned home. Avoid redundancy
He is more better than me. He is better than me. No double comparative
I have seen him yesterday. I saw him yesterday. Yesterday → Simple Past
Neither of them are ready. Neither of them is ready. Neither → singular verb in traditional SSC grammar
He is interested to learn English. He is interested in learning English. Interested in + V-ing

21. SSC Error Detection Tricks

🔥 Trick 1 – पहले Verb पकड़ो

Sentence पढ़ते समय सबसे पहले main verb को identify करें।

He go to school.

Subject = He → Verb = goes.

🔥 Trick 2 – Time Expression Check

Yesterday, last, ago, since, for, already, yet आदि words देखकर tense check करें।

🔥 Trick 3 – Fixed Preposition

Senior to

Interested in

Good at

Depend on

🔥 Trick 4 – Modal के बाद V1

can go ✔

should work ✔

must study ✔

can goes ❌

🔥 Trick 5 – Double Error Patterns

more better ❌

most fastest ❌

return back ❌

discuss about ❌

🔥 Trick 6 – Correlative Conjunction

Either...or ✔

Neither...nor ✔

Both...and ✔

Not only...but also ✔

22. SSC Error Detection Practice

Question 1 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

He go to school every day.

A. He
B. go
C. to school
D. every day
Answer: B. go

He singular subject है, इसलिए goes होना चाहिए।

Correct: He goes to school every day.

Question 2 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

He is an university student.

A. He
B. is
C. an university
D. student
Answer: C. an university

University का pronunciation “yu” sound से शुरू होता है। इसलिए a university होगा।

Question 3 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

He is senior than me.

A. He
B. is
C. senior than
D. me
Answer: C. senior than

Senior के साथ “to” का प्रयोग होता है।

Correct: He is senior to me.

Question 4 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

He can goes there.

A. He
B. can
C. goes
D. there
Answer: C. goes

Modal auxiliary “can” के बाद base form यानी V1 आती है।

Correct: He can go there.

Question 5 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

Although he is poor but he is honest.

A. Although
B. he is poor
C. but
D. he is honest
Answer: C. but

Although के साथ generally but का प्रयोग नहीं किया जाता।

Correct: Although he is poor, he is honest.

Question 6 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

I have seen him yesterday.

A. I
B. have seen
C. him
D. yesterday
Answer: B. have seen

Yesterday definite past time है, इसलिए simple past का प्रयोग होगा।

Correct: I saw him yesterday.

Question 7 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

He discussed about the problem.

A. He
B. discussed
C. about
D. the problem
Answer: C. about

Discuss के बाद direct object आता है। “Discuss about” standard exam grammar में गलत माना जाता है।

Correct: He discussed the problem.

Question 8 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

He is more better than his brother.

A. He
B. is
C. more better
D. than his brother
Answer: C. more better

Better स्वयं comparative form है। इसके साथ more नहीं लगाया जाता।

Correct: He is better than his brother.

Question 9 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

He can able to solve this problem.

A. He
B. can able
C. to solve
D. this problem
Answer: B. can able

Can और able दोनों ability express करते हैं। दोनों को एक साथ नहीं लगाना चाहिए।

Correct: He can solve this problem.

Question 10 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

She is good in English.

A. She
B. is
C. good in
D. English
Answer: C. good in

किसी subject/skill में proficiency बताने के लिए “good at” का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Correct: She is good at English.

Question 11 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

Each of the boys are ready.

A. Each
B. of the boys
C. are
D. ready
Answer: C. are

Each singular होता है, इसलिए singular verb “is” आएगी।

Correct: Each of the boys is ready.

Question 12 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

Let me to go there.

A. Let
B. me
C. to go
D. there
Answer: C. to go

Let + object + V1 होता है।

Correct: Let me go there.

Question 13 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

He returned back home yesterday.

A. He
B. returned back
C. home
D. yesterday
Answer: B. returned back

Return में वापस आने का meaning already होता है। “back” unnecessary है।

Correct: He returned home yesterday.

Question 14 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

Neither of the boys are present.

A. Neither
B. of the boys
C. are
D. present
Answer: C. are

Traditional SSC grammar pattern में Neither को singular माना जाता है।

Correct: Neither of the boys is present.

Question 15 SSC Pattern

Find the error:

I am interested to learn English.

A. I
B. am interested
C. to learn
D. English
Answer: C. to learn

Interested के बाद “in + V-ing” structure आता है।

Correct: I am interested in learning English.

23. Quick Revision – Error Detection

Rule 1 Subject-Verb Agreement check करें।
Rule 2 He/She/It → V1 + s/es
Rule 3 Each / Every → Singular Verb
Rule 4 Modal + V1
Rule 5 Yesterday → Simple Past
Rule 6 Senior + to
Rule 7 Good + at
Rule 8 Interested + in + V-ing
Rule 9 Discuss + Object
Rule 10 Let + Object + V1
Rule 11 No Double Comparative
Rule 12 Avoid unnecessary words
🎯 SSC Error Detection Final Checklist
  • सबसे पहले Subject और Main Verb identify करें।
  • Subject-Verb Agreement check करें।
  • Yesterday / ago / last जैसे words से tense check करें।
  • A / An / The का सही प्रयोग देखें।
  • Pronoun का case और number check करें।
  • Fixed Prepositions याद रखें।
  • Modal के बाद V1 लगाएँ।
  • Let / Make के बाद bare infinitive लगाएँ।
  • Comparative में double comparison न करें।
  • Senior / Junior / Superior / Inferior के साथ “to”।
  • Discuss के साथ “about” न लगाएँ।
  • Although के साथ unnecessary “but” न लगाएँ।
  • Redundant words जैसे return back से बचें।
  • Parallel structures का form समान रखें।
  • पूरे sentence का meaning और structure दोनों check करें।
