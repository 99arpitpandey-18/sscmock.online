1. Conjunction क्या होते हैं?

Conjunction वे words हैं जो दो या दो से अधिक words, phrases या clauses को जोड़ने का काम करते हैं।

आसान भाषा में, Conjunction का काम है — जोड़ना (joining)।

⭐ सबसे पहले यह याद रखें

Conjunction = Joining Word

इसका मुख्य काम words, phrases और clauses को जोड़ना है।

Ram and Shyam are friends.

राम और श्याम दोस्त हैं।

He is poor but honest.

वह गरीब लेकिन ईमानदार है।

Work hard or you will fail.

मेहनत करो या तुम असफल हो जाओगे।

2. Types of Conjunctions

Type Meaning Important Conjunctions Example
Coordinating समान स्तर के words/clauses जोड़ना and, but, or, nor, yet, so, for Ram and Shyam came.
Subordinating Main और dependent clause जोड़ना because, although, if, unless, when, while I stayed because it rained.
Correlative Pair में काम करते हैं either...or, neither...nor Either Ram or Shyam will come.

3. Coordinating Conjunctions

Coordinating conjunctions दो समान grammatical units को जोड़ते हैं। ये दो words, phrases या independent clauses को जोड़ सकते हैं।

Ram and Shyam came.

He is poor but honest.

Hurry up or you will miss the train.

⭐ Important

Coordinating conjunctions को याद रखने के लिए FANBOYS सबसे important shortcut है।

4. FANBOYS Rule

Letter Conjunction Meaning
F For क्योंकि / के लिए
A And और
N Nor न ही
B But लेकिन
O Or या
Y Yet फिर भी / लेकिन
S So इसलिए
🔥 SSC Trick

FANBOYS = For + And + Nor + But + Or + Yet + So

अगर question में coordinating conjunction पूछी जाए, FANBOYS तुरंत याद करें।

5. AND का प्रयोग

And का प्रयोग समान या संबंधित ideas, words, phrases और clauses को जोड़ने के लिए किया जाता है।

Ram and Shyam are brothers.

Tea and coffee are popular drinks.

She bought books and pens.

He worked hard and passed the exam.

AND से दो Clauses जोड़ना

He came home and he went to sleep.

She studied hard and she passed.

6. BUT का प्रयोग

But contrast या opposition दिखाता है।

He is poor but honest.

She is intelligent but careless.

He worked hard but failed.

❌ Although he is poor, but he is honest.

✅ Although he is poor, he is honest.

⭐ Important SSC Rule

Although/Though के साथ But का प्रयोग नहीं किया जाता।

7. OR का प्रयोग

Or का प्रयोग choice, alternative या possibility बताने के लिए किया जाता है।

Tea or coffee?

Hurry up or you will miss the bus.

You can take a pen or a pencil.

🔥 SSC Trick

OR = Choice / Alternative

8. NOR का प्रयोग

Nor का अर्थ होता है “न ही”। इसका प्रयोग negative alternatives को जोड़ने में किया जाता है।

He does not drink nor smoke.

She neither called nor wrote.

He is not rich, nor is he famous.

⭐ Remember

Neither → Nor

Either → Or

9. YET का प्रयोग

Yet का प्रयोग contrast या unexpected result बताने के लिए किया जाता है।

He is poor yet happy.

She is young yet very wise.

He worked hard, yet he failed.

10. SO का प्रयोग

So का प्रयोग result या consequence बताने के लिए किया जाता है।

It was raining, so we stayed home.

He was tired, so he went to bed.

Work hard, so you may succeed.

❌ Because he was ill, so he did not come.

✅ Because he was ill, he did not come.

11. FOR का Conjunction के रूप में प्रयोग

For कभी-कभी conjunction के रूप में reason बताता है। इसका meaning “because” के समान हो सकता है।

He must be tired, for he worked all day.

We stayed indoors, for it was raining.

Exam Tip

यहाँ for preposition नहीं बल्कि conjunction है।

12. Subordinating Conjunctions

Subordinating conjunction एक dependent clause को main clause से जोड़ता है।

I stayed at home because it was raining.

Although he was tired, he continued working.

I will call you when I arrive.

Conjunction Meaning Example
Because क्योंकि I stayed because it rained.
Although हालाँकि Although he was tired, he worked.
If यदि If you work hard, you will succeed.
Unless जब तक नहीं Unless you study, you will fail.
When जब Call me when you arrive.
While जबकि / जब He came while I was sleeping.

13. Because / Since / As

इन conjunctions का प्रयोग reason या cause बताने के लिए किया जाता है।

I stayed home because it was raining.

Since you are tired, take some rest.

As it was late, we went home.

❌ Because he was ill, therefore he stayed home.

✅ Because he was ill, he stayed home.

⭐ SSC Rule

Because के साथ सामान्यतः therefore को एक ही structure में नहीं लगाते।

14. Although / Though / Even Though

इनका प्रयोग contrast बताने के लिए किया जाता है।

Although he is poor, he is honest.

Though he was tired, he continued working.

Even though it was raining, we went outside.

❌ Although he is poor, but he is honest.

✅ Although he is poor, he is honest.

❌ Though he was tired, but he worked.

✅ Though he was tired, he worked.

15. IF / UNLESS

IF

If condition बताता है।

If you work hard, you will succeed.

If it rains, we will stay home.

UNLESS

Unless का अर्थ है if...not यानी “यदि नहीं”।

Unless you study, you will fail.

= If you do not study, you will fail.

❌ Unless you do not work hard, you will fail.

✅ Unless you work hard, you will fail.

⭐ Golden Rule

Unless = If...not

इसलिए Unless के साथ unnecessary “not” से बचें।

16. UNTIL / TILL

Until और Till का प्रयोग किसी समय तक की स्थिति बताने के लिए किया जाता है।

Wait until I come back.

Stay here till I return.

He waited until the train arrived.

🔥 Trick

Until / Till = किसी समय तक

17. BEFORE / AFTER

Wash your hands before you eat.

After he arrived, we started dinner.

Think before you speak.

We went home after the match ended.

18. WHILE / WHEN

While का प्रयोग अक्सर दो actions के एक साथ होने या contrast के लिए किया जाता है।

I was reading while he was sleeping.

While I was walking, it started raining.

Call me when you reach home.

While = Whereas

Ram is hardworking, while Shyam is careless.

19. LEST

Lest का अर्थ होता है “ऐसा न हो कि” / “for fear that”।

Work hard lest you should fail.

He ran fast lest he should miss the train.

⭐ SSC Favourite Rule

Lest के बाद traditional exam grammar में should + V1 का प्रयोग बहुत common है।

20. Correlative Conjunctions

Correlative conjunctions हमेशा pair में काम करते हैं।

Pair Meaning Example
Either...or या तो...या Either Ram or Shyam will come.
Neither...nor न तो...न ही Neither Ram nor Shyam came.
Both...and दोनों...और Both Ram and Shyam came.
Not only...but also न केवल...बल्कि He is not only smart but also honest.
Whether...or चाहे...या Whether you agree or not.

21. Either...Or

Either...or का प्रयोग दो alternatives में से किसी एक को बताने के लिए किया जाता है।

Either Ram or Shyam will come.

You can either study or play.

⭐ Subject-Verb Agreement

Either...or में verb अक्सर उस subject के according होती है जो verb के सबसे पास होता है।

Either Ram or his friends are coming.

Either his friends or Ram is coming.

22. Neither...Nor

Neither...nor का अर्थ है “न तो...न ही”।

Neither Ram nor Shyam came.

He likes neither tea nor coffee.

⭐ Important

Neither...nor पहले से negative meaning देता है। इसलिए unnecessary “not” न लगाएँ।

❌ He does not like neither tea nor coffee.

✅ He likes neither tea nor coffee.

23. Both...And

Both...and का प्रयोग दो positive subjects या ideas को जोड़ने के लिए किया जाता है।

Both Ram and Shyam are intelligent.

She is both intelligent and hardworking.

He can speak both Hindi and English.

⭐ SSC Rule

Both...and के साथ सामान्यतः plural verb आता है।

24. Not Only...But Also

इसका प्रयोग दो related या additional qualities/actions को जोड़ने के लिए किया जाता है।

He is not only intelligent but also hardworking.

She can not only sing but also dance.

He is not only a teacher but also a writer.

🔥 Parallel Structure

Not only के बाद जो grammatical form है, but also के बाद भी उसी प्रकार की form रखना बेहतर होता है।

25. Common Errors – SSC

Incorrect Correct Reason
Although he is poor, but he is honest. Although he is poor, he is honest. Although के साथ But नहीं।
Because he was ill, so he stayed home. Because he was ill, he stayed home. Because + so का double conjunction structure नहीं।
Unless you do not study, you will fail. Unless you study, you will fail. Unless already negative meaning देता है।
Neither Ram or Shyam came. Neither Ram nor Shyam came. Neither → Nor pair।
Either Ram and Shyam will come. Either Ram or Shyam will come. Either → Or pair।
Both Ram or Shyam came. Both Ram and Shyam came. Both → And pair।
He is not only intelligent but he is also honest. He is not only intelligent but also honest. Better parallel structure।
Though he is poor but he is honest. Though he is poor, he is honest. Though के साथ But नहीं।
He neither drinks nor he smokes. He neither drinks nor smokes. Parallel structure।
Both the boys is intelligent. Both the boys are intelligent. Both के साथ plural verb।

26. SSC Conjunctions Tricks

🔥 Trick 1 – FANBOYS

For – And – Nor – But – Or – Yet – So

🔥 Trick 2 – Fixed Pairs

Either → Or

Neither → Nor

Both → And

Not only → But also

Whether → Or

🔥 Trick 3 – Double Conjunction से बचें

Although + But ❌

Because + So ❌

Though + But ❌

🔥 Trick 4 – Unless

Unless = If...not

इसलिए Unless के साथ unnecessary “not” नहीं।

🔥 Trick 5 – Neither / Either

Neither → Nor

Either → Or

🔥 Trick 6 – Both

Both → And

Both के साथ generally plural verb।

27. SSC PYQ-Based / Exam Pattern Practice

Question 1 SSC Pattern

Although he is poor, _____ he is honest.

A. but
B. and
C. or
D. no conjunction
Answer: D. no conjunction

Although के साथ But का प्रयोग नहीं किया जाता।

Question 2 SSC Pattern

Work hard _____ you will fail.

A. and
B. or
C. but
D. because
Answer: B. or

यहाँ alternative/result warning है — Work hard or you will fail.

Question 3 SSC Pattern

Neither Ram _____ Shyam was present.

A. or
B. and
C. nor
D. but
Answer: C. nor

Neither → Nor fixed pair है।

Question 4 SSC Pattern

Either Ram _____ Shyam will represent the team.

A. and
B. nor
C. or
D. but
Answer: C. or

Either → Or fixed pair है।

Question 5 SSC Pattern

He stayed at home _____ he was ill.

A. because
B. but
C. or
D. yet
Answer: A. because

Because reason/cause बताता है।

Question 6 SSC Pattern

He is poor _____ honest.

A. or
B. but
C. so
D. because
Answer: B. but

But contrast दिखाता है।

Question 7 SSC Pattern

Both Ram _____ Shyam are intelligent.

A. or
B. nor
C. and
D. but
Answer: C. and

Both → And fixed pair है।

Question 8 SSC Pattern

He is not only intelligent _____ also hardworking.

A. and
B. but
C. or
D. nor
Answer: B. but

Not only → But also fixed pair है।

Error Detection Practice

Find the error:

Although he is poor, but he is honest.

Error: but

Correct: Although he is poor, he is honest.

Find the error:

Neither Ram or Shyam came.

Error: or

Correct: Neither Ram nor Shyam came.

Find the error:

Unless you do not work hard, you will fail.

Error: do not

Correct: Unless you work hard, you will fail.
