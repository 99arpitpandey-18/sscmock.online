1. What is Tense? – Tense क्या है?

Tense is the form of a verb that shows the time of an action or state.

हिंदी: Tense क्रिया के उस रूप को कहते हैं जिससे पता चलता है कि कोई काम वर्तमान में, भूतकाल में या भविष्य में हुआ, हो रहा है या होगा।

⭐ Basic Concept Tense mainly tells us two things:
  1. Action कब हुआ?
  2. Action किस अवस्था में है?
Examples

I play cricket.
मैं क्रिकेट खेलता हूँ।

I played cricket.
मैंने क्रिकेट खेला।

I will play cricket.
मैं क्रिकेट खेलूँगा।

2. Tense की पहचान कैसे करें?

Hindi sentence में verb के ending words देखकर अक्सर tense की पहचान की जा सकती है।

Tense सामान्य Hindi पहचान Example
Present Simple ता है / ती है / ते हैं वह स्कूल जाता है।
Present Continuous रहा है / रही है / रहे हैं वह पढ़ रहा है।
Present Perfect चुका है / चुकी है / चुके हैं / किया है वह काम कर चुका है।
Present Perfect Continuous से रहा है / से रही है वह दो घंटे से पढ़ रहा है।
Past Simple किया / गया / आया / खाया वह कल आया।
Past Continuous रहा था / रही थी / रहे थे वह पढ़ रहा था।
Past Perfect चुका था / कर लिया था वह जा चुका था।
Past Perfect Continuous से रहा था / से रही थी वह दो घंटे से पढ़ रहा था।
Future Simple गा / गी / गे वह आएगा।

Future Perfect Continuous से रहा होगा वह दो घंटे से पढ़ रहा होगा।

3. Tenses का Basic Structure

Time Simple Continuous Perfect Perfect Continuous
Present V1 / V1+s/es is/am/are + V-ing has/have + V3 has/have been + V-ing
Past V2 was/were + V-ing had + V3 had been + V-ing
Future will + V1 will be + V-ing will have + V3 will have been + V-ing

4. Present Tense – वर्तमान काल

Present Tense का प्रयोग वर्तमान समय में होने वाली, सामान्यतः होने वाली या अभी तक पूरी हुई actions के लिए किया जाता है।

⭐ Present Tense के 4 प्रकार
  1. Present Simple / Indefinite
  2. Present Continuous
  3. Present Perfect
  4. Present Perfect Continuous

5. Present Simple Tense

पहचान

Hindi में सामान्यतः: ता है, ती है, ते हैं आदि आते हैं।

Structure – Affirmative

Subject + V1(s/es) + Object Examples: He plays cricket. She reads a book. They play football.

Rule

Important Rule He / She / It / Singular Subject के साथ main verb में s/es लगाया जाता है।
Examples – बहुत महत्वपूर्ण

He plays cricket.
वह क्रिकेट खेलता है।

She reads English books.
वह English की किताबें पढ़ती है।

Ram goes to school.
राम स्कूल जाता है।

My brother works in Delhi.
मेरा भाई दिल्ली में काम करता है।

The sun rises in the east.
सूर्य पूर्व में उगता है।

Water boils at 100°C.
पानी 100°C पर उबलता है।

Negative

I / We / You / They + do not + V1 He / She / It + does not + V1
❌ Important Error Does/doesn't के बाद हमेशा V1 आता है।

❌ He doesn't goes to school.

✅ He doesn't go to school.

Interrogative

Do + Subject + V1 ...? Does + Subject + V1 ...?

Do you play cricket?
क्या तुम क्रिकेट खेलते हो?

Does he work here?
क्या वह यहाँ काम करता है?

Does she speak English?
क्या वह English बोलती है?

Uses of Present Simple

  1. Habit: I wake up early.
  2. Universal Truth: The earth revolves around the sun.
  3. General Truth: Honesty is the best policy.
  4. Regular Action: He goes to the gym every day.
  5. Permanent Situation: She lives in Delhi.
  6. Timetable: The train leaves at 6 p.m.

6. Present Continuous Tense

पहचान

रहा है / रही है / रहे हैं

Structure

Subject + is/am/are + V-ing + Object I → am He / She / It → is You / We / They → are
Examples

I am reading a book.
मैं एक किताब पढ़ रहा हूँ।

She is cooking food.
वह खाना बना रही है।

They are playing cricket.
वे क्रिकेट खेल रहे हैं।

We are preparing for SSC.
हम SSC की तैयारी कर रहे हैं।

The child is sleeping.
बच्चा सो रहा है।

Negative

Subject + is/am/are + not + V-ing

He is not playing cricket.
वह क्रिकेट नहीं खेल रहा है।

They are not studying.
वे पढ़ाई नहीं कर रहे हैं।

Interrogative

Is/Am/Are + Subject + V-ing ...?

Are you studying?
क्या तुम पढ़ रहे हो?

Is she coming?
क्या वह आ रही है?

⚠ SSC Trap कुछ verbs सामान्यतः Continuous में नहीं आते:

know, believe, understand, love, hate, like, want, need, remember, belong, own

Example:

❌ I am knowing him.

✅ I know him.

7. Present Perfect Tense

पहचान

चुका है / चुकी है / चुके हैं / किया है / लिया है

Subject + has/have + V3 He / She / It → has I / We / You / They → have

I have finished my work.
मैंने अपना काम पूरा कर लिया है।

He has completed the task.
उसने task पूरा कर लिया है।

She has gone to Delhi.
वह दिल्ली जा चुकी है।

They have won the match.
उन्होंने match जीत लिया है।

❌ Common Error

❌ He has went there.

✅ He has gone there.

क्योंकि has/have के बाद हमेशा V3 आता है।

Present Perfect के साथ Important Words

already, just, yet, ever, never, recently, lately, so far, till now, up to now

I have already finished the work.

She has just arrived.

Have you ever visited Delhi?

He has never seen the sea.

They have not completed the work yet.

8. Present Perfect Continuous Tense

पहचान

से रहा है / से रही है / से रहे हैं

Subject + has/have been + V-ing + since/for
Since vs For

Since = Starting Point

For = Duration / Period

Since For
since 2010 for ten years
since Monday for two days
since morning for five hours
since childhood for a long time

I have been studying for two hours.
मैं दो घंटे से पढ़ रहा हूँ।

She has been living here since 2020.
वह 2020 से यहाँ रह रही है।

They have been waiting for an hour.
वे एक घंटे से इंतजार कर रहे हैं।

10. Past Simple Tense

पहचान

किया, गया, आया, देखा, खाया, लिखा आदि।

Subject + V2 + Object Negative: Subject + did not + V1 Question: Did + Subject + V1 ...?

I went to Delhi yesterday.
मैं कल दिल्ली गया।

She bought a new dress.
उसने नई dress खरीदी।

They played football.
उन्होंने football खेला।

He wrote a letter.
उसने एक पत्र लिखा।

❌ SSC Favourite Rule Did के बाद V2 नहीं, बल्कि V1 आएगा।

❌ Did you went there?

✅ Did you go there?

❌ He did not came.

✅ He did not come.

11. Past Continuous Tense

पहचान

रहा था / रही थी / रहे थे

Subject + was/were + V-ing I / He / She / It → was You / We / They → were

I was reading a book.
मैं किताब पढ़ रहा था।

She was cooking food.
वह खाना बना रही थी।

They were playing cricket.
वे क्रिकेट खेल रहे थे।

We were watching television.
हम television देख रहे थे।

When + Past Continuous + Past Simple

जब एक action चल रही थी और उसी समय दूसरा छोटा action हुआ, तो अक्सर:

While + Past Continuous

तथा

When + Past Simple

का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

I was sleeping when he called me.

जब उसने मुझे call किया तब मैं सो रहा था।

While I was studying, my brother was watching TV.

12. Past Perfect Tense

पहचान

चुका था / कर लिया था / गया था

Subject + had + V3
⭐ Golden Rule जब past में दो actions हों और एक action दूसरे से पहले complete हो गया हो, तो पहले completed action के लिए Past Perfect का प्रयोग किया जा सकता है।

The train had left before we reached the station.

हमारे station पहुँचने से पहले train जा चुकी थी।

He had finished his work before I arrived.

मेरे आने से पहले वह अपना काम पूरा कर चुका था।

She had already eaten dinner.

वह पहले ही dinner कर चुकी थी।

13. Past Perfect Continuous Tense

पहचान

से रहा था / से रही थी / से रहे थे

Subject + had been + V-ing + since/for

He had been studying for three hours.

वह तीन घंटे से पढ़ रहा था।

She had been working there since 2019.

वह 2019 से वहाँ काम कर रही थी।

They had been waiting for two hours.

वे दो घंटे से इंतजार कर रहे थे।

15. Future Simple Tense

पहचान

गा / गी / गे

Subject + will + V1

I will help you.
मैं तुम्हारी मदद करूँगा।

She will come tomorrow.
वह कल आएगी।

They will play the match.
वे match खेलेंगे।

We will complete the work.
हम काम पूरा करेंगे।

16. Future Continuous Tense

पहचान

रहा होगा / रही होगी / रहे होंगे

Subject + will be + V-ing

I will be studying at 8 p.m.

मैं रात 8 बजे पढ़ रहा होऊँगा।

She will be travelling tomorrow.

वह कल यात्रा कर रही होगी।

They will be playing cricket.

वे क्रिकेट खेल रहे होंगे।

17. Future Perfect Tense

पहचान

चुका होगा / चुकी होगी / कर लिया होगा

Subject + will have + V3

I will have completed the work by tomorrow.

मैं कल तक काम पूरा कर चुका होऊँगा।

She will have reached Delhi by 8 p.m.

वह रात 8 बजे तक Delhi पहुँच चुकी होगी।

They will have finished the project.

वे project पूरा कर चुके होंगे।

18. Future Perfect Continuous Tense

पहचान

से रहा होगा / से रही होगी / से रहे होंगे

Subject + will have been + V-ing + since/for

By next month, I will have been working here for five years.

अगले महीने तक मुझे यहाँ काम करते हुए पाँच साल हो चुके होंगे।

He will have been studying for three hours by 9 p.m.

रात 9 बजे तक वह तीन घंटे से पढ़ रहा होगा।

20. SSC के लिए Important Tense Rules

Rule 1 – Since और For

Since = Point of Time

For = Period of Time

He has been living here since 2020.

He has been living here for six years.

Rule 2 – Ago

Ago के साथ सामान्यतः Past Simple का प्रयोग होता है।

He came here two years ago.

वह दो साल पहले यहाँ आया था।

❌ He has come here two years ago.

Rule 3 – Yesterday / Last / Ago

yesterday, last week, last year, ago आदि past time expressions के साथ सामान्यतः Past Simple आता है।

I met him yesterday.

She visited Delhi last year.

He left five minutes ago.

Rule 4 – Universal Truth

यदि sentence में universal truth हो तो reporting verb past में होने के बावजूद statement सामान्यतः Present Simple में रहती है।

The teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun.

❌ The teacher said that the earth revolved around the sun.

Rule 5 – If Clause

First Conditional:

If + Present Simple, will + V1

If you work hard, you will succeed.

अगर तुम मेहनत करोगे तो सफल हो जाओगे।

Rule 6 – Time Clauses

जब future meaning हो और clause में when, until, unless, before, after, as soon as जैसे words आएँ, subordinate clause में सामान्यतः will नहीं लगाया जाता।

❌ I will call you when I will reach home.

✅ I will call you when I reach home.

❌ Wait until he will come.

✅ Wait until he comes.

Rule 7 – Double Past Error

यदि sentence में did already है, तो main verb V1 में रहेगा।

❌ Did he went there?

✅ Did he go there?

Rule 8 – Has/Have/Had के बाद V3

❌ He has ate the food.

✅ He has eaten the food.

❌ They had went home.

✅ They had gone home.

Rule 9 – Modal Verbs

can, could, may, might, shall, should, will, would, must के बाद सामान्यतः V1 आता है।

He can swim.

She may come.

You should work hard.

They must obey the rules.

21. Common Errors – SSC में बार-बार होने वाली गलतियाँ

Wrong Correct Reason
He don't know me. He doesn't know me. He के साथ does
She does not goes. She does not go. does के बाद V1
Did he came? Did he come? did के बाद V1
He has went. He has gone. has के बाद V3
I am knowing him. I know him. stative verb
He will comes. He will come. will के बाद V1
I will call when he will come. I will call when he comes. time clause rule
He is living here since 2020. He has been living here since 2020. continuing action

22. SSC Tense Tricks

🔥 Trick 1 Sentence में yesterday / ago / last दिखे → Past Simple check करो।
🔥 Trick 2 Since / For दिखे → Perfect Continuous को पहले check करो।
🔥 Trick 3 Did दिखे → उसके बाद V1।
🔥 Trick 4 Has / Have / Had दिखे → उसके बाद V3।
🔥 Trick 5 Will दिखे → सामान्यतः उसके बाद V1।
🔥 Trick 6 When / Until / Unless / Before / After के बाद future meaning में सीधे will लगाने से पहले rule check करो।

23. SSC PYQ-Based Practice

नीचे दिए गए questions SSC के commonly tested tense concepts और exam-pattern पर आधारित practice questions हैं।

Question 1 SSC Pattern

He _____ to Delhi yesterday.

A. goes
B. has gone
C. went
D. will go
Answer: C. went

Explanation: “yesterday” past time को show करता है, इसलिए Past Simple होगा।

Question 2 SSC Pattern

She has _____ her work.

A. complete
B. completed
C. completing
D. completes
Answer: B. completed

has के बाद V3 आता है।

Question 3 SSC Pattern

Did you _____ the movie yesterday?

A. saw
B. seen
C. see
D. seeing
Answer: C. see

Did के बाद V1 आता है।

Question 4 SSC Pattern

I _____ here since morning.

A. am waiting
B. have been waiting
C. waited
D. was waiting
Answer: B. have been waiting

“since morning” + action continuing till present → Present Perfect Continuous.

Question 5 SSC Pattern

If you work hard, you _____ succeed.

A. would
B. will
C. have
D. had
Answer: B. will

First Conditional: If + Present Simple, will + V1.

Question 6 SSC Pattern

When I reached the station, the train _____.

A. leaves
B. has left
C. had left
D. will leave
Answer: C. had left

Train का जाना station पहुँचने से पहले complete हो चुका था, इसलिए Past Perfect।

Question 7 SSC Pattern

The sun _____ in the east.

A. rose
B. rises
C. is rising
D. has risen
Answer: B. rises

यह universal truth है, इसलिए Present Simple।

Question 8 SSC Pattern

He _____ for two hours before his friend arrived.

A. has been waiting
B. had been waiting
C. is waiting
D. will wait
Answer: B. had been waiting

Past में एक action दूसरे past action से पहले duration के साथ चल रहा था → Past Perfect Continuous।

Error Detection – Tenses

Find the error:

He did not went to school yesterday.

Error: went

Correct: He did not go to school yesterday.

Reason: did not के बाद V1 आता है।

Find the error:

She has completed the work yesterday.

Error: has completed

Correct: She completed the work yesterday.

“yesterday” के कारण Past Simple।

  • Does + V1

  • Ago → Past Simple
