1. Articles क्या होते हैं?

Articles वे words हैं जो noun से पहले लगाए जाते हैं और noun के बारे में information देते हैं।

English में मुख्यतः तीन articles होते हैं: A, An और The.

⭐ सबसे पहले यह याद रखें

A और An = Indefinite Articles

The = Definite Article

I saw a boy.
मैंने एक लड़का देखा।

She ate an apple.
उसने एक सेब खाया।

The boy was intelligent.
वह लड़का बुद्धिमान था।

2. Types of Articles

Article Type Meaning / Use Example
A Indefinite कोई एक a boy
An Indefinite कोई एक an apple
The Definite निश्चित / particular the boy

3. Article “A” का प्रयोग

A का प्रयोग सामान्यतः singular countable noun से पहले किया जाता है जब अगला sound consonant sound हो।

a boy

a girl

a book

a pen

a car

a table

A + Profession

He is a doctor.

She is a teacher.

My father is a farmer.

He wants to become a pilot.

A + Singular Countable Noun

I have a car.

She bought a dress.

He found a coin.

4. Article “An” का प्रयोग

An का प्रयोग उस singular countable noun से पहले किया जाता है जिसका पहला sound vowel sound हो।

an apple

an egg

an orange

an umbrella

an elephant

Vowel Sound वाले Examples

an honest man

an hour

an heir

an honour

5. Article का फैसला Letter से नहीं, Sound से होता है

⭐ सबसे Important SSC Rule

A/An का selection spelling देखकर नहीं, pronunciation / sound देखकर होता है।

“U” लेकिन “A”

यदि U का pronunciation “यु” (/juː/) है तो consonant sound माना जाएगा और A आएगा।

a university

a European

a union

a useful book

a unique idea

Silent H → An

an honest man

an hour

an honourable person

an heir

Pronounced H → A

a house

a horse

a hotel

a historical event

Abbreviations में Sound देखें

an MLA → “एम” vowel sound से शुरू होता है।

an FIR → “एफ” vowel sound से शुरू होता है।

an MBA → “एम” sound।

a B.A. → “बी” consonant sound।

a university → “यू” sound।

6. Article “The” का प्रयोग

The का प्रयोग किसी specific या definite person, place, thing या group के लिए किया जाता है।

I saw a dog.

The dog was black.

पहले sentence में कोई भी dog था।

दूसरे sentence में वही particular dog है।

पहले mention की गई चीज

I bought a book.

The book is very interesting.

7. Unique / One-of-a-kind Things

जिन चीजों को सामान्यतः unique या one-of-a-kind माना जाता है, उनके साथ The का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

the Sun

the Moon

the Earth

the sky

the universe

the world

The Sun rises in the east.

The Earth moves around the Sun.

The Moon shines at night.

8. Rivers, Seas, Oceans और Canals

Rivers, seas, oceans और canals के names से पहले सामान्यतः The आता है।

the Ganga

the Yamuna

the Brahmaputra

the Indian Ocean

the Arabian Sea

the Bay of Bengal

the Suez Canal

The Ganga is a sacred river.

The Indian Ocean is warm.

9. Mountains और Mountain Ranges

Individual Mountain → Usually No Article

Mount Everest

Mount Abu

Mount Kilimanjaro

Mountain Range → The

the Himalayas

the Alps

the Andes

the Rockies

Mount Everest is the highest peak in the world.

The Himalayas are in Asia.

10. Countries के साथ Articles

सामान्यतः single-word country names से पहले article नहीं आता।

India

Japan

France

China

Nepal

Plural / Union / Kingdom / Republic type names

the United States

the United Kingdom

the United Arab Emirates

the Netherlands

the Philippines

Exam Alert

Country का exact official/common name देखकर rule लगाएँ। हर country के नाम के साथ “the” नहीं लगाया जाता।

11. Newspapers और Famous Publications

Newspapers के names के साथ सामान्यतः The का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

The Times of India

The Hindu

The Indian Express

The Guardian

12. Superlative Degree के साथ “The”

Superlative degree से पहले सामान्यतः The का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

He is the best player.

She is the tallest girl in the class.

This is the most beautiful place.

He is the fastest runner.

❌ He is best player in the team.

✅ He is the best player in the team.

13. Ordinal Numbers के साथ “The”

First, second, third, fourth आदि ordinal numbers के साथ सामान्यतः The आता है।

the first chapter

the second question

the third floor

the fifth student

He came first.

लेकिन:

He was the first person to arrive.

14. Musical Instruments के साथ “The”

Musical instruments को general sense में बजाने की बात हो, तो सामान्यतः The का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

He plays the guitar.

She plays the piano.

He can play the flute.

She plays the violin.

15. पूरी Class / Species को Represent करना

किसी पूरे class/species को singular noun के माध्यम से represent करने पर The का प्रयोग किया जा सकता है।

The tiger is a dangerous animal.

The lion is known as the king of beasts.

The cow is a useful animal.

यहाँ किसी एक specific tiger की बात नहीं बल्कि पूरी species की बात की जा रही है।

16. Zero Article – जहाँ Article नहीं लगता

कई situations में noun से पहले कोई article नहीं लगाया जाता। इसे Zero Article कहते हैं।

Languages

I speak English.

He knows Hindi.

She is learning French.

Subjects

Mathematics is difficult.

Physics is interesting.

English is easy for him.

Meals

We had breakfast.

She is preparing dinner.

They had lunch together.

Games / Sports

He plays cricket.

They play football.

She likes tennis.

17. School / Hospital / Prison / Bed / Church

इन nouns के साथ article का use इस बात पर depend करता है कि व्यक्ति वहाँ मुख्य purpose से गया है या building/place के रूप में।

He goes to school.

यहाँ school जाने का purpose पढ़ाई है।

He went to the school to meet the principal.

यहाँ school एक specific building/place है।

Hospital

He was admitted to hospital.

She went to the hospital to visit her friend.

Prison

He was sent to prison.

His mother went to the prison to meet him.

Bed

He is in bed.

The cat is under the bed.

18. Abstract Nouns के साथ Article

Abstract nouns जैसे honesty, love, beauty, wisdom, kindness आदि जब general sense में हों तो सामान्यतः article नहीं लेते।

Honesty is the best policy.

Beauty is temporary.

Wisdom comes with experience.

लेकिन जब abstract noun specific हो जाए, तब The आ सकता है।

The honesty of the boy impressed me.

The beauty of Kashmir is famous.

19. Proper Nouns के साथ Articles

सामान्यतः व्यक्ति के नाम से पहले article नहीं आता।

Rahul is my friend.

Arpit lives in India.

Delhi is a large city.

लेकिन कुछ Proper Names के साथ “The”

the Himalayas

the Ganga

the United States

the United Kingdom

the Netherlands

20. Special Structures – SSC Favourite

Such + a/an + Noun

He is such a good boy.

It was such an interesting story.

Quite + a/an + Adjective + Noun

He is quite a good player.

It was quite an interesting book.

Rather + a/an

It is rather a difficult question.

It is rather an unusual situation.

Too + Adjective + a/an + Noun

It is too difficult a question to solve.

He is too honest a man to cheat.

As + Adjective + a/an + Noun

He is as good a player as his brother.

It was as difficult a question as I expected.

21. Common Errors – SSC

Incorrect Correct Reason
He is an university student. He is a university student. “University” starts with /yuː/ sound.
He is a honest man. He is an honest man. H is silent.
She is a MLA. She is an MLA. “M” has vowel sound.
He is an European. He is a European. “European” starts with /yuː/.
He is the doctor. He is a doctor. Profession in general → A.
Sun rises in east. The Sun rises in the east. Unique object / direction.
He plays guitar. He plays the guitar. Musical instrument.
Ganga is a holy river. The Ganga is a holy river. River names take “the”.
He is best player. He is the best player. Superlative → the.
I speak the English. I speak English. Language in general → zero article.

23. SSC PYQ-Based / Exam Pattern Practice

Question 1 SSC Pattern

He is _____ honest man.

A. a
B. an
C. the
D. no article
Answer: B. an

Honest में H silent है, इसलिए vowel sound आता है।

Question 2 SSC Pattern

He is _____ university student.

A. an
B. a
C. the
D. no article
Answer: B. a

University का sound “yu” है।

Question 3 SSC Pattern

_____ Ganga is one of the most important rivers of India.

A. A
B. An
C. The
D. No article
Answer: C. The

River names के साथ The।

Question 4 SSC Pattern

He is _____ best player in the team.

A. a
B. an
C. the
D. no article
Answer: C. the

Superlative degree से पहले The।

Question 5 SSC Pattern

She plays _____ piano very well.

A. a
B. an
C. the
D. no article
Answer: C. the

Musical instrument के साथ The।

Question 6 SSC Pattern

I speak _____ English fluently.

A. a
B. an
C. the
D. no article
Answer: D. no article

Language के general use में article नहीं आता।

Question 7 SSC Pattern

He wants to become _____ engineer.

A. a
B. an
C. the
D. no article
Answer: B. an

Engineer vowel sound से शुरू होता है।

Question 8 SSC Pattern

Mount Everest is _____ highest peak in the world.

A. a
B. an
C. the
D. no article
Answer: C. the

Highest superlative degree है।

Error Detection Practice

Find the error:

He is an university student.

Error: an

Correct: He is a university student.

Find the error:

He is a honest officer.

Error: a

Correct: He is an honest officer.

Find the error:

Ganga is a holy river.

Error: Ganga

Correct: The Ganga is a holy river.
