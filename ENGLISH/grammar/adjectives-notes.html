1. Adjective क्या होता है?

Adjective वह word है जो किसी noun या pronoun की quality, quantity, number, size, colour, condition आदि के बारे में information देता है।

हिंदी में इसे विशेषण कहते हैं।

📌 Definition An adjective is a word that qualifies or describes a noun or pronoun.

यानी जो शब्द noun या pronoun की विशेषता बताए, वह adjective कहलाता है।

Examples

  • He is a good boy.
  • She has a beautiful dress.
  • I have two books.
  • There is little water in the glass.
  • Delhi is a large city.
Example Analysis:

Rahul is an intelligent student.

यहाँ intelligent Rahul/student की quality बता रहा है। इसलिए यह adjective है।

2. Types of Adjectives

SSC English में adjectives को अलग-अलग आधार पर classify किया जाता है।

Type Hindi Examples
Adjective of Quality गुणवाचक विशेषण good, bad, beautiful, honest
Adjective of Quantity परिमाणवाचक विशेषण much, little, enough, sufficient
Adjective of Number संख्यावाचक विशेषण one, two, first, second
Demonstrative Adjective संकेतवाचक विशेषण this, that, these, those
Possessive Adjective अधिकारवाचक विशेषण my, your, his, her, our
Interrogative Adjective प्रश्नवाचक विशेषण which, what, whose

3. Adjective of Quality

जो adjective किसी noun की quality या nature बताता है, उसे Adjective of Quality कहते हैं।

Examples

  • He is an honest man.
  • She is a beautiful girl.
  • This is a red car.
  • He is a brave soldier.
  • India is a great country.
  • This is an interesting story.
Remember: Good, bad, tall, short, honest, brave, beautiful, intelligent, clever, foolish, red, black आदि quality या characteristic बता सकते हैं।

4. Adjective of Quantity

जो adjective किसी uncountable noun की मात्रा बताता है, उसे Adjective of Quantity कहते हैं।

Important Words

Word Use
much Uncountable noun
little Small quantity
a little Some quantity
the little Whatever small quantity exists
enough Sufficient quantity
sufficient Enough quantity
Examples:

There is little water in the tank.
I have a little money.
We have enough food.
He does not have much patience.

5. Adjective of Number

जो adjective count या order बताता है, उसे Adjective of Number कहते हैं।

Examples

  • I have three books.
  • He stood first in the race.
  • Many students were absent.
  • Several candidates appeared in the examination.
  • Few students passed the test.
⭐ SSC Point Cardinal numbers: one, two, three, four...

Ordinal numbers: first, second, third, fourth...

6. Demonstrative Adjectives

This, that, these और those जब noun के पहले आकर noun को modify करते हैं, तब ये demonstrative adjectives होते हैं।

Word Number Distance Example
this Singular Near This book is useful.
that Singular Far That book is old.
these Plural Near These books are useful.
those Plural Far Those books are old.
❌ Common Error This books are expensive.

✅ Correct: These books are expensive.

7. Possessive Adjectives

My, your, his, her, its, our और their possession/ownership दिखाते हैं और noun से पहले आते हैं।

Person Possessive Adjective Example
I my my book
We our our house
You your your pen
He his his car
She her her bag
They their their school
Remember: My, your, his, her, our, their के बाद generally noun आता है।

This is my book.
This book is mine.

8. Interrogative Adjectives

What, which और whose जब noun के साथ प्रश्न पूछने के लिए प्रयोग होते हैं, तो ये interrogative adjectives कहलाते हैं।

Examples

  • Which book do you want?
  • What subject do you like?
  • Whose pen is this?
Important Difference:

Which book do you want?
यहाँ "which" के बाद noun book है, इसलिए adjective।

Which do you want?
यहाँ noun नहीं है, इसलिए pronoun।

9. Degrees of Comparison

Adjectives की comparison करने के लिए तीन degrees होती हैं।

Degree Purpose Example
Positive Simple quality Ram is tall.
Comparative Two persons/things Ram is taller than Mohan.
Superlative More than two / highest degree Ram is the tallest boy.

10. Positive Degree

Positive degree adjective की simple form होती है। इसमें comparison नहीं किया जाता।

Examples:

Rahul is tall.
This is a beautiful flower.
He is intelligent.

Equality

दो लोगों या चीजों को समान बताने के लिए:

as + adjective + as
Ram is as tall as Shyam.

This book is as useful as that book.

Negative Equality

not as / so + adjective + as
Ram is not as tall as Shyam.

11. Comparative Degree

दो persons या things के बीच comparison के लिए comparative degree का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Subject + Comparative Adjective + than + Object
Ram is taller than Shyam.
Delhi is larger than Agra.
Gold is more expensive than silver.

Common Forms

Positive Comparative
tall taller
small smaller
beautiful more beautiful
intelligent more intelligent
important more important

12. Superlative Degree

जब तीन या तीन से अधिक persons/things में highest degree बतानी हो, तो superlative degree का प्रयोग होता है।

Subject + the + Superlative Adjective + ...
Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world.

Rahul is the tallest boy in the class.
⭐ Important Superlative degree के साथ generally the का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

13. Special Rules for SSC

Rule 1 — Double Comparative नहीं

❌ He is more taller than me.

✅ He is taller than me.

Rule 2 — Double Superlative नहीं

❌ He is the most tallest boy.

✅ He is the tallest boy.

Rule 3 — Senior / Junior

Senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior आदि के साथ to का प्रयोग किया जाता है, "than" का नहीं।

He is senior to me.

She is junior to him.

This product is superior to that one.
❌ He is senior than me.
✅ He is senior to me.

Rule 4 — Preferable

Preferable के साथ भी to का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Tea is preferable to coffee.

Rule 5 — Inferior / Superior

Quality of this product is inferior to that one.

This method is superior to the old method.

Rule 6 — Elder / Older

Word Use
elder Generally family relationships
older General comparison
My elder brother is a doctor.

Delhi is older than many modern cities.

Rule 7 — Farther / Further

We walked farther than before.

For further information, contact the office.

Rule 8 — Less / Fewer

Fewer → Countable nouns
Less → Uncountable nouns
There are fewer students in the class.

There is less water in the tank.

Rule 9 — Few / A few / The few

Expression Meaning
few Almost none / बहुत कम
a few Some / कुछ
the few Whatever small number exists
Few students passed the exam.
→ लगभग कोई नहीं।

A few students passed the exam.
→ कुछ students pass हुए।

The few students who passed were rewarded.
→ जितने थोड़े students pass हुए, उन्हें reward किया गया।

Rule 10 — Little / A little / The little

Expression Meaning
little Almost no quantity
a little Some quantity
the little Whatever small quantity exists
There is little hope of success.

There is a little hope of success.

He spent the little money he had.

14. Common Errors

❌ Error 1 He is more taller than his brother.

✅ Correct: He is taller than his brother.
❌ Error 2 She is senior than me.

✅ Correct: She is senior to me.
❌ Error 3 This is the most tallest building.

✅ Correct: This is the tallest building.
❌ Error 4 He has less books than me.

✅ Correct: He has fewer books than me.
❌ Error 5 There are much students in the class.

✅ Correct: There are many students in the class.
❌ Error 6 He is one of the tallest boy in the class.

✅ Correct: He is one of the tallest boys in the class.
❌ Error 7 Of the two boys, Rahul is the tallest.

Traditional SSC grammar:

✅ Of the two boys, Rahul is the taller.

15. SSC PYQs & Exam-Level Questions

PYQ 01 SSC Pattern

He is senior ______ me.

A. than
B. to
C. from
D. with
Answer: B. to
Explanation: Senior के साथ "to" का प्रयोग होता है।
PYQ 02 SSC Pattern

This book is ______ than that one.

A. more useful
B. most useful
C. usefulest
D. more usefull
Answer: A. more useful
Explanation: Two books की comparison है, इसलिए comparative degree।
PYQ 03 SSC Pattern

Mount Everest is ______ mountain in the world.

A. high
B. higher
C. the highest
D. most high
Answer: C. the highest
Explanation: पूरे world में highest degree है, इसलिए superlative।
PYQ 04 SSC Pattern

There are ______ students in this class than in that class.

A. less
B. lesser
C. fewer
D. little
Answer: C. fewer
Explanation: Students countable plural noun है, इसलिए fewer।
PYQ 05 SSC Pattern

He is one of the ______ players in the team.

A. good
B. better
C. best
D. goodest
Answer: C. best
Explanation: Structure:
one of + the + superlative + plural noun
PYQ 06 SSC Pattern

Of the two brothers, Ram is the ______.

A. tallest
B. taller
C. most tall
D. more tallest
Answer: B. taller
Explanation: केवल दो persons की comparison है, इसलिए comparative degree।
PYQ 07 SSC Pattern

There is ______ water in the bottle.

A. few
B. a few
C. little
D. a little
Answer: C/D depending on intended meaning
Explanation: "little" = almost no water.
"a little" = some water.

Exam में context के अनुसार सही option चुनना होता है।
PYQ 08 SSC Pattern

Tea is preferable ______ coffee.

A. than
B. from
C. to
D. over
Answer: C. to
Explanation: Preferable के साथ "to" का प्रयोग किया जाता है।
PYQ 09 SSC Pattern

He is ______ than his brother.

A. intelligent
B. more intelligent
C. most intelligent
D. intelligently
Answer: B. more intelligent
Explanation: Two persons की comparison है और intelligent long adjective है, इसलिए "more intelligent"।
PYQ 10 SSC Pattern

He is the ______ boy in the class.

A. taller
B. tallest
C. more tall
D. most taller
Answer: B. tallest
Explanation: पूरी class में highest degree है, इसलिए superlative "tallest"।

16. Quick Revision

Possessive my, your, his, her, our, their
Comparative taller, better, more beautiful
