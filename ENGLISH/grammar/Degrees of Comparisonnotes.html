1. Degrees of Comparison क्या होते हैं?

Degrees of Comparison adjectives (विशेषण) का वह रूप है जो दो या दो से अधिक nouns/pronouns के बीच comparison (तुलना) दिखाता है।

Adjectives के तीन degrees होते हैं:
1. Positive Degree (मूल अवस्था)
2. Comparative Degree (उत्तर अवस्था)
3. Superlative Degree (उत्तम अवस्था)

⭐ सबसे पहले यह याद रखें

Positive – कोई comparison नहीं (simple quality)
Comparative – दो के बीच comparison (than)
Superlative – तीन या अधिक में सबसे ऊपर (the)

Positive: Ram is tall.
Comparative: Ram is taller than Shyam.
Superlative: Ram is the tallest in the class.

Positive: This book is interesting.
Comparative: This book is more interesting than that one.
Superlative: This is the most interesting book I have read.

2. The Three Degrees – Detailed Explanation

Degree Definition Structure Example
Positive किसी व्यक्ति/वस्तु के गुण को बिना comparison के बताना Subject + verb + adjective She is beautiful.
Comparative दो व्यक्तियों/वस्तुओं के बीच comparison Subject + verb + adj-er/more + than + object She is more beautiful than her sister.
Superlative तीन या अधिक में सबसे ऊपर / top position Subject + verb + the + adj-est/most + (in/of) She is the most beautiful in the class.

3. Formation Rules – Overview

Comparative और Superlative बनाने के तीन main ways हैं:

Type Comparative Superlative Examples
Short adjectives (1-2 syllables) + -er + -est tall → taller → tallest
Long adjectives (3+ syllables) more + adjective most + adjective beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful
Irregular completely different words completely different words good → better → best

4. One-Syllable Adjectives – -er / -est

One-syllable adjectives में comparative के लिए -er और superlative के लिए -est जोड़ते हैं।

Positive Comparative Superlative
tall taller tallest
short shorter shortest
fast faster fastest
strong stronger strongest
small smaller smallest
big bigger biggest
hot hotter hottest
thin thinner thinnest
⭐ Spelling Rules
  • Consonant + y → ier / iest
    happy → happier → happiest
  • CVC (consonant-vowel-consonant) → double last consonant
    big → bigger → biggest
    hot → hotter → hottest
  • Silent e → -r / -st
    nice → nicer → nicest
    large → larger → largest

5. Two-Syllable Adjectives

Two-syllable adjectives में दोनों ways हो सकते हैं:
1. -er / -est (some adjectives)
2. more / most (other adjectives)

Positive Comparative Superlative Rule
happy happier happiest -y → -ier / -iest
funny funnier funniest -y → -ier / -iest
clever cleverer / more clever cleverest / most clever Both ways
quiet quieter / more quiet quietest / most quiet Both ways
simple simpler / more simple simplest / most simple Both ways
polite politer / more polite politest / most polite Both ways
famous more famous most famous more / most (common)
boring more boring most boring more / most

6. Multi-Syllable Adjectives (3+ syllables)

Three or more syllables वाले adjectives में comparative के लिए more और superlative के लिए most का प्रयोग करते हैं।

Positive Comparative Superlative
beautiful more beautiful most beautiful
intelligent more intelligent most intelligent
interesting more interesting most interesting
expensive more expensive most expensive
comfortable more comfortable most comfortable
dangerous more dangerous most dangerous

7. Irregular Comparisons – SSC Favourite

कुछ adjectives के comparative और superlative forms completely different होते हैं (irregular)। ये SSC में बहुत important हैं।

Positive Comparative Superlative
good better best
bad worse worst
little (quantity) less least
much / many more most
far (distance) farther farthest
far (figurative) further furthest
old older / elder oldest / eldest
late later / latter latest / last
many more most
Exam Alert – Important Differences

Elder / Eldest – family members के लिए (before noun)
Older / Oldest – people/things के लिए (general)

Farther / Farthest – physical distance
Further / Furthest – figurative / additional

Later / Latest – time
Latter / Last – order / sequence

8. Double Comparatives (The + comparative, the + comparative)

Double comparatives का प्रयोग यह दिखाने के लिए होता है कि एक चीज़ दूसरी चीज़ पर directly proportional (समानुपाती) है।

⭐ Structure

The + comparative + subject + verb, the + comparative + subject + verb

The harder you work, the better you will succeed.

The more you read, the more you learn.

The older he gets, the wiser he becomes.

The sooner we start, the earlier we will finish.

9. Than vs To – Important SSC Rule

सामान्यतः comparative adjectives के बाद than आता है। लेकिन कुछ adjectives के साथ to का प्रयोग होता है।

Adjective Preposition Example
Superior to This product is superior to that one.
Inferior to His work is inferior to hers.
Senior to He is senior to me in the office.
Junior to She is junior to him.
Prefer to I prefer tea to coffee.
Prior to This is prior to that event.
Common Error

❌ He is superior than me.
✅ He is superior to me.

❌ She is senior than him.
✅ She is senior to him.

10. Absolute Adjectives – No Comparison

कुछ adjectives ऐसे हैं जिनका comparison नहीं हो सकता क्योंकि वे absolute (पूर्ण) स्थिति को दिखाते हैं।

⭐ SSC Favourite Rule

Absolute adjectives – इनके comparative/superlative forms नहीं होते हैं (logically incorrect)।

Examples of Absolute Adjectives:
perfect, unique, complete, universal, infinite, absolute, ideal, final, dead, alive, empty, full

❌ This is more perfect than that.
✅ This is nearer to perfection than that.

❌ This is the most unique design.
✅ This is a unique design.

❌ She is more dead than him.
✅ She is dead.

11. All the + Comparative – Emphasis

All the + comparative का प्रयोग emphasis (ज़ोर) देने के लिए होता है।

I am all the happier to see you.

He works all the harder for his family.

Your words made me all the more determined.

12. Common Errors – SSC

Incorrect Correct Reason
He is more taller than me. He is taller than me. Double comparative (more + -er)
She is most beautiful than her sister. She is more beautiful than her sister. Comparative में most नहीं, more
This is the bestest book. This is the best book. 'Best' already superlative – no -est
He is superior than me. He is superior to me. Superior के साथ to (not than)
She is more perfect than him. She is perfect. 'Perfect' is absolute – no comparison
Ram is more good than Shyam. Ram is better than Shyam. Good → better (irregular)
This is the most unique idea. This is a unique idea. 'Unique' is absolute
He is more honester than his brother. He is more honest than his brother. Double comparative

13. SSC Degrees of Comparison Tricks

🔥 Trick 1 – One syllable = -er / -est

short → shorter → shortest
tall → taller → tallest

🔥 Trick 2 – Three+ syllables = more / most

beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful
intelligent → more intelligent → most intelligent

🔥 Trick 3 – Irregular forms याद करें

good → better → best
bad → worse → worst
little → less → least

🔥 Trick 4 – Superior / Inferior = to

Superior to, inferior to
Senior to, junior to

🔥 Trick 5 – Double comparative न करें

❌ more better
✅ better

❌ most tallest
✅ tallest

🔥 Trick 6 – Absolute adjectives – no comparison

perfect, unique, complete, universal, infinite, ideal, final, dead, alive, empty, full

14. SSC PYQ-Based / Exam Pattern Practice

Question 1 SSC CGL 2022

Choose the correct degree of comparison:

This is the _____ book I have ever read.

A. more interesting
B. most interesting
C. interesting
D. more interesting than
Answer: B. most interesting

Superlative degree – 'the' + most + adjective (3+ syllables)।

Question 2 SSC CHSL 2021

Choose the correct option:

He is _____ than his brother.

A. more taller
B. taller
C. tallest
D. more tall
Answer: B. taller

Comparative degree – two things (he vs his brother) → taller than।

Question 3 SSC CPO 2022

Choose the correct option:

Her performance was _____ to his.

A. superior
B. superior than
C. superior to
D. more superior
Answer: C. superior to

'Superior' के साथ 'to' आता है (not 'than')।

Question 4 SSC MTS 2022

Choose the correct option:

She is the _____ girl in the class.

A. more beautiful
B. most beautiful
C. beautiful
D. more beautiful than
Answer: B. most beautiful

Superlative – 'the' + most beautiful (in the class)।

Question 5 SSC CGL 2023

Choose the correct option:

His condition is _____ than before.

A. worse
B. worst
C. more bad
D. badder
Answer: A. worse

'Bad' का comparative 'worse' है (irregular)।

Question 6 SSC CHSL 2023

Choose the correct option:

This is the _____ painting I have ever seen.

A. more beautiful
B. most beautiful
C. beautiful
D. beautifulest
Answer: B. most beautiful

Superlative – 'the' + most + beautiful (ever seen)।

Question 7 SSC CPO 2023

Choose the correct option:

_____ you study, _____ you will score.

A. The more / the better
B. More / better
C. The more / better
D. More / the better
Answer: A. The more / the better

Double comparative structure: The + comparative, the + comparative।

Question 8 SSC MTS 2023

Choose the correct option:

This is the _____ book I have ever read.

A. more good
B. best
C. better
D. good
Answer: B. best

'Good' का superlative 'best' है (irregular)।

15. Quick Revision – Degrees of Comparison

Rule 1 Positive = no comparison
Rule 2 Comparative = two (than)
Rule 3 Superlative = three+ (the)
Rule 4 One syllable → -er / -est
Rule 5 3+ syllables → more / most
Rule 6 Irregular: good→better→best
Rule 7 Superior/inferior → to
Rule 8 Double comparative ❌
Rule 9 Absolute adjectives – no comparison
Rule 10 The + comp, the + comp
🎯 SSC Degrees of Comparison Final Checklist
  • Positive: No comparison – simple quality (tall, beautiful)।
  • Comparative: Two things – adjective + -er / more + than।
  • Superlative: Three+ things – the + adjective + -est / most + in/of।
  • One syllable: -er / -est (tall → taller → tallest)।
  • Two syllables: -er/-est or more/most (depending on word)।
  • Three+ syllables: more / most (beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful)।
  • Irregular forms: good→better→best, bad→worse→worst, little→less→least।
  • Superior / Inferior / Senior / Junior = to (not than)।
  • Double comparative never use (more better ❌, better ✅)।
  • Absolute adjectives: perfect, unique, complete, universal – no comparison।
  • Double comparative structure: The + comparative, the + comparative।
