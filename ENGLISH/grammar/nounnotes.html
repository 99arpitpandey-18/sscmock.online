Nouns – Types, Gender, Number & Cases

1. Nouns क्या होते हैं?

Noun (संज्ञा) वह शब्द है जो किसी person (व्यक्ति), place (स्थान), thing (वस्तु), idea (विचार) या quality (गुण) के नाम को दर्शाता है।

English grammar में Noun सबसे important part of speech है क्योंकि यह sentence का subject या object बनता है।

⭐ सबसे पहले यह याद रखें

Noun = किसी भी चीज़ का नाम।
चाहे वह चीज़ दिखती हो या न दिखती हो।

Examples of Nouns:

Person: Ram, teacher, doctor, mother

Place: Delhi, temple, school, park

Thing: book, pen, car, tree

Idea/Quality: honesty, beauty, wisdom, love

2. Types of Nouns

Type Definition Examples
Proper Noun किसी specific person, place या thing का name Rahul, Delhi, Ganga, Monday
Common Noun किसी class/group के सभी members के लिए common name boy, city, river, day
Collective Noun लोगों या चीज़ों के समूह (group) का name team, army, flock, crowd
Abstract Noun quality, state, action या idea – जो दिखाई न दे honesty, love, courage, childhood
Material Noun किसी वस्तु को बनाने वाली material/substance gold, wood, water, iron, silver

3. Proper Nouns (व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा)

Proper nouns किसी specific person, place, organization या thing का unique name होते हैं। ये हमेशा capital letter से start होते हैं।

Person: Mohan, Sita, Amitabh Bachchan

Place: Mumbai, London, Mount Everest

Organization: Google, United Nations

Day/Month: Sunday, January, Diwali

River: Ganga, Yamuna, Nile

Book: Ramayana, The Bible

Exam Alert

Proper nouns हमेशा capitalized होते हैं।
❌ i live in delhi.
✅ I live in Delhi.

4. Common Nouns (जातिवाचक संज्ञा)

Common nouns किसी class या category के सभी members के लिए common name होते हैं। ये capitalized नहीं होते (except beginning of sentence)।

Person: boy, girl, teacher, doctor, mother

Place: city, village, park, school, hospital

Thing: book, pen, table, car, tree

Animal: dog, cat, tiger, elephant

Proper vs Common – Comparison

Proper Noun Common Noun
Rahul boy
Delhi city
Ganga river
Sunday day
Google company

5. Collective Nouns (समूहवाचक संज्ञा)

Collective nouns लोगों, जानवरों या चीज़ों के समूह (group) के लिए प्रयोग होते हैं।

Collective Noun Group of
team players
army soldiers
flock birds / sheep
herd cattle / elephants
crowd people
class students
family members of a family
committee members of a committee
audience spectators / listeners
swarm bees / insects

The team is playing well.

A flock of birds flew over the mountain.

The class was silent.

The army has marched forward.

⭐ Important SSC Rule

Collective noun को singular या plural verb के साथ प्रयोग किया जा सकता है:

Singular: जब group को एक unit के रूप में देखें।
The team is ready.

Plural: जब group के individual members पर focus हो।
The team are arguing among themselves.

6. Abstract Nouns (भाववाचक संज्ञा)

Abstract nouns वे nouns हैं जो दिखाई नहीं देते। ये quality, state, action, emotion या idea को दर्शाते हैं।

Category Examples
Quality honesty, beauty, courage, kindness, wisdom
State childhood, poverty, sickness, happiness
Action movement, growth, arrival, departure
Emotion/Feeling love, anger, fear, hope, sadness
Idea truth, freedom, democracy, justice

Honesty is the best policy.

She is known for her beauty.

He showed great courage.

Childhood is the best part of life.

I have great love for my country.

Abstract Noun कैसे बनाएँ (Common Suffixes)

Suffix Adjective/Verb Abstract Noun
-ness happy, kind, dark happiness, kindness, darkness
-ity active, generous, pure activity, generosity, purity
-tion / -sion decide, inform, express decision, information, expression
-ment develop, enjoy, achieve development, enjoyment, achievement
-hood child, man, neighbor childhood, manhood, neighborhood
-ship friend, leader, relation friendship, leadership, relationship
-th strong, true, warm strength, truth, warmth

7. Material Nouns (द्रव्यवाचक संज्ञा)

Material nouns वे nouns हैं जो किसी substance या raw material को दर्शाते हैं जिससे चीज़ें बनाई जाती हैं।

Metals: gold, silver, iron, copper, steel

Liquids: water, milk, oil, wine

Materials: wood, wool, cotton, leather, glass

Food items: rice, wheat, sugar, salt, butter

This ring is made of gold.

I need water to drink.

The table is made of wood.

We should save money for the future.

⭐ Important SSC Rule

Material nouns generally have no plural form और इनके साथ a/an नहीं लगता।

❌ I need a water.
✅ I need water.

❌ We bought golds.
✅ We bought gold.

8. Countable vs Uncountable Nouns

Countable Nouns Uncountable Nouns
जिन्हें गिना जा सके जिन्हें गिना न जा सके
Singular और Plural दोनों forms होते हैं Usually singular form only
A/an, many, few, several का प्रयोग होता है Much, little, some, any का प्रयोग होता है
Examples: book, pen, apple, city, student Examples: water, air, rice, knowledge, honesty

Examples

Countable:
I have three books.
Many students are present.
She ate an apple.

Uncountable:
I need some water.
He has much knowledge.
Rice is grown in India.

9. Gender of Nouns (लिंग)

English grammar में nouns के चार genders होते हैं:

Gender Definition Examples
Masculine पुल्लिंग – male gender boy, man, father, king, lion, tiger
Feminine स्त्रीलिंग – female gender girl, woman, mother, queen, lioness, tigress
Neuter नपुंसकलिंग – no gender (lifeless things) book, table, pen, car, house
Common उभयलिंग – can be male or female teacher, doctor, parent, friend, student

Masculine से Feminine बनाने के Rules

Rule Masculine Feminine
-or → -ress actor actress
-er → -ress waiter waitress
-ess जोड़कर host, lion, tiger hostess, lioness, tigress
अलग-अलग words boy, man, father, brother girl, woman, mother, sister
-ine जोड़कर hero, usher heroine, usherette

Common Gender:
teacher, doctor, student, parent, friend, child, cousin

Neuter Gender:
book, table, chair, computer, tree, mountain

10. Number – Singular & Plural

Nouns के दो numbers होते हैं:
Singular (एक) और Plural (एक से अधिक)।

Singular से Plural बनाने के Rules

Rule Singular Plural
Most nouns → +s boy, book, pen boys, books, pens
s, sh, ch, x, z → +es bus, box, watch, brush buses, boxes, watches, brushes
consonant + y → ies baby, city, fly babies, cities, flies
vowel + y → +s boy, key, toy boys, keys, toys
f/fe → ves leaf, wife, thief leaves, wives, thieves
o (consonant + o) → +es potato, hero, mango potatoes, heroes, mangoes
o (vowel + o) → +s radio, zoo, studio radios, zoos, studios
Irregular man, woman, child, tooth men, women, children, teeth
Same form sheep, deer, fish, species sheep, deer, fish, species

Irregular Plurals – Important List

man → men
woman → women
child → children
tooth → teeth
foot → feet
mouse → mice
ox → oxen
goose → geese
louse → lice

Same in Singular & Plural:
sheep, deer, fish, species, series, aircraft

Exam Alert

'Police' और 'people' हमेशा plural होते हैं।
✅ The police are on the way.
❌ The police is on the way.

'News', 'Physics', 'Mathematics', 'Politics' → दिखने में plural (s के साथ) लेकिन singular।
✅ News is good.

11. Cases of Nouns (कारक)

Noun के cases noun और verb/preposition के बीच के relationship को दिखाते हैं।

Case Definition Example
Nominative (Subjective) Subject of the verb (कर्ता) Ram plays cricket.
Objective (Accusative) Object of the verb (कर्म) I saw Mohan.
Possessive Ownership / relation (संबंध) This is Rahul's book.
Vocative Direct address (संबोधन) Teacher, please help me.

Possessive Case – Apostrophe Rules

⭐ SSC Favourite Rule

Singular noun + 's → boy's book
Plural noun ending in s + ' → boys' school
Plural noun not ending in s + 's → children's toys

Singular:
Ram's book, girl's dress, teacher's pen

Plural (ending with s):
Boys' school, teachers' room, girls' hostel

Plural (not ending with s):
Children's park, women's rights, men's club

12. Common Errors – SSC

Incorrect Correct Reason
I need a water. I need water. Uncountable noun – no 'a'
He bought fruits. He bought fruit. जब 'fruit' का general sense हो तो uncountable
The police is here. The police are here. 'Police' always plural
The news are good. The news is good. 'News' singular
His book is red. His book is red. (Correct – possessive case)
The team are playing well. The team is playing well. Team as a unit → singular
I saw deers. I saw deer. 'Deer' singular/plural same
She is my cousin sister. She is my cousin. 'Cousin' is common gender

13. SSC Nouns Tricks

🔥 Trick 1 – Proper vs Common

Proper = unique name → capital letter
Common = general name → no capital

🔥 Trick 2 – Collective Nouns

Team, army, flock, herd → singular या plural हो सकते हैं
Sense पर depend करता है।

🔥 Trick 3 – Uncountable Nouns

Water, air, rice, honesty, knowledge → no 'a/an', no plural

🔥 Trick 4 – Plural vs Singular

Police, people → plural
News, physics, mathematics, politics → singular

🔥 Trick 5 – Possessive Apostrophe

Singular → 's (Ram's)
Plural ending with s → ' (boys')
Plural not ending with s → 's (children's)

🔥 Trick 6 – Irregular Plurals

man→men, child→children, tooth→teeth
sheep→sheep, deer→deer (same form)

14. SSC PYQ-Based / Exam Pattern Practice

Question 1 SSC CGL 2022

Identify the type of noun:

Honesty is the best policy.

A. Proper Noun
B. Common Noun
C. Abstract Noun
D. Collective Noun
Answer: C. Abstract Noun

'Honesty' एक quality है जो दिखाई नहीं देती → Abstract Noun।

Question 2 SSC CHSL 2021

Choose the correct plural form:

The _____ are grazing in the field.

A. sheep
B. sheeps
C. sheepes
D. sheep's
Answer: A. sheep

'Sheep' का singular और plural दोनों same होता है।

Question 3 SSC CPO 2022

Identify the type of noun:

The army marched forward.

A. Proper Noun
B. Common Noun
C. Abstract Noun
D. Collective Noun
Answer: D. Collective Noun

'Army' soldiers का एक समूह है → Collective Noun।

Question 4 SSC MTS 2022

Choose the correct possessive form:

This is the _____ school.

A. boys
B. boy's
C. boys'
D. boyes
Answer: C. boys'

Plural noun (boys) ending with s → possessive = boys'

Question 5 SSC CGL 2023

Choose the correct noun:

The _____ of the children were playing.

A. child
B. childs
C. children
D. childrens
Answer: C. children

'Child' का plural 'children' है (irregular)।

Question 6 SSC CHSL 2023

Identify the type of noun:

Gold is a precious metal.

A. Proper Noun
B. Common Noun
C. Abstract Noun
D. Material Noun
Answer: D. Material Noun

'Gold' एक substance / material है → Material Noun।

Question 7 SSC CPO 2023

Choose the correct option:

The news _____ very shocking.

A. are
B. is
C. were
D. have been
Answer: B. is

'News' singular है, इसलिए singular verb 'is'।

Question 8 SSC MTS 2023

Identify the type of noun:

Mumbai is the financial capital of India.

A. Proper Noun
B. Common Noun
C. Collective Noun
D. Abstract Noun
Answer: A. Proper Noun

'Mumbai' एक specific city का name है → Proper Noun।

15. Quick Revision – Nouns

Rule 1 Noun = name of person/place/thing/idea
Rule 2 Proper = specific → capital letter
Rule 3 Common = general → no capital
Rule 4 Collective = group (team, army, flock)
Rule 5 Abstract = quality/idea (honesty, love)
Rule 6 Material = substance (gold, water, wood)
Rule 7 Police, people = plural
Rule 8 News, physics = singular
Rule 9 Singular → 's, Plural → '
Rule 10 Irregular plurals: man→men, child→children
🎯 SSC Nouns Final Checklist
  • Proper nouns → unique name, capital letter।
  • Common nouns → general name, no capital।
  • Collective nouns → group (team, army, flock)।
  • Abstract nouns → quality/idea/emotion (honesty, love)।
  • Material nouns → substance (gold, water, wood)।
  • Uncountable nouns → no 'a/an', no plural (water, rice)।
  • Police, people, cattle → always plural।
  • News, physics, mathematics, politics → singular।
  • Possessive: singular → 's, plural → '।
  • Irregular plurals: man→men, child→children, tooth→teeth।
  • Same form: sheep, deer, fish, species।
  • Collective noun का verb singular/plural sense पर depend करता है।
