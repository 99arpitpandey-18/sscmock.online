1. Adverb क्या होता है?

Adverb वह word है जो किसी verb, adjective, दूसरे adverb या कभी-कभी पूरे sentence के बारे में additional information देता है।

हिंदी में Adverb को क्रिया-विशेषण कहते हैं।

📌 Definition An adverb is a word that modifies a verb, an adjective, another adverb, or sometimes an entire sentence.

यानी जो शब्द बताए कि कोई काम कैसे, कब, कहाँ, कितनी बार या कितनी मात्रा में हुआ, वह adverb हो सकता है।

Examples

  • He runs fast.
  • She sings beautifully.
  • He came yesterday.
  • She is very intelligent.
  • He speaks quite slowly.
Example Analysis:

He runs quickly.

यहाँ quickly बताता है कि वह कैसे runs करता है। इसलिए "quickly" एक adverb है।

2. Adverb को कैसे पहचानें?

Sentence में निम्न questions पूछकर adverb identify किया जा सकता है:

Question Meaning Example
How? कैसे? He runs quickly.
When? कब? He came yesterday.
Where? कहाँ? Come here.
How often? कितनी बार? He always comes.
How much? कितनी मात्रा में? He is very happy.

4. Adverb of Manner

जो adverb किसी काम के होने का तरीका या manner बताता है, उसे Adverb of Manner कहते हैं।

यह सामान्यतः How? का answer देता है।

Examples

  • He works hard.
  • She sings beautifully.
  • He completed the work carefully.
  • The child walked slowly.
  • He answered the question correctly.
⭐ Common Pattern बहुत से adverbs adjective + -ly से बनते हैं।

slow → slowly
careful → carefully
quick → quickly

5. Adverb of Place

जो adverb किसी action की place/location बताता है, उसे Adverb of Place कहते हैं।

Important Words

here, there, everywhere, nowhere, somewhere, inside, outside, above, below, nearby, away

Come here.

He lives there.

I looked everywhere.

The children are playing outside.

6. Adverb of Time

जो adverb action के समय को बताता है, उसे Adverb of Time कहते हैं।

Important Words

now, today, tomorrow, yesterday, soon, already, recently, immediately, later, afterwards

I will meet him tomorrow.

He has already finished the work.

She arrived yesterday.

Come soon.

7. Adverb of Frequency

जो adverb बताता है कि कोई action कितनी बार होता है, उसे Adverb of Frequency कहते हैं।

Adverb Meaning
always हमेशा
usually आमतौर पर
often अक्सर
sometimes कभी-कभी
rarely बहुत कम
seldom शायद ही कभी
never कभी नहीं

Examples

  • He always speaks the truth.
  • She usually comes on time.
  • I often read books.
  • He never tells a lie.
  • They sometimes play cricket.

8. Adverb of Degree

जो adverb किसी adjective, verb या दूसरे adverb की intensity/degree बताता है, उसे Adverb of Degree कहते हैं।

Important Words

very, too, quite, rather, almost, enough, extremely, completely, nearly, hardly

He is very intelligent.

She is extremely beautiful.

The tea is too hot.

He is quite happy.

I am almost ready.

9. Adverb of Reason

जो word result या reason को express करता है, उसे adverb of reason/result के रूप में पढ़ा जाता है।

Important Words

therefore, hence, thus, consequently, accordingly

He worked hard; therefore, he succeeded.

He was ill; hence, he could not attend the class.

He did not study; therefore, he failed.

10. Interrogative Adverbs

जब when, where, why और how question पूछने के लिए प्रयोग होते हैं, तो इन्हें interrogative adverbs कहा जाता है।

Word Question Example
When कब? When did he come?
Where कहाँ? Where do you live?
Why क्यों? Why are you late?
How कैसे? How did you do it?

11. Relative Adverbs

When, where और why कभी-कभी दो clauses को जोड़ते हैं। तब ये relative adverbs कहलाते हैं।

I remember the day when we met.

This is the place where he lives.

I know the reason why he left.
⭐ SSC Trick जब when/where/why के पहले कोई suitable noun हो और उसके बाद clause आए, तो relative adverb का possibility check करें।

12. Formation of Adverbs

कई adverbs adjectives में suffix जोड़कर बनाए जाते हैं, लेकिन हर adverb "-ly" से नहीं बनता।

Adjective Adverb
quick quickly
slow slowly
careful carefully
beautiful beautifully
happy happily
easy easily
angry angrily

Important Exceptions

⚠️ Hard ≠ Hardly

hard = मेहनत से / intensely
hardly = मुश्किल से / almost not
He works hard.
→ वह मेहनत करता है।

He hardly works.
→ वह लगभग काम नहीं करता।

Late / Lately

He arrived late.
→ वह देर से आया।

I haven't seen him lately.
→ मैंने उसे हाल में नहीं देखा।

Near / Nearly

Come near.

It is nearly midnight.

High / Highly

The bird flew high.

He is highly respected.

13. Position of Adverbs

Adverb की position sentence में उसके type और function पर depend करती है।

1. Adverb of Manner

सामान्यतः verb/object के बाद आता है।

He completed the work carefully.

2. Adverb of Frequency

Main verb से पहले लेकिन "be" verb के बाद सामान्यतः आता है।

He always comes on time.

He is always punctual.

3. Adverb of Time

अक्सर sentence के अंत में आ सकता है।

I met him yesterday.

4. Adverb of Place

Come here.

Go there.

14. Important SSC Rules

Rule 1 — Good vs Well

Good → Adjective
Well → Generally Adverb
He is a good player.

He plays well.

Rule 2 — Fast

Fast adjective और adverb दोनों हो सकता है।

He is a fast runner.

He runs fast.

Rule 3 — Daily vs Dailily

He goes to school daily.

He reads a daily newspaper.

Dailily standard English में सामान्य प्रयोग नहीं है।

Rule 4 — Too vs Very

Very = बहुत

Too = जरूरत से ज्यादा / excessively
He is very tired.
→ वह बहुत थका हुआ है।

He is too tired to walk.
→ वह इतना ज्यादा थका है कि चल नहीं सकता।

Rule 5 — Enough

Enough adjective के बाद लेकिन adverb के रूप में verb के साथ अलग position ले सकता है।

He is strong enough.

He worked enough.

Rule 6 — Very / Much

Very → सामान्यतः adjective/adverb के positive form के साथ।

Much → comparative या past participle contexts में common है।
He is very happy.

He is much better now.

15. Common Errors

❌ Error 1 He runs very fastly.

✅ Correct: He runs very fast.

"Fast" स्वयं adverb है; fastly नहीं।
❌ Error 2 He works hardly.

✅ Correct: He works hard.

यदि meaning "मेहनत से" है, तो hard होगा।
❌ Error 3 She sings good.

✅ Correct: She sings well.
❌ Error 4 He is enough strong.

✅ Correct: He is strong enough.
❌ Error 5 He is very better now.

✅ Correct: He is much better now.
❌ Error 6 He arrived lately yesterday.

✅ Correct: He arrived late yesterday.
❌ Error 7 He nearly ran fastly.

Correct usage: He ran very fast.

16. SSC PYQs & Exam-Level Questions

PYQ 01 SSC Pattern

He works ______.

A. hardly
B. hard
C. harderly
D. hardily
Answer: B. hard
Explanation: यहाँ meaning है "मेहनत से", इसलिए hard।
PYQ 02 SSC Pattern

She sings ______.

A. good
B. well
C. betterly
D. bestly
Answer: B. well
Explanation: Sing एक verb है और उसके manner को modify करने के लिए adverb "well" आएगा।
PYQ 03 SSC Pattern

He is ______ better now.

A. very
B. much
C. too
D. many
Answer: B. much
Explanation: Comparative adjective "better" के साथ much का प्रयोग किया जा सकता है।
PYQ 04 SSC Pattern

He is strong ______ to lift the box.

A. too
B. very
C. enough
D. much
Answer: C. enough
Explanation: Structure:
adjective + enough
PYQ 05 SSC Pattern

He ______ comes late to the office.

A. never
B. very
C. too
D. much
Answer: A. never
Explanation: Never frequency बताता है और main verb से पहले आ सकता है।
PYQ 06 SSC Pattern

The child walked ______.

A. slow
B. slowly
C. slowness
D. slower
Answer: B. slowly
Explanation: Walk के manner को describe करने के लिए adverb चाहिए।
PYQ 07 SSC Pattern

He ______ completed the work.

A. already
B. very
C. too
D. enough
Answer: A. already
Explanation: Already time बताता है कि action पहले ही complete हो चुका है।
PYQ 08 SSC Pattern

He is ______ tired to walk.

A. very
B. too
C. enough
D. much
Answer: B. too
Explanation: Structure:
too + adjective + to-infinitive
PYQ 09 SSC Pattern

I have not seen him ______.

A. late
B. lately
C. latest
D. laterly
Answer: B. lately
Explanation: Lately = recently.
Late = देर से।
PYQ 10 SSC Pattern

The bird flew ______ in the sky.

A. highly
B. high
C. higherly
D. highestly
Answer: B. high
Explanation: यहाँ height/distance की बात है, इसलिए high।
