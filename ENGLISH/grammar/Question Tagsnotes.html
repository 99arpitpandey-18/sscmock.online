1. Question Tags क्या होते हैं?

Question Tag एक छोटा question होता है जो statement के अंत में लगाया जाता है। इसका प्रयोग सामान्यतः confirmation या agreement प्राप्त करने के लिए किया जाता है।

⭐ सबसे Important Definition

Statement + Short Question = Question Tag

You are a student, aren't you?

तुम विद्यार्थी हो, है ना?

He is intelligent, isn't he?

वह बुद्धिमान है, है ना?

2. Question Tags का Basic Rule

🔥 Golden Rule

Positive Statement → Negative Tag

Negative Statement → Positive Tag

You are coming, aren't you? ✔

You are not coming, are you? ✔

He is a doctor, isn't he?

He isn't a doctor, is he?

⚠ SSC Alert

Statement और Tag का polarity pattern हमेशा ध्यान रखें: Positive के साथ Negative और Negative के साथ Positive।

3. Positive Statement → Negative Tag

यदि main statement affirmative या positive है, तो question tag negative होगा।

She is beautiful, isn't she?

They are ready, aren't they?

He can swim, can't he?

You like cricket, don't you?

She went home, didn't she?

4. Negative Statement → Positive Tag

यदि statement negative है, तो question tag positive होगा।

She isn't beautiful, is she?

They aren't ready, are they?

He can't swim, can he?

You don't like cricket, do you?

She didn't go home, did she?

5. Auxiliary Verb का Rule

Question Tag में सामान्यतः वही auxiliary verb दोबारा प्रयोग किया जाता है जो main sentence में दिया गया हो।

He is coming, isn't he?

They are ready, aren't they?

She has finished the work, hasn't she?

You can drive, can't you?

He will come, won't he?

🔥 Shortcut

Main sentence में helping verb दिखाई दे रही है? वही verb tag में use करो।

6. Do / Does का प्रयोग

Simple Present Tense में यदि auxiliary verb नहीं दिया गया हो, तो Question Tag में do / does का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

You play cricket, don't you?

They work hard, don't they?

He plays cricket, doesn't he?

She likes music, doesn't she?

❌ He plays cricket, don't he?

✅ He plays cricket, doesn't he?

क्योंकि subject “He” singular है।

7. Simple Past में Did

Simple Past Tense में यदि helping verb नहीं है, तो Question Tag में did / didn't का प्रयोग होता है।

He went there, didn't he?

She visited Delhi, didn't she?

They played well, didn't they?

He didn't come, did he?

❌ He went there, wasn't he?

✅ He went there, didn't he?

8. Be Verbs के साथ Question Tags

Is, am, are, was और were को tag में directly use किया जाता है।

He is honest, isn't he?

She is not ready, is she?

They are students, aren't they?

They weren't late, were they?

He was present, wasn't he?

She wasn't angry, was she?

9. Modal Verbs के साथ Question Tags

Can, could, may, might, shall, should, will, would, must आदि modal verbs tag में वही modal रखते हैं।

He can swim, can't he?

She could help us, couldn't she?

You will come, won't you?

He should study, shouldn't he?

They would help us, wouldn't they?

We must obey the rules, mustn't we?

10. Pronoun Rule

Question Tag में noun को appropriate pronoun से replace किया जाता है।

Rahul is a student, isn't he?

Sita is intelligent, isn't she?

The boys are playing, aren't they?

The book is interesting, isn't it?

The girls are ready, aren't they?

⭐ SSC Trick

Person → He / She

Thing / Animal → It

Plural persons/things → They

11. Imperative Sentences के साथ Question Tags

Imperative sentence में order, request, advice या command दी जाती है। ऐसे sentences में सामान्यतः will you? का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Open the door, will you?

Close the window, will you?

Help me, will you?

Take your seat, will you?

Negative Imperative

Don't make noise, will you?

Don't be late, will you?

12. “Let's” के साथ Question Tag

🔥 SSC Favourite Rule

“Let's” = Let us in the sense of suggestion.

Let's के साथ Question Tag सामान्यतः shall we? होता है।

Let's go for a walk, shall we?

Let's play cricket, shall we?

Let's start the work, shall we?

Let's have some tea, shall we?

13. Let Us / Let Me

“Let us” यदि permission/request के sense में है, तो सामान्यतः will you? प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Let us go now, will you?

Let me help you, will you?

Let me have a look, will you?

⚠ Difference याद रखें

Let's go, shall we?

Let us go, will you? जब permission/request का sense हो।

14. “I am” → “Aren't I?”

🔥 सबसे Important Special Rule

“I am” के positive sentence का question tag aren't I? होता है।

I am late, aren't I?

I am your friend, aren't I?

I am right, aren't I?

❌ I am right, amn't I?

✅ I am right, aren't I?

15. “There” के साथ Question Tags

जब sentence में existential “there” हो, तो tag में भी there का प्रयोग होता है।

There is a problem, isn't there?

There are many students, aren't there?

There isn't any water, is there?

There weren't many people, were there?

16. This / That के साथ Question Tags

This is your book, isn't it?

That is your car, isn't it?

This isn't difficult, is it?

That wasn't your bag, was it?

⭐ Trick

This / That → it

17. These / Those के साथ Question Tags

These are your books, aren't they?

Those are your shoes, aren't they?

These aren't yours, are they?

Those weren't your friends, were they?

18. Have / Has / Had के साथ Question Tags

यदि have/has/had helping verb के रूप में perfect tense में प्रयुक्त है, तो वही verb tag में आता है।

He has finished the work, hasn't he?

They have arrived, haven't they?

She had completed the task, hadn't she?

He hasn't come yet, has he?

Have as Main Verb

He has a car, doesn't he?

She has a beautiful house, doesn't she?

⚠ Exam Alert

“Have” का use helping verb है या main verb, इसे पहचानना बहुत important है।

19. Used to के साथ Question Tag

“Used to” past habit को show करता है। SSC questions में इसके tag को लेकर confusion हो सकता है।

He used to play cricket, didn't he?

She used to live here, didn't she?

They used to visit us, didn't they?

20. Need / Dare के साथ Question Tags

Need और dare कभी modal की तरह और कभी ordinary verb की तरह behave कर सकते हैं। इसलिए sentence structure देखना जरूरी है।

He needn't go, need he?

You don't need to worry, do you?

He dare not oppose me, dare he?

He doesn't dare to speak, does he?

21. Negative Meaning वाले Words

कुछ words sentence में grammatical “not” के बिना भी negative meaning देते हैं। ऐसे cases में tag positive होगा।

⭐ SSC Important

hardly, scarcely, rarely, seldom, never, nobody, no one, nothing आदि negative sense दे सकते हैं।

He never lies, does he?

She hardly works, does she?

He rarely comes here, does he?

Nobody came, did they?

Nothing happened, did it?

22. Common Errors – SSC

Incorrect Correct Reason
He is a doctor, is he? He is a doctor, isn't he? Positive statement → negative tag.
He isn't ready, isn't he? He isn't ready, is he? Negative statement → positive tag.
She likes music, don't she? She likes music, doesn't she? She → does.
They went home, weren't they? They went home, didn't they? Simple Past → did.
I am right, amn't I? I am right, aren't I? Special form: aren't I?
Let's go, will we? Let's go, shall we? Let's → shall we?
Open the door, shall we? Open the door, will you? Imperative → will you?
There is a problem, isn't it? There is a problem, isn't there? There → there.
Nobody came, didn't he? Nobody came, did they? Nobody → they.
He never lies, doesn't he? He never lies, does he? Never is negative in meaning.

23. Special Cases – SSC Favourite

Nobody / Somebody / Everybody

Everybody is happy, aren't they?

Nobody came, did they?

Somebody has taken my book, haven't they?

Nothing / Everything

Nothing is impossible, is it?

Everything is ready, isn't it?

Negative Adverbs

He seldom goes there, does he?

She rarely complains, does she?

He hardly knows me, does he?

24. SSC Question Tags Tricks

🔥 Trick 1 – Polarity

Positive → Negative Tag

Negative → Positive Tag

🔥 Trick 2 – Helping Verb

Main sentence में helping verb है? वही tag में use करो।

🔥 Trick 3 – No Helping Verb

Present → do / does

Past → did

🔥 Trick 4 – Let's

Let's go, shall we?

🔥 Trick 5 – I am

I am right, aren't I?

🔥 Trick 6 – Imperative

Open the door, will you?

🔥 Trick 7 – Negative Meaning

Never / hardly / rarely / seldom → Positive tag

🔥 Trick 8 – There

There is a problem, isn't there?

25. SSC PYQ-Based / Exam Pattern Practice

Question 1 SSC Pattern

He is a good student, _____?

A. is he
B. isn't he
C. doesn't he
D. wasn't he
Answer: B. isn't he?

Positive statement है और auxiliary “is” है, इसलिए negative tag “isn't he?” होगा।

Question 2 SSC Pattern

She isn't coming, _____?

A. isn't she
B. doesn't she
C. is she
D. did she
Answer: C. is she?

Statement negative है, इसलिए tag positive होगा।

Question 3 SSC Pattern

He plays cricket, _____?

A. don't he
B. doesn't he
C. isn't he
D. didn't he
Answer: B. doesn't he?

Simple Present + singular subject “He” → doesn't he?

Question 4 SSC Pattern

They went to Delhi, _____?

A. weren't they
B. didn't they
C. don't they
D. haven't they
Answer: B. didn't they?

Simple Past में helping verb नहीं है, इसलिए did का प्रयोग होगा।

Question 5 SSC Pattern

Let's go for a walk, _____?

A. will we
B. shall we
C. do we
D. aren't we
Answer: B. shall we?

Let's के साथ सामान्यतः shall we? आता है।

Question 6 SSC Pattern

I am late, _____?

A. amn't I
B. isn't I
C. aren't I
D. don't I
Answer: C. aren't I?

“I am” के positive statement के साथ special tag “aren't I?” आता है।

Question 7 SSC Pattern

There is a problem, _____?

A. isn't it
B. isn't there
C. doesn't there
D. is there
Answer: B. isn't there?

Existential “there” sentence में है, इसलिए tag में भी “there” आएगा।

Question 8 SSC Pattern

He never tells lies, _____?

A. doesn't he
B. does he
C. isn't he
D. did he
Answer: B. does he?

“Never” negative meaning देता है, इसलिए positive tag “does he?” होगा।

Question 9 SSC Pattern

Open the door, _____?

A. shall we
B. will you
C. do you
D. aren't you
Answer: B. will you?

Imperative sentence के साथ सामान्यतः “will you?” का प्रयोग होता है।

Question 10 SSC Pattern

Nobody came to the party, _____?

A. did they
B. didn't they
C. did he
D. weren't they
Answer: A. did they?

“Nobody” negative sense देता है और इसके लिए pronoun “they” का प्रयोग किया जाता है।
