1. Sentence Improvement क्या होता है?

Sentence Improvement में दिए गए sentence के किसी underlined या highlighted part को grammar, usage और meaning के अनुसार improve करना होता है।

SSC exams में question का उद्देश्य केवल grammar rule पूछना नहीं होता, बल्कि यह check करना होता है कि candidate sentence में सही structure, tense, preposition, article, pronoun, conjunction और word usage पहचान सकता है या नहीं।

⭐ सबसे पहले यह याद रखें

Sentence Improvement में केवल छोटा हिस्सा मत देखिए — पूरे sentence का context देखिए।

Correct option वही होगा जो grammar के साथ-साथ meaning और sentence structure भी सही रखे।

Incorrect: He do not know the answer.

Correct: He does not know the answer.

Reason: Singular subject He के साथ does आएगा।

2. Sentence Improvement कैसे Solve करें?

🎯 SSC Solving Method

Step 1: पहले पूरा sentence पढ़ें।

Step 2: Underlined part के पहले और बाद का context देखें।

Step 3: Subject और verb identify करें।

Step 4: Tense, article, preposition, pronoun और conjunction check करें।

Step 5: Meaning और logical connection देखें।

Step 6: सबसे grammatically correct option चुनें।

3. Subject-Verb Agreement

Subject और verb का number और person आपस में agree करना चाहिए। Singular subject के साथ singular verb और plural subject के साथ plural verb का सामान्य नियम लागू होता है।

❌ He go to school every day.

✅ He goes to school every day.

❌ The boys plays cricket.

✅ The boys play cricket.

Each / Every

Each, Every, Everyone, Everybody, Someone, Nobody जैसे words सामान्यतः singular verb लेते हैं।

❌ Every student are present.

✅ Every student is present.

❌ Each of the boys have a book.

✅ Each of the boys has a book.

Either / Neither

❌ Neither of the answers are correct.

✅ Neither of the answers is correct.

4. Tense Errors

Sentence Improvement में tense की consistency बहुत important है। Time expression और context देखकर tense choose करें।

Simple Present

❌ He go to office every day.

✅ He goes to office every day.

Past Time

❌ I meet him yesterday.

✅ I met him yesterday.

Since / For

Since = starting point

For = duration

❌ He has been living here since five years.

✅ He has been living here for five years.

❌ She has been ill for Monday.

✅ She has been ill since Monday.

5. Article Errors

A, An और The का use noun के context और pronunciation के अनुसार होता है।

❌ He is an university student.

✅ He is a university student.

❌ He is a honest man.

✅ He is an honest man.

Superlative

❌ He is best player in the team.

✅ He is the best player in the team.

6. Preposition Errors

Preposition की छोटी गलती sentence का पूरा meaning बदल सकती है। SSC में fixed usage और context-based prepositions बहुत important हैं।

Interested in

❌ He is interested on music.

✅ He is interested in music.

Good at

❌ She is good in mathematics.

✅ She is good at mathematics.

Depend on

❌ Success depends of hard work.

✅ Success depends on hard work.

Different from

❌ Your answer is different than mine.

✅ Your answer is different from mine.

7. Pronoun Errors

Pronoun को अपने antecedent के number, person और grammatical role के अनुसार होना चाहिए।

❌ Every boy must bring their book.

✅ Every boy must bring his book.

❌ Me and Rahul went to the market.

✅ Rahul and I went to the market.

Subject vs Object

❌ The teacher called he.

✅ The teacher called him.

8. Adjective और Adverb

Adjective noun/pronoun को modify करता है, जबकि adverb verb, adjective या दूसरे adverb को modify कर सकता है।

❌ He runs quick.

✅ He runs quickly.

❌ She is a beautifully girl.

✅ She is a beautiful girl.

Good / Well

He is a good player.

He plays well.

9. Comparison

Comparative और superlative structures में degree और comparison का सही use जरूरी है।

❌ He is more taller than his brother.

✅ He is taller than his brother.

❌ This is the most easiest question.

✅ This is the easiest question.

Than के साथ Comparative

❌ She is more intelligent to her sister.

✅ She is more intelligent than her sister.

One of the + Superlative + Plural Noun

❌ He is one of the best player in the team.

✅ He is one of the best players in the team.

10. Conjunction Errors

Conjunctions sentence के parts को logically connect करते हैं। Correlative conjunctions का pair सही होना चाहिए।

Either...or

❌ Either Rahul and Mohan will come.

✅ Either Rahul or Mohan will come.

Neither...nor

❌ Neither he or his brother came.

✅ Neither he nor his brother came.

Although / But

❌ Although he is poor, but he is honest.

✅ Although he is poor, he is honest.

Because / Therefore

❌ Because he was ill, therefore he could not come.

✅ Because he was ill, he could not come.

11. Modifier Errors

Modifier को उस word के पास होना चाहिए जिसे वह modify कर रहा है। गलत placement से sentence का meaning confusing हो सकता है।

❌ Walking down the road, the trees looked beautiful.

✅ Walking down the road, I saw that the trees looked beautiful.

⭐ SSC Tip

Introductory phrase के बाद तुरंत ऐसा subject आना चाहिए जो उस action को logically perform कर सके।

12. Parallelism

जब sentence में समान grammatical functions की list हो, तो उनका structure भी समान होना चाहिए।

❌ He likes reading, writing and to play.

✅ He likes reading, writing and playing.

❌ She wants to sing, dancing and acting.

✅ She wants to sing, dance and act.

13. Infinitive & Gerund

Enjoy + Gerund

❌ I enjoy to read books.

✅ I enjoy reading books.

Want + To-infinitive

❌ I want going home.

✅ I want to go home.

Look forward to + Gerund

❌ I look forward to meet you.

✅ I look forward to meeting you.

14. Active-Passive Related Improvement

कभी-कभी sentence का grammatical structure passive या active voice के कारण incorrect होता है।

❌ The work has completed by him.

✅ The work has been completed by him.

❌ The letter was wrote by Rahul.

✅ The letter was written by Rahul.

15. Narration Related Improvement

Reported speech में tense, pronoun और time-expression का context के अनुसार सही बदलाव जरूरी हो सकता है।

Direct: He said, “I am busy.”

Reported: He said that he was busy.

16. Redundancy – अनावश्यक शब्द

जब sentence में एक ही meaning देने वाले unnecessary words आ जाते हैं, तो Sentence Improvement में concise और grammatically correct expression चुना जाता है।

❌ Return back home.

✅ Return home.

❌ Repeat again.

✅ Repeat.

❌ Discuss about the issue.

✅ Discuss the issue.

17. Word Choice

कुछ words देखने में similar होते हैं लेकिन उनका grammatical या contextual use अलग होता है।

Advice / Advise

Advice = noun

Advise = verb

He gave me good advice.

The doctor advised him to rest.

Accept / Except

Accept = receive / agree

Except = excluding

Its / It's

Its = possessive

It's = it is / it has

18. Idiomatic Usage

कई expressions fixed form में use होते हैं। SSC में इनके गलत combinations को improve करना पूछा जा सकता है।

❌ He is senior than me.

✅ He is senior to me.

❌ She is prefer tea than coffee.

✅ She prefers tea to coffee.

❌ He is capable to do this work.

✅ He is capable of doing this work.

19. Common Errors – SSC

Incorrect Correct Reason
He do not know. He does not know. He → singular subject.
She is an university student. She is a university student. University begins with /yuː/ sound.
He has been here since five years. He has been here for five years. For → duration.
She is good in English. She is good at English. Fixed usage: good at.
Neither he or I came. Neither he nor I came. Neither...nor pair.
He is more taller than me. He is taller than me. Double comparative.
One of the best player. One of the best players. One of + plural noun.
He discussed about the matter. He discussed the matter. Discuss does not require about.
I look forward to meet you. I look forward to meeting you. Look forward to + gerund.
Although he was tired but he worked. Although he was tired, he worked. Although + but together avoid करें.

20. Sentence Improvement – Golden Rules

🔥 Golden Rule 1 – पहले Subject और Verb Check करें

He go ❌ → He goes ✔

🔥 Golden Rule 2 – Time Expression को पहचानें

Yesterday → past context

Since/for → perfect/perfect continuous structures में context देखें।

🔥 Golden Rule 3 – Fixed Preposition याद रखें

interested in, good at, depend on, capable of

🔥 Golden Rule 4 – Correlative Conjunction Pair Check करें

either...or

neither...nor

not only...but also

🔥 Golden Rule 5 – Double Comparative से बचें

more better ❌

better ✔

🔥 Golden Rule 6 – Parallel Structure रखें

reading, writing and playing ✔

🔥 Golden Rule 7 – Unnecessary Words हटाएँ

discuss about ❌ → discuss ✔

21. SSC Sentence Improvement Tricks

🔥 Trick 1 – Whole Sentence पढ़ो

केवल underlined portion देखकर option select मत करो। पूरा context check करो।

🔥 Trick 2 – Subject खोजो

Verb से पहले असली subject identify करो। बीच में आने वाले words से confuse मत हो।

🔥 Trick 3 – Time Words पकड़ो

yesterday, already, since, for, by the time, last year आदि tense की ओर संकेत कर सकते हैं।

🔥 Trick 4 – Fixed Phrases पहचानो

interested in, depend on, good at, capable of, prefer X to Y.

🔥 Trick 5 – Double Error Check

ऐसा option मत चुनो जो एक error सुधारकर दूसरा error पैदा कर दे।

🔥 Trick 6 – Meaning भी Check करो

Grammar सही होने के साथ sentence का original intended meaning भी preserve होना चाहिए।

22. SSC Pattern Practice

Question 1 SSC Pattern

He do not know the answer.

A. He does not know the answer.
B. He did not knows the answer.
C. He do not knows the answer.
D. No improvement
Answer: A. He does not know the answer.

He singular है, इसलिए does + base verb “know” आएगा।

Question 2 SSC Pattern

She is good in mathematics.

A. good on mathematics
B. good at mathematics
C. good with mathematics
D. No improvement
Answer: B. good at mathematics

Fixed usage: good at.

Question 3 SSC Pattern

He is more taller than his brother.

A. very taller
B. more tall
C. taller
D. No improvement
Answer: C. taller

Comparative adjective के साथ “more” की आवश्यकता नहीं है।

Question 4 SSC Pattern

I look forward to meet you.

A. to meeting you
B. meeting to you
C. to met you
D. No improvement
Answer: A. to meeting you

“Look forward to” में to preposition है, इसलिए उसके बाद gerund आता है।

Question 5 SSC Pattern

Neither Rahul or Mohan came to the meeting.

A. Neither Rahul nor Mohan
B. Neither Rahul and Mohan
C. Either Rahul nor Mohan
D. No improvement
Answer: A. Neither Rahul nor Mohan

Correct pair: neither...nor.

Question 6 SSC Pattern

He has been working here since five years.

A. from five years
B. for five years
C. by five years
D. No improvement
Answer: B. for five years

Five years duration है, इसलिए for आएगा।

Question 7 SSC Pattern

One of the best player in the team is injured.

A. one of the best players
B. one of best player
C. one of the better player
D. No improvement
Answer: A. one of the best players

One of the + superlative + plural noun.

Question 8 SSC Pattern

He discussed about the problem.

A. discussed on the problem
B. discussed the problem
C. discussed over the problem
D. No improvement
Answer: B. discussed the problem

“Discuss” के साथ सामान्यतः “about” नहीं लगाया जाता।

Question 9 SSC Pattern

Although he was tired but he continued working.

A. Although he was tired, he continued working.
B. Although but he was tired, he continued working.
C. Although he tired, but continued working.
D. No improvement
Answer: A. Although he was tired, he continued working.

Although के साथ इस structure में “but” unnecessary है।

Question 10 SSC Pattern

He enjoys to play cricket.

A. enjoys playing cricket
B. enjoys play cricket
C. enjoy to playing cricket
D. No improvement
Answer: A. enjoys playing cricket

Enjoy के बाद gerund (-ing form) आता है।

23. Error Detection Practice

Find the error:

He is senior than me.

Error: than

Correct: He is senior to me.

Find the error:

She is good in English.

Error: in

Correct: She is good at English.

Find the error:

He has returned back home.

Error: back

Correct: He has returned home.

Find the error:

She wants going to Delhi.

Error: going

Correct: She wants to go to Delhi.
