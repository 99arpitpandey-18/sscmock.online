1. Subject-Verb Agreement क्या है?

Subject-Verb Agreement का अर्थ है कि sentence में verb अपने subject के number और person के अनुसार होना चाहिए।

हिंदी: कर्ता (Subject) एकवचन है तो उसके अनुसार singular verb और कर्ता बहुवचन है तो plural verb का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

⭐ Golden Rule

Singular Subject → Singular Verb

Plural Subject → Plural Verb

Examples

He plays cricket.
वह क्रिकेट खेलता है।

They play cricket.
वे क्रिकेट खेलते हैं।

The boy is happy.
लड़का खुश है।

The boys are happy.
लड़के खुश हैं।

2. Basic Subject-Verb Rule

Present Simple में सबसे ज्यादा confusion होता है क्योंकि singular third-person subject के साथ verb में s/es आता है।

Subject Verb Example
I play I play cricket.
We play We play cricket.
You play You play cricket.
They play They play cricket.
He plays He plays cricket.
She plays She plays cricket.
Ram plays Ram plays cricket.
❌ Common Mistake

❌ He play cricket.

✅ He plays cricket.

❌ They plays cricket.

✅ They play cricket.

3. Singular और Plural Subject

Singular Subject

One person, one thing या one unit को singular माना जाता है।

The boy plays football.

The girl is intelligent.

My brother works hard.

The book is interesting.

Plural Subject

The boys play football.

The girls are intelligent.

My brothers work hard.

The books are interesting.

4. Third Person Singular Rule

He / She / It / Singular Noun के साथ Present Simple में:

V1 + s/es

कब -s लगाएँ?

He runs fast.

She reads books.

Ram plays cricket.

The dog barks.

कब -es लगाएँ?

सामान्यतः s, sh, ch, x, o पर समाप्त होने वाले verbs में -es लगाया जाता है।

go → goes

wash → washes

watch → watches

fix → fixes

pass → passes

Consonant + y

यदि verb consonant + y पर समाप्त हो तो y → ies हो जाता है।

study → studies

try → tries

carry → carries

लेकिन vowel + y में केवल s लगता है।

play → plays

enjoy → enjoys

buy → buys

5. Each / Every / Each of / Every one of

⭐ Important SSC Rule

Each और Every को grammatically singular माना जाता है।

इसलिए इनके साथ singular verb आती है।

Each boy has a book.

Every student is present.

Each of the students is intelligent.

Every one of the players was ready.

❌ Error

❌ Each of the boys are present.

✅ Each of the boys is present.

“boys” plural है, लेकिन main subject Each है।

Each + Plural Noun?

Each boy सही है।

Each boys गलत है।

6. Either / Neither

Either / Neither सामान्यतः singular होते हैं और इनके साथ singular verb का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Either boy is capable.

Neither answer is correct.

Either of the two boys is ready.

Neither of the candidates has arrived.

❌ Neither of the boys are ready.

✅ Neither of the boys is ready.

Either...or / Neither...nor

जब दो subjects को either...or या neither...nor से जोड़ा जाए, तो verb सामान्यतः उस subject के अनुसार होती है जो verb के सबसे पास हो।

Either Ram or his friends are coming.

Either his friends or Ram is coming.

Neither the teacher nor the students are ready.

Neither the students nor the teacher is ready.

7. As Well As

⭐ SSC Rule

A as well as B में verb पहले subject यानी A के अनुसार आती है।

Ram as well as his friends is coming.

The teacher as well as the students is responsible.

The students as well as the teacher are responsible.

❌ Ram as well as his friends are coming.

✅ Ram as well as his friends is coming.

8. Along With

along with, together with, accompanied by, as well as, including आदि subject को plural नहीं बनाते।

Verb मुख्य subject के अनुसार होगी।

Ram along with his friends is going.

The teacher along with the students is present.

The students along with their teacher are present.

9. Together With / Accompanied By

The principal together with the teachers is attending the meeting.

Rahul accompanied by his friends was present.

The captain along with his players is ready.

10. Collective Nouns

Collective noun किसी group को एक unit के रूप में represent करता है।

Examples: team, committee, jury, family, class, crowd, army, audience

यदि पूरा group एक unit की तरह काम कर रहा है, तो singular verb का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

The team is winning.

The committee has taken a decision.

The family is happy.

यदि members individually अलग-अलग काम कर रहे हों, तो context के अनुसार plural verb आ सकती है।

The team are arguing among themselves.

यहाँ team के members individually act कर रहे हैं।

11. A Number of / The Number of

A number of + plural noun + plural verb

The number of + plural noun + singular verb

A number of students are absent.

A number of people have applied.

The number of students is increasing.

The number of applicants has increased.

❌ The number of students are increasing.

✅ The number of students is increasing.

12. One of + Plural Noun

One of + plural noun + singular verb

One of my friends is a doctor.

One of the boys has won the race.

One of the best players was injured.

One of those who...

यह SSC में important structure है।

He is one of those students who work hard.

यहाँ “who” का antecedent “students” है, इसलिए plural verb work।

13. Pair Nouns – जो हमेशा Pair में आते हैं

कुछ nouns pair की चीजों को represent करते हैं:

scissors, trousers, spectacles, shoes, gloves, shorts, clothes आदि।

इन nouns के साथ सामान्यतः plural verb आती है।

My trousers are new.

These scissors are sharp.

His spectacles are broken.

A Pair of

A pair of + pair noun → singular verb

A pair of scissors is on the table.

A pair of trousers is expensive.

Two pairs of shoes are missing.

14. Time, Money, Distance और Quantity

जब time, money, distance या quantity को एक single unit के रूप में माना जाए, तो singular verb का प्रयोग होता है।

Ten kilometres is a long distance.

Five years is a long time.

Ten thousand rupees is enough.

Two litres of water is sufficient.

लेकिन यदि individual items की बात हो रही हो, तो plural verb हो सकती है।

Five years have passed since we met.

यहाँ पाँच अलग-अलग years की duration की बात हो रही है।

15. There / Here के साथ Verb

There/Here के बाद verb का agreement उसके बाद आने वाले वास्तविक subject के अनुसार होता है।

There is a book on the table.

There are many books on the table.

There was a problem.

There were many problems.

❌ There is many students in the class.

✅ There are many students in the class.

16. Who / Which / That के साथ Agreement

Relative pronoun की verb उसके antecedent के number/person के अनुसार होती है।

He is one of the boys who work hard.

She is the only one of the students who works hard.

The boys who are playing are my friends.

The boy who is playing is my brother.

17. Special SSC Rules

Rule – Mathematics / Physics / Economics

कुछ subjects दिखने में plural होते हैं लेकिन एक academic subject के रूप में singular verb लेते हैं।

Mathematics is an interesting subject.

Physics is difficult for some students.

Economics is an important subject.

News

The news is true.

❌ The news are true.

Politics

“Politics” context के अनुसार singular या plural sense में इस्तेमाल हो सकता है, लेकिन academic subject के रूप में singular verb common है।

Politics is an interesting subject.

Bread and Butter

जब दो nouns मिलकर एक idea या single item को represent करें, singular verb आ सकती है।

Bread and butter is my usual breakfast.

Plural-looking Countries / Names

The United States is a powerful country.

The United Nations has taken a decision.

18. Common Errors – SSC में सबसे ज्यादा पूछे जाने वाले

Incorrect Correct Rule
Each of the boys are ready. Each of the boys is ready. Each → singular
Neither of them are present. Neither of them is present. Neither → singular
Ram as well as his friends are coming. Ram as well as his friends is coming. First subject controls verb
A number of students is absent. A number of students are absent. A number → plural
The number of students are increasing. The number of students is increasing. The number → singular
One of the boys are absent. One of the boys is absent. One → singular
The scissors is sharp. The scissors are sharp. Scissors → plural
A pair of scissors are on the table. A pair of scissors is on the table. Pair → singular
There is many books. There are many books. Real subject = books
Mathematics are difficult. Mathematics is difficult. Subject name → singular

19. SSC Subject-Verb Agreement Tricks

🔥 Trick 1

Sentence में बीच में आने वाले words से confuse मत होना।

The boy with his friends is coming.

Main subject = boy.

🔥 Trick 2

as well as / along with / together with subject को plural नहीं बनाते।

🔥 Trick 3

Each / Every / Either / Neither → generally singular verb.

🔥 Trick 4

A number of → plural verb.

The number of → singular verb.

🔥 Trick 5

One of + plural noun → singular verb.

🔥 Trick 6

There is/are में verb real subject के अनुसार।

20. SSC PYQ-Based / Exam Pattern Practice

ये questions SSC में पूछे जाने वाले Subject-Verb Agreement concepts और common exam patterns पर आधारित practice हैं।

Question 1 SSC Pattern

Each of the students _____ a book.

A. have
B. has
C. are having
D. were
Answer: B. has

Each singular माना जाता है।

Question 2 SSC Pattern

A number of students _____ absent today.

A. is
B. was
C. are
D. has
Answer: C. are

A number of + plural noun → plural verb.

Question 3 SSC Pattern

The number of applicants _____ increasing every year.

A. are
B. were
C. is
D. have
Answer: C. is

The number → singular.

Question 4 SSC Pattern

Ram as well as his friends _____ coming.

A. are
B. is
C. were
D. have
Answer: B. is

Verb पहले subject “Ram” के अनुसार आएगी।

Question 5 SSC Pattern

Neither of the two boys _____ ready.

A. are
B. were
C. is
D. have
Answer: C. is

Neither → singular.

Question 6 SSC Pattern

One of my friends _____ in Delhi.

A. live
B. lives
C. are living
D. have lived
Answer: B. lives

Main subject = One.

Question 7 SSC Pattern

There _____ many books on the table.

A. is
B. are
C. was
D. has
Answer: B. are

Real subject = books, which is plural.

Question 8 SSC Pattern

Mathematics _____ an interesting subject.

A. are
B. were
C. is
D. have
Answer: C. is

Mathematics एक academic subject के रूप में singular है।

Error Detection Practice

Find the error:

Each of the players are ready for the match.

Error: are

Correct: Each of the players is ready for the match.

Find the error:

The number of students are increasing every year.

Error: are

Correct: The number of students is increasing every year.

Find the error:

Ram along with his friends are going to Delhi.

Error: are

Correct: Ram along with his friends is going to Delhi.
