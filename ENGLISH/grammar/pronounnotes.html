1. Pronouns क्या होते हैं?

Pronoun (सर्वनाम) वह शब्द है जो किसी noun (संज्ञा) की जगह प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Pronouns का use sentences को repeat होने से बचाता है और उन्हें more clear तथा concise बनाता है।

⭐ सबसे पहले यह याद रखें

Pronoun हमेशा उस noun (antecedent) को refer करता है जो पहले आया हो। Pronoun और antecedent में number, gender और person का agreement होना चाहिए।

Without Pronoun:
Rahul is a boy. Rahul is intelligent.

With Pronoun:
Rahul is a boy. He is intelligent.

यहाँ 'He' pronoun है जो 'Rahul' को refer कर रहा है।

2. Types of Pronouns

Type Examples Use
Personal I, you, he, she, it, we, they व्यक्ति या वस्तु के लिए
Possessive mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs ownership दिखाने के लिए
Reflexive myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, themselves verb का action subject पर वापस आता है
Demonstrative this, that, these, those specific noun को point करने के लिए
Interrogative who, whom, whose, which, what questions पूछने के लिए
Relative who, whom, whose, which, that clauses को join करने के लिए
Indefinite someone, anyone, everyone, nobody, all, some, none non-specific person/thing के लिए
Distributive each, either, neither group के individual members के लिए
Reciprocal each other, one another mutual relationship के लिए

3. Personal Pronouns

Personal pronouns व्यक्ति या वस्तु के लिए प्रयोग होते हैं। ये तीन persons में आते हैं:

Person Subjective (Nominative) Objective (Accusative) Possessive (Determiner)
First Person Singular I me my
Second Person Singular/Plural you you your
Third Person Singular (Male) he him his
Third Person Singular (Female) she her her
Third Person Singular (Neutral) it it its
First Person Plural we us our
Third Person Plural they them their

Subjective Case (कर्ता)

I am a teacher.

He goes to school.

We are playing cricket.

They have finished their work.

Objective Case (कर्म)

She gave the book to me.

The teacher called him.

We saw them at the park.

4. Possessive Pronouns

Possessive pronouns ownership या relation दिखाते हैं। ये noun के बिना आते हैं (unlike possessive adjectives).

Possessive Adjective (with noun) Possessive Pronoun (without noun)
This is my book. This book is mine.
That is your pen. That pen is yours.
It is his car. The car is his.
These are her shoes. These shoes are hers.
This is our house. This house is ours.
Those are their bags. Those bags are theirs.
Exam Alert

'Its' और 'It's' में confusion न करें।
Its = possessive (uski)
It's = it is / it has

❌ The dog wagged it's tail.
✅ The dog wagged its tail.

5. Reflexive Pronouns

Reflexive pronouns तब प्रयोग होते हैं जब subject और object एक ही व्यक्ति/वस्तु हो। ये verb के action को subject पर वापस लाते हैं।

Subject Reflexive Pronoun
I myself
You (singular) yourself
He himself
She herself
It itself
We ourselves
You (plural) yourselves
They themselves

I hurt myself.

He cut himself while shaving.

She taught herself to play piano.

They enjoyed themselves at the party.

Emphasis के लिए (Emphatic)

I myself saw the accident.

The Prime Minister himself visited the site.

Common Error

❌ He hurt hisself.
✅ He hurt himself.

❌ They enjoyed theirselves.
✅ They enjoyed themselves.

6. Demonstrative Pronouns

Demonstrative pronouns किसी specific noun को point (indicate) करने के लिए प्रयोग होते हैं।

Proximity Singular Plural
Near (close) this these
Far (distant) that those

This is my book.

These are my friends.

That is a beautiful house.

Those are expensive cars.

Exam Alert

'This/These' का प्रयोग निकट (near) की चीज़ों के लिए, 'That/Those' का प्रयोग दूर (far) की चीज़ों के लिए।

7. Interrogative Pronouns

Interrogative pronouns questions पूछने के लिए प्रयोग होते हैं।

Pronoun Use Example
who subject (person) Who is coming?
whom object (person) Whom did you meet?
whose possession Whose book is this?
which selection (person/thing) Which is your bag?
what thing/idea What is your name?

Who broke the window?

Whom are you waiting for?

Whose car is parked outside?

Which color do you prefer?

What happened yesterday?

8. Relative Pronouns

Relative pronouns दो clauses (independent + dependent) को join करते हैं और dependent clause को main clause से relate करते हैं।

Pronoun Used for Example
who people (subject) The man who called you is my brother.
whom people (object) The woman whom you met is my aunt.
whose possession (people/things) The boy whose bag was lost is crying.
which things / animals The book which I read was interesting.
that people or things (restrictive clauses) The car that I bought is red.

Who vs. Whom

⭐ SSC Favourite Rule

Who = subject (कर्ता)
Whom = object (कर्म)

Trick: यदि 'he/she/they' से replace कर सकते हैं → who
यदि 'him/her/them' से replace कर सकते हैं → whom

Who is at the door? (He is at the door.)

Whom did you invite? (I invited him.)

The man who stole the watch was caught. (He stole the watch.)

The man whom I saw was tall. (I saw him.)

9. Indefinite Pronouns

Indefinite pronouns non-specific people, things, or amounts को refer करते हैं।

Type Examples
Singular anyone, anyone, someone, no one, everybody, everything, each, either, neither, nothing
Plural both, few, many, several, others
Singular or Plural all, some, none, any, more, most

Everyone is invited to the party.

Somebody left their umbrella here.

Many are called, but few are chosen.

All are welcome.

None of the students was present.

Exam Alert

Indefinite pronouns like everyone, each, either, neither, anyone are singular – they take singular verbs.

✅ Everyone is happy.
❌ Everyone are happy.

✅ Each of the boys has a pen.
❌ Each of the boys have a pen.

10. Distributive Pronouns

Distributive pronouns group के individual members को अलग-अलग refer करते हैं।

Pronoun Meaning Example
each हर एक (individually) Each of the students received a prize.
either दो में से कोई एक Either of the two roads leads to the station.
neither दो में से कोई नहीं Neither of the answers was correct.
⭐ Important Rule

Each, either, neither हमेशा singular होते हैं और singular verb लेते हैं।

Each boy has his own room.

Either of the two is acceptable.

Neither of the players was selected.

11. Reciprocal Pronouns

Reciprocal pronouns mutual relationship (आपसी संबंध) दिखाते हैं।

Pronoun Meaning Example
each other दो व्यक्तियों के बीच The two friends helped each other.
one another दो से अधिक व्यक्तियों के बीच The team members encouraged one another.

They hugged each other.

The students shared their notes with one another.

12. Cases of Pronouns

Case Definition Examples
Subjective (Nominative) Subject of the verb I, you, he, she, it, we, they
Objective (Accusative) Object of the verb or preposition me, you, him, her, it, us, them
Possessive Ownership / relation mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs

Subjective Case Examples

He is my friend.

She and I went to the market.

They have completed the project.

Objective Case Examples

The teacher called him.

I gave the book to her.

Please listen to them.

Possessive Case Examples

This pen is mine.

The car is theirs.

That house is ours.

13. Common Errors – SSC

Incorrect Correct Reason
He is older than me. He is older than I. 'Than' के बाद subjective case (I)
She is taller than him. She is taller than he. 'Than' के बाद subjective case (he)
Let he go. Let him go. 'Let' के बाद objective case (him)
Between you and I. Between you and me. Preposition 'between' के बाद objective case (me)
He hurt hisself. He hurt himself. Correct reflexive pronoun is 'himself'
Everyone are happy. Everyone is happy. 'Everyone' is singular
It's tail is long. Its tail is long. Possessive is 'its' (without apostrophe)
The man who I met. The man whom I met. 'Whom' because it's object of 'met'
Neither of the boys were present. Neither of the boys was present. 'Neither' is singular

14. SSC Pronouns Tricks

🔥 Trick 1 – Subjective vs Objective

Subject (कर्ता) → I, he, she, we, they
Object (कर्म) → me, him, her, us, them

🔥 Trick 2 – Who vs Whom

He/She → Who
Him/Her → Whom

🔥 Trick 3 – Singular Indefinites

Everyone, each, either, neither → singular verb

🔥 Trick 4 – Reflexive Pronouns

Myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, themselves

❌ hisself, theirselves → never use

🔥 Trick 5 – Preposition के बाद Objective

Between me, for him, with them

🔥 Trick 6 – Possessive vs Contraction

Its = possessive
It's = it is / it has

Their = possessive
They're = they are

15. SSC PYQ-Based / Exam Pattern Practice

Question 1 SSC CGL 2022

Choose the correct pronoun:

Between you and _____, I don't trust him.

A. I
B. me
C. myself
D. mine
Answer: B. me

Preposition 'between' के बाद objective case (me) आता है।

Question 2 SSC CHSL 2021

Choose the correct pronoun:

He is smarter than _____.

A. me
B. I
C. myself
D. mine
Answer: B. I

'Than' के बाद subjective case आता है। (He is smarter than I [am].)

Question 3 SSC CPO 2022

Choose the correct pronoun:

The girl _____ won the prize is my sister.

A. whom
B. who
C. which
D. whose
Answer: B. who

'Won' का subject 'the girl' है, इसलिए relative pronoun 'who' (subject)।

Question 4 SSC MTS 2022

Choose the correct pronoun:

Everyone _____ invited to the party.

A. are
B. is
C. were
D. have been
Answer: B. is

'Everyone' singular है, इसलिए singular verb 'is'।

Question 5 SSC CGL 2023

Choose the correct pronoun:

The man _____ you met yesterday is my uncle.

A. who
B. whom
C. whose
D. which
Answer: B. whom

'You met' में 'met' का object 'the man' है, इसलिए 'whom' (objective)।

Question 6 SSC CHSL 2023

Choose the correct pronoun:

Neither of the two students _____ eligible for the scholarship.

A. is
B. are
C. were
D. have
Answer: A. is

'Neither' singular है, इसलिए singular verb 'is'।

Question 7 SSC CPO 2023

Choose the correct pronoun:

She hurt _____ while playing.

A. herself
B. hisself
C. herself
D. herself
Answer: A. herself

'She' का reflexive pronoun 'herself' है।

Question 8 SSC MTS 2023

Choose the correct pronoun:

These books are _____.

A. our
B. ours
C. us
D. we
Answer: B. ours

Possessive pronoun (without noun) 'ours' का प्रयोग हुआ है।

16. Quick Revision – Pronouns

Rule 1 Pronoun → noun की जगह
Rule 2 Subjective: I, he, she, we, they
Rule 3 Objective: me, him, her, us, them
Rule 4 Possessive: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs
Rule 5 Reflexive: myself, himself, herself, themselves
Rule 6 Who = subject, Whom = object
Rule 7 Everyone, each, either, neither → singular
Rule 8 Between + objective case
Rule 9 Its (possessive) vs It's (it is)
Rule 10 Each other (2), One another (3+)
🎯 SSC Pronouns Final Checklist
  • Pronoun का antecedent clear होना चाहिए।
  • Number, gender, person का agreement mandatory है।
  • Subjective case (I, he, she, we, they) – कर्ता के लिए।
  • Objective case (me, him, her, us, them) – कर्म / preposition के बाद।
  • Possessive pronouns (mine, yours, etc.) – noun के बिना।
  • Reflexive pronouns (myself, himself, etc.) – subject को वापस।
  • Who (subject), Whom (object) – याद रखें।
  • Everyone, each, either, neither – always singular।
  • Its (possessive) vs It's (it is) – confusion न करें।
  • Each other (two), one another (more than two)।
