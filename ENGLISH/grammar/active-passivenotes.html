1. Active & Passive Voice क्या होता है?

Voice यह बताता है कि sentence में action करने वाला कौन है और action किस पर हो रहा है।

⭐ सबसे पहले यह याद रखें

Active Voice में Subject action करता है।

Passive Voice में Object पर action होता है।

Active: Ram writes a letter.

राम एक पत्र लिखता है।

Passive: A letter is written by Ram.

एक पत्र राम द्वारा लिखा जाता है।

2. Active Voice

जिस sentence में Subject स्वयं action करता है, उसे Active Voice कहते हैं।

Basic Structure

Subject + Verb + Object

Ram reads a book.

She writes a letter.

They play cricket.

The boy opened the door.

3. Passive Voice

जिस sentence में action का effect Object पर होता है, उसे Passive Voice कहते हैं।

Basic Structure

Object + Helping Verb + V3 + by + Subject

Active: Ram writes a letter.

Passive: A letter is written by Ram.

Active: She cleaned the room.

Passive: The room was cleaned by her.

4. Active और Passive Voice में Difference

Active Voice Passive Voice
Subject important होता है। Object important हो जाता है।
Subject action करता है। Subject पर action होता है।
Main verb सामान्य form में होती है। Main verb की V3 form आती है।
Ram writes a letter. A letter is written by Ram.

5. Active से Passive बनाने का Basic Rule

⭐ SSC का सबसे Important Formula

Active का Object → Passive का Subject

Active का Subject → Passive में by + Object Form

Main Verb → V3

Active: Ram writes a letter.

Object = a letter

Subject = Ram

Passive: A letter is written by Ram.

Step-by-Step Method

Step 1: Object पहचानो।

Step 2: Object को sentence के beginning में लाओ।

Step 3: Tense के अनुसार helping verb लगाओ।

Step 4: Main verb की V3 form लगाओ।

Step 5: आवश्यकता होने पर by + subject लगाओ।

6. Passive Voice में Pronoun Changes

Active Subject Passive Object Example
I me by me
We us by us
You you by you
He him by him
She her by her
They them by them
It it by it
Who whom by whom

He helped me.

I was helped by him.

They invited her.

She was invited by them.

7. Present Simple Tense

Active Structure

Subject + V1/V5 + Object

Passive Structure

Object + is/am/are + V3 + by + Subject

Active: Ram writes a letter.

Passive: A letter is written by Ram.

Active: They play cricket.

Passive: Cricket is played by them.

Active: She teaches English.

Passive: English is taught by her.

Negative

Active: Ram does not write a letter.

Passive: A letter is not written by Ram.

Interrogative

Active: Does Ram write a letter?

Passive: Is a letter written by Ram?

8. Present Continuous Tense

Active

Subject + is/am/are + V-ing + Object

Passive

Object + is/am/are + being + V3 + by + Subject

Active: Ram is writing a letter.

Passive: A letter is being written by Ram.

Active: They are repairing the road.

Passive: The road is being repaired by them.

Active: She is preparing food.

Passive: Food is being prepared by her.

9. Present Perfect Tense

Active

Subject + has/have + V3 + Object

Passive

Object + has/have + been + V3 + by + Subject

Active: Ram has written a letter.

Passive: A letter has been written by Ram.

Active: They have completed the work.

Passive: The work has been completed by them.

Active: She has cleaned the room.

Passive: The room has been cleaned by her.

10. Past Simple Tense

Active

Subject + V2 + Object

Passive

Object + was/were + V3 + by + Subject

Active: Ram wrote a letter.

Passive: A letter was written by Ram.

Active: They cleaned the room.

Passive: The room was cleaned by them.

Active: She bought a book.

Passive: A book was bought by her.

Negative

Active: Ram did not write a letter.

Passive: A letter was not written by Ram.

11. Past Continuous Tense

Active

Subject + was/were + V-ing + Object

Passive

Object + was/were + being + V3 + by + Subject

Active: Ram was writing a letter.

Passive: A letter was being written by Ram.

Active: They were repairing the road.

Passive: The road was being repaired by them.

12. Past Perfect Tense

Active

Subject + had + V3 + Object

Passive

Object + had + been + V3 + by + Subject

Active: Ram had completed the work.

Passive: The work had been completed by Ram.

Active: She had written a letter.

Passive: A letter had been written by her.

13. Future Simple Tense

Active

Subject + will + V1 + Object

Passive

Object + will + be + V3 + by + Subject

Active: Ram will write a letter.

Passive: A letter will be written by Ram.

Active: They will complete the work.

Passive: The work will be completed by them.

Active: She will prepare the food.

Passive: The food will be prepared by her.

14. Future Perfect Tense

Active

Subject + will have + V3 + Object

Passive

Object + will have been + V3 + by + Subject

Active: Ram will have completed the work.

Passive: The work will have been completed by Ram.

Active: They will have finished the project.

Passive: The project will have been finished by them.

Exam Alert

Future Continuous का standard passive सामान्यतः competitive-exam grammar में नहीं बनाया जाता।

Tense-wise Passive Voice Formula

Tense Passive Structure Example
Present Simple is/am/are + V3 The work is done.
Present Continuous is/am/are + being + V3 The work is being done.
Present Perfect has/have + been + V3 The work has been done.
Past Simple was/were + V3 The work was done.
Past Continuous was/were + being + V3 The work was being done.
Past Perfect had + been + V3 The work had been done.
Future Simple will + be + V3 The work will be done.
Future Perfect will have been + V3 The work will have been done.

15. Modal Verbs के साथ Passive Voice

Can, could, may, might, must, should, ought to, have to, has to, etc. के साथ passive voice का बहुत important pattern होता है।

Golden Rule

Modal + be + V3

Active: Ram can do the work.

Passive: The work can be done by Ram.

Active: You should obey the rules.

Passive: The rules should be obeyed by you.

Active: We must follow the instructions.

Passive: The instructions must be followed by us.

Could

The police could arrest the thief.

The thief could be arrested by the police.

May / Might

They may postpone the meeting.

The meeting may be postponed by them.

Have to / Has to / Had to

have to + V1 → have to be + V3

has to + V1 → has to be + V3

had to + V1 → had to be + V3

We have to complete the work.

The work has to be completed by us.

He had to finish the task.

The task had to be finished by him.

16. Imperative Sentences का Passive

Imperative sentence में command, order, request, advice या instruction दी जाती है।

Basic Passive Formula

Let + Object + be + V3

Active: Open the door.

Passive: Let the door be opened.

Active: Close the window.

Passive: Let the window be closed.

Active: Read the book.

Passive: Let the book be read.

Negative Imperative

Active: Do not open the door.

Passive: Let the door not be opened.

Active: Do not waste time.

Passive: Let time not be wasted.

Request / Advice

Active: Please help me.

Passive: You are requested to help me.

Active: Please keep silence.

Passive: You are requested to keep silence.

17. Interrogative Sentences का Passive

Question sentence को passive में बदलते समय helping verb sentence के beginning में रहती है।

Yes / No Questions

Active: Does Ram write a letter?

Passive: Is a letter written by Ram?

Active: Did she clean the room?

Passive: Was the room cleaned by her?

Active: Has he completed the work?

Passive: Has the work been completed by him?

Modal Questions

Active: Can you solve this problem?

Passive: Can this problem be solved by you?

Active: Should we follow the rules?

Passive: Should the rules be followed by us?

18. WH-Questions का Passive

Who, whom, what, which, when, why, how आदि से शुरू होने वाले questions को passive में बदलते समय WH-word को सामान्यतः beginning में रखा जाता है।

Active: Who wrote this book?

Passive: By whom was this book written?

Active: Who teaches you English?

Passive: By whom are you taught English?

Active: What did Ram buy?

Passive: What was bought by Ram?

⭐ SSC Trick

Active में Who subject हो, तो passive में अक्सर By whom आता है।

19. Two Objects वाले Sentences

कुछ verbs जैसे give, send, tell, teach, offer, show आदि दो objects लेते हैं।

Active: Ram gave me a book.

यहाँ:

me = Indirect Object

a book = Direct Object

ऐसे sentences के दो passive forms संभव हो सकते हैं।

I was given a book by Ram.

A book was given to me by Ram.

Active: She teaches us English.

Passive: We are taught English by her.

Passive: English is taught to us by her.

20. Prepositional Verbs

कुछ verbs के साथ preposition fixed होती है। Passive बनाते समय preposition को हटाया नहीं जाता।

Active: Everyone laughed at him.

Passive: He was laughed at by everyone.

Active: They looked after the child.

Passive: The child was looked after by them.

Active: We should listen to the teacher.

Passive: The teacher should be listened to by us.

Exam Alert

Preposition को passive में बिना reason के remove मत करो।

21. Passive में “BY” का Use

Passive voice में active sentence के subject को बताने के लिए सामान्यतः by का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Ram wrote a letter.

A letter was written by Ram.

She cleaned the room.

The room was cleaned by her.

“By” हमेशा जरूरी नहीं है

यदि action करने वाला व्यक्ति unknown, obvious या unimportant हो, तो passive sentence में by + subject हटाया जा सकता है।

Someone stole my bicycle.

My bicycle was stolen.

People speak English all over the world.

English is spoken all over the world.

🔥 SSC Trick

Passive sentence में by देखकर यह मत मानो कि हर बार by लगाना compulsory है।

22. किन Sentences का Passive नहीं बनता?

Passive voice बनाने के लिए generally object होना आवश्यक होता है।

Intransitive Verb

जिन verbs के साथ object नहीं होता, उनका सामान्यतः passive नहीं बनाया जाता।

He sleeps.

She laughed.

The baby cried.

They arrived.

इन sentences में direct object नहीं है, इसलिए सामान्य passive conversion नहीं किया जाता।

Common Intransitive Verbs

sleep

arrive

come

go

die

laugh

cry

happen

23. Common Errors – SSC

Incorrect Correct Reason
A letter is write by Ram. A letter is written by Ram. Passive में V3 चाहिए।
The work is completed by him yesterday. The work was completed by him yesterday. Yesterday → Past tense.
The road is being repair. The road is being repaired. being + V3.
The work has been complete. The work has been completed. has been + V3.
The letter was wrote by him. The letter was written by him. Passive में V3.
The work will completed tomorrow. The work will be completed tomorrow. will + be + V3.
The book is being read by she. The book is being read by her. by के बाद object pronoun.
The rules should followed. The rules should be followed. Modal + be + V3.
The door was opened by he. The door was opened by him. by + object form.
Let the door be open. Let the door be opened. Passive में V3 चाहिए।

24. SSC Active-Passive Tricks

🔥 Trick 1 – Object ढूँढो

सबसे पहले Active sentence का object पहचानो।

वही Passive sentence का subject बनेगा।

🔥 Trick 2 – Passive में V3

Passive Voice का सबसे important rule: Main Verb हमेशा V3 form में।

write → written

do → done

eat → eaten

🔥 Trick 3 – Continuous

Continuous Passive → being + V3

is writing → is being written

was writing → was being written

🔥 Trick 4 – Perfect

Perfect Passive → been + V3

has written → has been written

had written → had been written

🔥 Trick 5 – Modal

Modal Passive → Modal + be + V3

can do → can be done

should follow → should be followed

🔥 Trick 6 – Imperative

Command का common passive:

Let + Object + be + V3

🔥 Trick 7 – Pronoun

He → him

She → her

They → them

We → us

I → me

25. SSC PYQ-Based / Exam Pattern Practice

Question 1 SSC Pattern

Ram writes a letter.

Choose the correct passive voice.

A. A letter was written by Ram.
B. A letter is written by Ram.
C. A letter is wrote by Ram.
D. A letter has written by Ram.
Answer: B. A letter is written by Ram.

Present Simple का passive: is/am/are + V3.

Question 2 SSC Pattern

She is writing a letter.

A. A letter is written by her.
B. A letter was being written by her.
C. A letter is being written by her.
D. A letter has been written by her.
Answer: C. A letter is being written by her.

Present Continuous Passive → is/am/are + being + V3.

Question 3 SSC Pattern

They have completed the work.

A. The work has completed by them.
B. The work has been completed by them.
C. The work was completed by them.
D. The work is being completed by them.
Answer: B. The work has been completed by them.

Present Perfect Passive → has/have + been + V3.

Question 4 SSC Pattern

Ram wrote a letter.

A. A letter is written by Ram.
B. A letter was written by Ram.
C. A letter has been written by Ram.
D. A letter was being written by Ram.
Answer: B. A letter was written by Ram.

Past Simple Passive → was/were + V3.

Question 5 SSC Pattern

They were repairing the road.

A. The road was repaired by them.
B. The road was being repaired by them.
C. The road is being repaired by them.
D. The road had been repaired by them.
Answer: B. The road was being repaired by them.

Past Continuous Passive → was/were + being + V3.

Question 6 SSC Pattern

Ram had completed the work.

A. The work has been completed by Ram.
B. The work had been completed by Ram.
C. The work was completed by Ram.
D. The work is completed by Ram.
Answer: B. The work had been completed by Ram.

Past Perfect Passive → had + been + V3.

Question 7 SSC Pattern

Ram will complete the work.

A. The work will complete by Ram.
B. The work will be completed by Ram.
C. The work would be completed by Ram.
D. The work is completed by Ram.
Answer: B. The work will be completed by Ram.

Future Simple Passive → will + be + V3.

Question 8 SSC Pattern

You should obey the rules.

A. The rules should obeyed by you.
B. The rules should be obeyed by you.
C. The rules should been obeyed by you.
D. The rules are obeyed by you.
Answer: B. The rules should be obeyed by you.

Modal Passive → modal + be + V3.

Question 9 SSC Pattern

Open the door.

A. Let the door opened.
B. Let the door be opened.
C. The door is opened.
D. The door was opened.
Answer: B. Let the door be opened.

Imperative Passive → Let + object + be + V3.

Question 10 SSC Pattern

Who wrote this book?

A. Who was this book written?
B. By whom was this book written?
C. By who this book was written?
D. This book was written who?
Answer: B. By whom was this book written?

Who जब Active में subject होता है, तो Passive में अक्सर By whom आता है।

Question 11 SSC Pattern

Can you solve this problem?

A. Can this problem solved by you?
B. Can this problem be solved by you?
C. Is this problem solved by you?
D. Could this problem be solved by you?
Answer: B. Can this problem be solved by you?

Can + be + V3.

Question 12 SSC Pattern

The teacher teaches us English.

A. We are taught English by the teacher.
B. We were taught English by the teacher.
C. English was taught us by the teacher.
D. We have been taught English by the teacher.
Answer: A. We are taught English by the teacher.

Present Simple में is/am/are + V3 आता है।
