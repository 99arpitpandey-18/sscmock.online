⏳ 01. Tenses

Time of an action ko correctly express karne ke liye Tenses sabse important grammar topics mein se ek hai.

1. What is a Tense?

Tense verb ke through ye batata hai ki koi action present, past ya future mein hua, ho raha hai ya hoga.

Three Main Times
  • Present – वर्तमान
  • Past – भूतकाल
  • Future – भविष्य

2. Four Forms of Each Tense

Form Meaning Example
Simple General action / fact I play cricket.
Continuous Action in progress I am playing cricket.
Perfect Completed action I have played cricket.
Perfect Continuous Action continuing for a duration I have been playing cricket for two hours.

3. Present Simple Tense

Subject + V1 / V1+s/es + Object

Uses

  • Habitual actions
  • Universal truths
  • General facts
  • Regular activities
  • Timetables and schedules
Examples:

He goes to school every day.

The sun rises in the east.

Water boils at 100°C.

Important Rule:

He, She, It ya singular noun ke saath generally verb mein s/es lagta hai.

He plays cricket.

She teaches English.

4. Present Continuous Tense

Subject + is/am/are + V1-ing + Object

Present moment par chal rahe action ke liye use hota hai.

I am studying English.

They are playing football.

She is reading a book.

5. Present Perfect Tense

Subject + has/have + V3 + Object

Aisa action jo complete ho chuka hai aur jiska connection present se hai.

I have completed my work.

She has finished the book.

6. Present Perfect Continuous

Subject + has/have + been + V1-ing + since/for

Past mein start hua action jo abhi tak continue kar raha hai.

I have been studying for two hours.

She has been living here since 2020.

7. Past Simple

Subject + V2 + Object

I visited Delhi yesterday.

She completed her work.

8. Past Continuous

Subject + was/were + V1-ing

I was studying when he called.

They were playing football.

9. Past Perfect

Subject + had + V3

Past ke do actions mein jo action pehle complete hua ho, uske liye Past Perfect use hota hai.

The train had left before we reached the station.

10. Past Perfect Continuous

Subject + had been + V1-ing + since/for

He had been working for five hours before he took a break.

11. Future Simple

Subject + will + V1

I will help you.

She will come tomorrow.

12. Future Continuous

Subject + will be + V1-ing

This time tomorrow, I will be travelling.

13. Future Perfect

Subject + will have + V3

I will have completed the work by Monday.

14. Future Perfect Continuous

Subject + will have been + V1-ing + since/for

By next month, he will have been working here for five years.

15. Since vs For

Since For
Starting point Duration
Since Monday For two days
Since 2020 For five years
⚠ Common SSC Trap

Since ke baad duration nahi, balki starting point aata hai.

❌ Since two years

✅ For two years

16. Tense Common Errors

❌ He go to school every day.

✅ He goes to school every day.


❌ I am knowing him.

✅ I know him.


❌ She has gone yesterday.

✅ She went yesterday.

👥 02. Subject-Verb Agreement

Subject aur verb ke number/person ke according correct agreement.

1. Basic Rule

Singular subject ke saath singular verb aur plural subject ke saath plural verb use hota hai.

He plays.

They play.

2. He / She / It Rule

Simple Present mein He, She, It ke saath main verb mein generally s/es lagta hai.

He works hard.

She teaches English.

It rains heavily.

3. And

Do separate subjects ko and join kare to usually plural verb use hota hai.

Ram and Shyam are friends.

4. Each / Every

Each aur Every ke baad singular noun aur singular verb generally use hota hai.

Each student has a book.

Every boy is present.

5. Either / Neither

Either aur Neither generally singular construction ke saath use hote hain.

Neither boy is ready.

Either answer is acceptable.

6. Along With / As Well As

In phrases ke baad aane wala noun verb ko control nahi karta. Verb first subject ke according hota hai.

The teacher, along with the students, is present.

7. Collective Nouns

Team, committee, family, jury, class jaise words context ke according singular ya plural sense de sakte hain.

The team is winning.

8. Common Errors

❌ Each students are ready.

✅ Each student is ready.

❌ The teacher along with his students are present.

✅ The teacher along with his students is present.

🅰️ 03. Articles

A, An aur The ke complete concepts.

1. What are Articles?

Articles noun ke before use hone wale words hain. English mein mainly A, An, The use hote hain.

2. A

A consonant sound se pehle generally use hota hai.

a boy

a book

a university

Sound important hai, spelling nahi.

University ka pronunciation "yu" sound se start hota hai, isliye a university.

3. An

Vowel sound se pehle an use hota hai.

an apple

an honest man

an hour

Honest aur hour mein initial h sound silent hai.

4. The

The kisi specific ya already known noun ko indicate karta hai.

The book on the table is mine.

The sun rises in the east.

5. Superlative Degree

Superlative degree ke saath generally the use hota hai.

He is the best player.

6. Unique Things

the sun

the moon

the earth

7. Zero Article

General sense mein plural aur uncountable nouns ke saath article omit ho sakta hai.

Books are useful.

Milk is healthy.

Common Errors

❌ He is an university student.

✅ He is a university student.

❌ He is a honest man.

✅ He is an honest man.

📍 04. Prepositions

Time, place, direction aur fixed expressions ke important rules.

1. What is a Preposition?

Preposition noun/pronoun ka relationship kisi doosre word ke saath show karta hai.

The book is on the table.

2. In / On / At – Time

Preposition Usage Example
At Exact time at 5 PM
On Day / Date on Monday
In Month / Year / Long period in 2026

3. In / On / At – Place

in the room

on the table

at the station

4. Since / For

Since = starting point

For = duration

5. Between / Among

Between generally two people/things ke relation mein use hota hai. Among generally more than two ke liye use hota hai.

The money was divided between Ram and Shyam.

The prize was divided among the students.

6. Beside / Besides

Beside Besides
Next to In addition to
He sat beside me. Besides English, he knows Hindi.

7. Common Errors

❌ He is good in English.

✅ He is good at English.

❌ She is married with a doctor.

✅ She is married to a doctor.

🔄 05. Active & Passive Voice

Voice transformation ke fundamental rules aur structures.

1. Active Voice

Active voice mein subject action perform karta hai.

Ram writes a letter.

2. Passive Voice

Passive voice mein focus action/object par hota hai.

A letter is written by Ram.

3. Basic Transformation

Active: Subject + Verb + Object Passive: Object + Helping Verb + V3 + by + Subject

4. Simple Present

Active: Subject + V1 + Object Passive: Object + is/am/are + V3 + by + Subject

Ram writes a letter.

A letter is written by Ram.

5. Present Continuous

Object + is/am/are + being + V3

Ram is writing a letter.

A letter is being written by Ram.

6. Present Perfect

Object + has/have + been + V3

She has completed the work.

The work has been completed by her.

7. Simple Past

Object + was/were + V3

He wrote a letter.

A letter was written by him.

8. Modal Verbs

Object + modal + be + V3

You can solve the problem.

The problem can be solved by you.

9. Imperative Sentences

Order/request/advice ko passive mein convert karte waqt appropriate structure use kiya jata hai.

Open the door.

Let the door be opened.

Important:

Passive voice mein main verb ka V3 form use hota hai.

💬 06. Direct & Indirect Speech

Reported speech aur narration transformation ke important rules.

1. Direct Speech

Speaker ke exact words quotation marks mein diye jaate hain.

He said, "I am busy."

2. Indirect Speech

Speaker ke words ko reporting form mein express kiya jata hai.

He said that he was busy.

3. Basic Changes

Direct Indirect
am/is was
are were
was/were had been
has/have had
will would
can could
may might

4. Pronoun Changes

Pronoun reporting situation aur speaker/listener ke relationship ke according change hota hai.

5. Time and Place Changes

Direct Indirect
today that day
tomorrow the next day
yesterday the previous day
now then
here there

6. Statements

Statement mein generally reporting verb ke baad that use kiya ja sakta hai.

He said, "I am tired."

He said that he was tired.

7. Questions

Yes/No question mein generally if/whether use hota hai.

He said, "Are you ready?"

He asked if I was ready.

8. Commands and Requests

Command/request mein reporting verb situation ke according change hota hai, aur infinitive structure use kiya ja sakta hai.

The teacher said to me, "Open the book."

The teacher told me to open the book.

🔗 07. Conjunctions

Words aur clauses ko connect karne ke rules.

1. What is a Conjunction?

Conjunction words, phrases ya clauses ko connect karta hai.

Ram and Shyam are friends.

2. Coordinating Conjunctions

Common examples: and, but, or, nor, for, yet, so

And – addition

But – contrast

Or – choice

So – result

3. Subordinating Conjunctions

because, although, if, unless, when, while, before, after etc.

I stayed home because it was raining.

4. Correlative Conjunctions

Pairs mein use hone wale conjunctions.

  • Either...or
  • Neither...nor
  • Both...and
  • Not only...but also
  • Whether...or

5. Either...Or

Either Ram or Shyam is responsible.

6. Neither...Nor

Neither Ram nor Shyam is present.

7. Although / Though

Although aur though contrast show karte hain.

Although he was tired, he continued working.

SSC Trap:

Although ke saath generally but ko unnecessarily use nahi karte.

❌ Although he is poor but he is honest.

✅ Although he is poor, he is honest.

👤 08. Pronouns

Pronouns ke types, usage aur common SSC errors.

1. What is a Pronoun?

Pronoun noun ke place par use hota hai.

Rahul is a student. He is intelligent.

2. Personal Pronouns

Subject Object Possessive
I me my/mine
He him his
She her her/hers
They them their/theirs

3. Reflexive Pronouns

myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves.

He hurt himself.

4. Demonstrative Pronouns

this, that, these, those.

This is my book.

Those are my shoes.

5. Relative Pronouns

who, whom, whose, which, that.

The boy who won the race is my friend.

6. Who vs Whom

Who generally subject ke liye use hota hai.

Whom generally object ke liye use hota hai.

7. Each Other / One Another

Traditional grammar mein each other generally two persons aur one another more than two ke liye explain kiya jata hai.

8. Common Errors

❌ Between you and I

✅ Between you and me

❌ He himself did it by his own.

✅ He himself did it.

⚡ Quick Revision

Exam se pehle important grammar points ko quickly revise karo.

🔥 Important Rules

  1. He/She/It ke saath Simple Present mein generally V1+s/es.
  2. Since = starting point, For = duration.
  3. Each/Every ke saath generally singular noun aur singular verb.
  4. A consonant sound se pehle, An vowel sound se pehle.
  5. Superlative degree ke saath generally The.
  6. Passive Voice mein main verb V3 hota hai.
  7. Modal passive structure: modal + be + V3.
  8. Either...or / Neither...nor mein verb agreement carefully check karo.
  9. Although ke saath unnecessary "but" avoid karo.
  10. Between two aur Among multiple ke basic distinction ko yaad rakho.

⚠️ Common Errors

❌ He go to school.

✅ He goes to school.

❌ Each students are present.

✅ Each student is present.

❌ He is an university student.

✅ He is a university student.

❌ She is married with him.

✅ She is married to him.

❌ Although he is poor but he is honest.

✅ Although he is poor, he is honest.

❌ Between you and I.

✅ Between you and me.
