🏆 SSC CGL – Complete Premium Guide

Combined Graduate Level Examination · Group B & C Posts

🎓 Graduate Level
🎓QualificationGraduation (post-wise)
👤AgePost-wise · 18–32 yrs
💰SalaryLevel 4 to 8
💻Exam ModeComputer Based
📊StagesTier I + Tier II
🏛️PostsASO, Inspector, Auditor, etc.

📌 About CGL

SSC CGL is a major graduate-level recruitment for Group B & C posts in Central Government departments, ministries and organizations.

🎓 Qualification

  • Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university.
  • Some posts require specific subjects (e.g., Statistics, Mathematics).
  • Verify post-wise eligibility before applying.

👤 Age Limit

Post-wise age limits vary. Category-wise relaxation as per Government rules.

📌 Always check the latest CGL notification for exact age criteria.

💼 Posts & Career

  • Assistant Section Officer (ASO)
  • Inspector (Income Tax / Customs / etc.)
  • Auditor / Accountant
  • Tax Assistant
  • Other Group B & C posts

💰 Salary & Pay Level

Salary depends on the specific post and department. Basic pay ranges from Level 4 (₹25,500+) to Level 8 (₹47,600+) with DA, HRA and other allowances.

📝 Selection Process

StageModePurpose
Tier IComputer BasedScreening / Merit
Tier IIComputer BasedAdvanced assessment + post-specific papers

📚 CGL Syllabus

🔢 Quantitative Aptitude

Number System, Percentage, Ratio, Profit & Loss, Average, Time & Work, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry, Statistics, Data Interpretation.

📖 English

Grammar, Vocabulary, Error Detection, Sentence Improvement, Fill in the Blanks, Comprehension, Synonyms, Antonyms.

🧠 Reasoning

Analogy, Classification, Series, Coding-Decoding, Logical Reasoning, Non-Verbal, Spatial Ability.

🌍 General Awareness

History, Geography, Polity, Economy, General Science, Current Affairs, Culture.

📌 Verify Official Notification

🔗 SSC Official Portal → Check latest CGL notification PDF for exact vacancies, post-wise eligibility and exam dates.

📝 SSC CHSL – Complete Premium Guide

Combined Higher Secondary Level Examination

📌 About CHSL

  • 12th / Higher Secondary pass from a recognized board.
  • Some posts may have additional subject requirements.

👤 Age

  • Data Entry Operator
  • Postal / Sorting Assistant

💰 Salary

📝 Exam Pattern

StageModeAssessment
Tier ICBTObjective-type

📖 English

Grammar, Vocabulary, Error Detection, Sentence Improvement, Comprehension.

💰 Salary & Pay Level Explained

Understand the Central Government pay structure for SSC posts.

📊 Pay Level System

Central Government uses a Pay Level system (Level 1 to 18) based on the 7th Pay Commission. SSC posts typically range from Level 1 to Level 8.

  • Level 1: ₹18,000 – ₹18,800
  • Level 2: ₹19,900 – ₹20,800
  • Level 3: ₹21,700 – ₹22,700
  • Level 4: ₹25,500 – ₹26,600
  • Level 5: ₹29,200 – ₹30,500
  • Level 6: ₹35,400 – ₹37,000
  • Level 7: ₹44,900 – ₹46,900
  • Level 8: ₹47,600 – ₹49,700
📌 DA, HRA, TA and other allowances are added to the basic pay as per Government rules.

🏛️ Post-wise Pay Levels

  • CGL: ASO (Level 7), Inspector (Level 7), Auditor (Level 4), Tax Assistant (Level 4)
  • CHSL: LDC / JSA (Level 2), DEO (Level 4)
  • MTS: Multi-Tasking Staff (Level 1), Havaldar (Level 3)
  • GD: Constable (Level 3)
  • CPO: Sub-Inspector (Level 6)
  • Stenographer: Grade C (Level 6), Grade D (Level 4)
  • JE: Junior Engineer (Level 6–7)
  • Selection Post: Level 1–6 (post-dependent)
  • Hindi Translator: Level 6–7
⚠️ Exact pay level may vary by department and notification. Always verify latest official details.

🧠 PYQ + Mock Test Strategy

How to leverage previous year questions and mocks effectively.

📂 PYQ Strategy

  • Solve at least 10–15 years of previous year papers.
  • Analyze topic-wise weightage and question patterns.
  • Identify recurring topics and prioritize them.
  • Time yourself while solving PYQs to build speed.
  • Review mistakes and understand the correct approach.

📊 Mock Test Strategy

  • Take 1–2 full-length mocks per week initially, then increase frequency.
  • Simulate real exam conditions — time, environment, and section order.
  • Analyze each mock: section-wise scores, accuracy, time spent.
  • Focus on weak areas between mocks.
  • Track progress over time and adjust strategy accordingly.
