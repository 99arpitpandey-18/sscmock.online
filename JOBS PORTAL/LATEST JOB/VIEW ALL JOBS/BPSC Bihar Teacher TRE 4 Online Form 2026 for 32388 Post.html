📢 Important Note

  • BPSC OTR: One Time Registration is necessary only for those who want to appear in any BPSC recruitment in future. With the help of this registration, the candidate will save a lot of time while applying.
  • Candidates planning to apply for BPSC TRE 4.0 should complete BPSC OTR registration immediately to avoid delays when applications open.
  • Bihar STET / CTET Appearing Candidate are not eligible to fill form.
  • While applying, BSEB Unique ID number has to be written in place of Bihar STET Result Card Number.
  • Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) candidates will fill the serial number mentioned in the certificate/mark sheet in the form.
  • Do Not Need to Send Application Form to BPSC.
  • More Details Must Read the BPSC TRE 4.0 Notification.

📝 How to Apply for BPSC Bihar Teacher TRE 4.0 Vacancy 2026

  1. Visit the official website of BPSC (https://www.bpsc.bihar.gov.in/).
  2. Click on “BPSC Bihar Teacher TRE 4.0 2026 Apply Online” Link.
  3. Complete the BPSC OTR (One Time Registration) process first.
  4. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  5. Login using registration ID and password.
  6. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details.
  7. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.).
  8. Pay the application fee through online mode.
  9. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page.
  10. Candidate must read the BPSC TRE 4.0 Notification 2026 before applying.
Important Note: All the information given in this post is in short. Candidates who are thinking of applying should first read the complete notification and then apply.
