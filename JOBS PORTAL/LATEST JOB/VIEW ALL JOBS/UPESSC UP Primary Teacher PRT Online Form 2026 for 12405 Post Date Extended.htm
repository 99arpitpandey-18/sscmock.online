💼 UPESSC UP Primary Teacher Vacancy Details 2026: Total 12405 Post

Post Name Total Post Eligibility Criteria
Assistant Teacher in Urban Area Primary Schools 11508
  • Bachelor's degree from a university established by law in India, or a degree recognized as equivalent by the Government.
  • Any Government-recognized teacher training qualification: Basic Teacher Certificate (BTC), Two-year BTC (Urdu), Special BTC, Two-year Diploma in Special Education approved by the Rehabilitation Council of India, or Four-year degree in Elementary Education (B.El.Ed.), Two-year Diploma in Elementary Education as per NCTE Regulations, 2002.
  • Mandatory to pass the Teacher Eligibility Test (TET) conducted by the State Government or the Government of India.
  • Note: At present, the BTC course is being conducted under the name of D.El.Ed. course.
Assistant Teacher in Affiliated Primary Schools for Boys/Girls (Aided Schools) 897
  • Bachelor's degree from a university established by law in India or recognized by the Government, or from a deemed university/institution.
  • BTC (B.T.C.) or D.El.Ed. (D.L.Ed.) qualification from an NCTE-recognized course.
  • Must have successfully passed the Teacher Eligibility Test (T.E.T.) for Classes 1 to 5 conducted by the Government of Uttar Pradesh or the Government of India.

📝 How to Apply for UPESSC UP Primary Teacher Vacancy 2026

  1. Visit the official website of UPESSC (https://upessc.up.gov.in/).
  2. Click on “UPESSC UP Primary Teacher 2026 Apply Online” Link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number (OTR Registration).
  4. Login using registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.).
  7. Pay the application fee through online mode.
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page.
  9. Candidate must read the UPESSC UP Primary Teacher Notification 2026 before applying.
Important Note: All the information given in this post is in short. Candidates who are thinking of applying should first read the complete notification and then apply.
