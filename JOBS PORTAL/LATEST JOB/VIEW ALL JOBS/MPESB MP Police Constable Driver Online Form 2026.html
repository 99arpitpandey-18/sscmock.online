📝 How to Apply for MPESB MP Police Constable Driver Vacancy 2026

  1. Visit the official website of MPESB (https://esb.mp.gov.in/).
  2. Click on “MPESB MP Police Constable Driver 2026 Apply Online” Link.
  3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  4. Login using registration ID and password.
  5. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and category details.
  6. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature, etc.).
  7. Pay the application fee through online mode.
  8. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page.
  9. Candidate must read the MPESB MP Police Constable Driver Notification 2026 before applying.
Important Note: All the information given in this post is in short. Candidates who are thinking of applying should first read the complete notification and then apply.

📢 Important Note

  • Total 1000 Post — Special Forces 200 Post & District Police Force 800 Post.
  • 10th Pass under 10+2 system required (8th Pass for ST Category) along with Heavy Motor Vehicle (HMV) Driving License.

  • Application Fee: General/Other State Rs. 760/- and OBC/EWS/SC/ST Rs. 410/- (Portal Charges Rs. 60/- included).
