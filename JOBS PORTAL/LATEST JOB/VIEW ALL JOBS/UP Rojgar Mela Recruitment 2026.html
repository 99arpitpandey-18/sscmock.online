❓ UP Rojgar Mela Recruitment 2026 – Important FAQs

Q1. What is UP Rojgar Mela 2026?

✅ UP Rojgar Mela is a Job Fair organized by the Uttar Pradesh Employment Office (Sewayojan Portal) across various districts of Uttar Pradesh for both government (Samvida) and private job vacancies.

Q2. When will the UP Rojgar Mela 2026 be held?

✅ The Rojgar Mela dates are different for each district. Candidates are advised to check the district-wise Rojgar Mela date in the above table before applying.

Q3. How many vacancies are available in UP Rojgar Mela Recruitment 2026?

✅ Total vacancies are District Wise and may increase or decrease at any time. Candidates are advised to check the official website on the day of Rojgar Mela.

Q4. Is there any application fee for UP Rojgar Mela 2026?

✅ No, there is no application fee for any category (General / OBC / EWS / SC / ST / PH / Female).

Q5. How can I apply online for UP Rojgar Mela Recruitment 2026?

✅ To apply online, candidates need to: Visit the official Sewayojan Portal (https://sewayojan.up.nic.in/), Complete the registration process, Fill the online application form carefully, Upload required documents, photo & signature, Submit and print the final application form for future reference.

Q6. Who can apply for UP Rojgar Mela Recruitment 2026?

✅ Candidates who fulfill the required educational qualification (High School / 10+2 / Graduation / PG / Engineering / Diploma / MBA / ITI or Any Higher) and age limit mentioned in the official notification can apply online for UP Rojgar Mela Vacancy 2026.
