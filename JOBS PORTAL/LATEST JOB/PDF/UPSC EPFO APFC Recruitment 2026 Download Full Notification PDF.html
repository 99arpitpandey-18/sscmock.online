APPENDIX-I: SCHEME, SYLLABUS & WEIGHTAGE

Scheme:

  • The test will be of two hours duration.
  • All questions will carry equal marks.
  • Objective type questions with multiple choices.
  • Medium: English and Hindi.
  • Negative Marking: 1/3rd of the marks assigned to that question will be deducted for wrong answers.

Syllabus:

  1. General English
  2. Indian Culture, Heritage and Freedom Movements
  3. Developmental Issues and Present Trends in Indian Economy
  4. Governance and Constitution of India
  5. General Science and Basic Knowledge of Computer Applications
  6. Elementary Mathematics, Statistics and General Mental Ability
  7. Industrial Relations, Labour Codes and Social Security in India
  8. Principles of Accountancy, Auditing and Insurance
  9. Current Events of National and International Importance

Weightage:

  • Recruitment Test (RT): 300 Marks
  • Interview: 100 Marks
  • Ratio: 75:25

Standard of Suitability for Interview:

  • General/EWS: 50 and above
  • OBC: 45 and above
  • SC/ST/PwBD: 40 and above
APPENDICES (Formats)
  • Appendix-III: Certificate regarding physical limitation in an examinee to write (for Scribe/Compensatory time).
  • Appendix-IV: Letter of Undertaking for Using Own Scribe (for candidates having 40% or more disability).
  • Appendix-V: Certificate for Person with Specified Disability Covered Under Section 2(s) but not 2(r) (less than 40% disability and difficulty in writing).
  • Appendix-VI: Letter of Undertaking by the person with specified disability covered under Section 2(s) but not 2(r).
