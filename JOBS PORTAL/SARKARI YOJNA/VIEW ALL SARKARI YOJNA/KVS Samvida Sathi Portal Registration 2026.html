❓ KVS Samvida Sathi Portal Recruitment 2026 – Important FAQs

Q1. What is the Last Date to apply for KVS Samvida Sathi Portal Recruitment 2026?

✅ The last date to submit the online application form for KVS Samvida Sathi Portal Recruitment 2026 is Not Available.

Q2. What is the purpose of this portal?

✅ This portal has been developed by Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan to facilitate online registration of eligible candidates for purely part-time contractual engagement in Kendriya Vidyalayas. Through this portal, Kendriya Vidyalayas may prepare a panel of eligible candidates for various contractual posts as per requirement.

Q3. Does submission of an application guarantee appointment?

✅ No. Submission of an application does not guarantee appointment. The portal is only meant for preparation of a panel of eligible candidates. Engagement may be offered only if a vacancy arises in the concerned Kendriya Vidyalaya.

Q4. What is the nature of contractual engagement?

✅ Any engagement offered through this panel will be: Purely part-time, Contractual in nature, Temporary and need-based. Such engagement does not confer any claim for regular appointment or continuation.

Q5. How can I apply online for KVS Samvida Sathi Portal Recruitment 2026?

✅ To apply online for Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan Recruitment 2026, candidates need to: Visit the official KVS Samvida Sathi Portal website, Complete the registration process, Fill the online application form carefully, Upload required documents, photo & signature, Submit and print the final application form for future reference.

Q6. Who can apply through this portal ?

✅ Candidates who meet the eligibility criteria prescribed by Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan for the respective contractual posts may apply through this portal. Applicants should carefully read the eligibility conditions and guidelines before submitting the application.

Q7. Can I apply for more than one Kendriya Vidyalaya?

✅ The portal may allow candidates to apply for multiple Kendriya Vidyalayas depending on the portal configuration and applicable guidelines. Candidates should select their preferences carefully while filling the form.

Q8. Can I modify my application after submission?

✅ Once the application is submitted, modification may not be permitted. Applicants are therefore advised to carefully review all details before final submission.

Q9. What documents are required during application?

✅ Applicants may be required to upload the following documents: Educational qualification certificates, Professional qualification certificates (if applicable), Experience certificates (if applicable), Identity proof, Passport-size photograph, Any other documents as specified.

Q10. Is there any application fee for registration on the portal?

✅ No Application Fees.
