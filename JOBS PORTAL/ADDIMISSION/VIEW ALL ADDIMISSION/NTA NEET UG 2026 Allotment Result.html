❓ NTA NEET UG 2026 – Important FAQs

Q1. What is NTA NEET UG 2026 Allotment Result date?

✅ NTA NEET UG 2026 Allotment Result has been declared. Post update date is 21-08-2026. Candidates can check the result on the official website.

Q2. Who conducts NEET UG 2026?

✅ The National Eligibility Cum Entrance Test (NEET UG) 2026 is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA).

Q3. When was the NEET UG 2026 exam held?

✅ The NEET UG 2026 exam was held on 03/05/2026. The Re Exam was conducted on 21/06/2026.

Q4. What is the Application Fee for NEET UG 2026?

✅ General: Rs. 1700/-, EWS/OBC NCL: Rs. 1600/-, SC/ST/PH (Divyang): Rs. 1000/-, Outside India: Rs. 9500/-. Payment Mode: Online Payment.

Q5. What is the age limit for NEET UG 2026?

✅ Minimum Age: 17 Years. Born on or before: 31/12/2009. For age relaxation, kindly read the official notification.

Q6. What is the eligibility for NEET UG 2026?

✅ Passed / Appearing 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology & English. Course: MBBS / BDS.
