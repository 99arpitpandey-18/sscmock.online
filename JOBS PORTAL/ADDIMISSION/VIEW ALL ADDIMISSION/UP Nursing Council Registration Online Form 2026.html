📝 How to Apply Uttar Pradesh UP Nursing Council Registration Form 2026

  1. Go to the official website of the U.P. Nurses & Midwives Council or directly access the online services portal at https://online.upnursescouncil.org/OnLineServices/
  2. Navigate to the Application Form for Nursing Registration section.
  3. U.P. Passout Students: (e.g., B.Sc. Nursing, M.Sc. Nursing, Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing, GNM, ANM)
  4. Other State Passout Students: Candidates who completed their nursing education outside UP and are registered with another state’s nursing council
  5. Army Students: Candidates passing out from Army boards.
  6. Provide your personal details (Name, Father’s Name, Mother’s Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Aadhaar Number, and Contact Information) accurately.
  7. Enter your academic and training institution details (Training Center code, university/examining body, month/year of joining, and passing).
  8. Verify all entered details carefully before proceeding, as edits are typically not permitted once the payment stage is reached.
  9. Complete the payment via the integrated online digital payment gateway on the portal.
  10. Keep a note of your Reference Number and transaction details for future tracking.
  11. After successful payment submission, generate and take a clear colored printout of your final Application Registration Form.
  12. Candidate Read the UP Nursing Council Registration Form Notification 2026 Before Apply the UP Nursing Council Application Form 2026 in SSC MOCKⒶ Latest UP Council Section.
