📥 How to Download Bihar Vidhan Parishad 2026 Admit Card

  1. First Open Bihar Legislative Council Secretariat Official Website (https://vidhanparishad.bihar.gov.in/)
  2. Go to Download Admit Card Section.
  3. Enter All Necessary Details Such as Application Number / Registration Number / Password / Date of Birth.
  4. Login through with Required Details.
  5. After entering the details your admit card will be displayed.
  6. Finally Download your 2026 Admit Card.
  7. Take a Printout for Colour or B&W as per Examination Instructions.

✅ Candidates can download the admit card by logging in with their Registration Number / Roll Number and Date of Birth or Password on the official website https://vidhanparishad.bihar.gov.in/.

Q3. What is the Bihar Vidhan Parishad Exam Date 2026?

✅ The official Bihar Vidhan Parishad Exam Date 2026 is printed on the Admit Card. Candidates should check their admit card for the exact exam date and time.

Q4. What details are mentioned on the Bihar Vidhan Parishad Admit Card?
