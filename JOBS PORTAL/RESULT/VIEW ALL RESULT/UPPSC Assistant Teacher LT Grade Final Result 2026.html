📥 How to Download UPPSC Assistant Teacher LT Grade Final Result 2026

  1. Visit the official website of UPPSC (https://uppsc.up.nic.in/).
  2. Click on the “Results” / “Final Result” section available on the homepage.
  3. Find the subject-wise link — Sanskrit or Home Science — under "Download Final Result".
  4. Click on the subject link to open the result PDF.
  5. Search your Roll Number / Name using Ctrl+F in the PDF.
  6. Check your qualifying status and other details carefully.
  7. Download and save the Final Result PDF to your device.
  8. Take a clear printout of the result for document verification and future recruitment stages.
  9. Keep your login credentials and downloaded result safe until the recruitment process is completed.
Important Note: All the information given in this post is in short. Candidates must read the complete official notification and result notice carefully.
