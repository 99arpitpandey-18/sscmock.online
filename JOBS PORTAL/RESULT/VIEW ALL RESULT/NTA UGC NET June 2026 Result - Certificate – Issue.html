📥 How to Download UGC NET June 2026 Result

  1. Visit the official University Grants Commission (UGC) website at https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in/
  2. Click on the “Recruitment” or “Result” section available on the homepage.
  3. Find and open the “NTA UGC NET Result 2026” link.
  4. Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth or Roll Number, if required.
  5. Complete the CAPTCHA code or other security verification, if prompted.
  6. Click on the Submit button to view your NTA UGC NET June 2026 Result.
  7. Verify your responses and compare them with the official answer key.
  8. Download the UGC NET June 2026 Result PDF for future reference.
  9. If the objection window is available, submit your objections within the prescribed dates along with the required fee (if applicable).
  10. Keep a copy of the downloaded answer key for future reference until the recruitment process is completed.

❓ NTA UGC NET June 2026 Result – Important FAQs

Q1. What is NTA UGC NET June 2026 Result date?

✅ NTA UGC NET June 2026 Re-Exam Result and Certificate Issue has been released on 24/09/2026. Candidates can check the result from the official website or through the direct link given above.

Q2. How can I download UGC NET June 2026 Result?

✅ To download UGC NET June 2026 Result, visit the official website https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in/, click on the Result section, enter your Application Number and Date of Birth, submit and download the result PDF.

Q3. What is the UGC NET June 2026 Re-Exam Date?

✅ UGC NET June 2026 Re-Exam Date was 09 Sep 2026 for English & Commerce and 10 Sep 2026 for Sociology.

Q4. What is the eligibility for UGC NET June 2026?

✅ Candidates must have a Master Degree in Any Subject with 55% Marks from any Recognized University in India. More details read the official notification.

Q5. What is the age limit for UGC NET June 2026?

✅ The minimum age is 21 Years and maximum age is 30 Years. For age relaxation, kindly read the notification.

Q6. What is the UGC NET 2026 Exam Pattern?

✅ UGC NET 2026 exam will be conducted in Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode. There are 2 papers, 150 questions, 300 marks, and duration is 3 hours. There is no negative marking.
