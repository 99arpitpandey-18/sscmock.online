📥 How to Check UP Police Constable Final Result 2026

  1. Visit the official UP Police Recruitment & Promotion Board website at https://uppbpb.gov.in/.
  2. Click on the “Results” or “Final Result” link available on the homepage.
  3. Find and click on the “UP Police Constable Final Result 2026” link.
  4. Enter your Registration Number / Roll Number and Date of Birth, as required.
  5. Complete the CAPTCHA code or other security verification, if prompted.
  6. Click on the Submit / Login button to view your Final Result.
  7. Check your name, roll number, category, and qualifying status in the PDF.
  8. Download the UP Police Constable Final Result 2026 PDF to your device.
  9. Take a clear printout for document verification (DV/PST) and future recruitment stages.
  10. Keep your login credentials and downloaded result safe until the recruitment process is completed.
