📝 How to Download DSSSB Result 2026

  1. First Open Delhi Subordinate Service Selection Board Official Website (http://dsssb.delhi.gov.in/).
  2. Go to Download Result Section.
  3. Enter All Necessary Details Such as Application Number / Registration Number / Password / Date of Birth.
  4. Login through with Required Details.
  5. After entering the details your Result will be displayed.
  6. Finally Download your 2026 Result.
  7. Take a Printout for Colour or B&W as per Examination Instructions.
Important Note: All the information given in this post is in short. Candidates who are thinking of applying should first read the complete notification and then apply.

❓ DSSSB Result 2026 – Important FAQs

Q1. When was the DSSSB Result 2026 declared?

✅ The DSSSB Result 2026 was declared on 13/10/2026. Candidates can check their results on the official website.

Q2. How can I check my DSSSB Assistant Teacher Result 2026?

✅ To check the result, visit the official website, go to the Download Result section, enter your Application Number/Registration Number and Date of Birth, and submit. Your result will be displayed on the screen.

Q3. What is the next step after qualifying DSSSB Result 2026?

✅ Candidates who have qualified in the DSSSB exam will be called for the next stage (e-Dossier / Document Verification). Information regarding this will be available on the official website.

Q4. Where can I find the direct link to download the DSSSB Result?

✅ The direct links to download the result for various post codes are provided in the "Some Useful Important Links" section above.

Q5. Is this a government job result?

✅ Yes, DSSSB is a government organization under the Government of NCT of Delhi, and these are government job results.
