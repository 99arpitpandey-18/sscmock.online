📝 How to Download UPSESSB UP TGT 2022 Admit Card 2026

  1. First Open UP Secondary Education Service Selection Board Official Website
  2. Go to Download Admit Card Section.
  3. Enter All Necessary Details Such as Application Number / Registration Number / Password / Date of Birth.
  4. Login through with Required Details.
  5. After entering the details your admit card will be displayed.
  6. Finally Download your 2026 Admit Card.
  7. Take a Printout for Colour or B&W as per Examination Instructions.
Note: After the release of the admit card, candidates must check their details like – candidate’s name, father’s name / mother’s name, date of birth, gender, roll number, date of application, exam date and time, exam center code and exam address. If there is any mistake, then immediately contact UP Secondary Education Service Selection Board Recruitment Help desk.
