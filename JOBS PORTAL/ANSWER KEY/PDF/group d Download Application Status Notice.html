IMPORTANT NOTICE
Attention to the Candidates of CEN No.09/2025 Recruitment for the Various posts of Level 1 of 7th CPC
Notice on Application Status
Notice Details

Vide CEN No. 09/2025 (Various posts of Level 1 of 7th CPC) issued on 31.01.2026, applications were invited for the various posts of Level 1 of 7th CPC. The scrutiny of the applications has been completed and candidates can view status of their applications by logging in with their user credentials at www.rrbapply.gov.in.

Application Status Categories
(i) Provisionally accepted
(ii) Conditionally accepted with conditions
(iii) Rejected (Along with reasons for rejection)
  • The link will be live from 09-07-2026 (00:00 hours).
  • Regarding application status, SMS and email will be sent to the candidate’s registered mobile number and email ID mentioned in the application.
Important Instructions
  • While every care has been taken in preparing the list of provisionally eligible candidates, RRB reserves the right to rectify any inadvertent error or typographical / printing mistakes.
  • RRB regrets inability to entertain any correspondence from ineligible/rejected candidates.
  • Candidatures of all the eligible/accepted candidates is purely provisional and their candidature is liable to be cancelled at any stage of recruitment process or thereafter, in case of any inconsistency/deficiency/falsified record or data furnished by them in their application or any malpractice on the part of candidates coming to the notice of RRB at any stage of the recruitment process.
  • Candidates are advised to refer only to the official websites of RRBs for latest updates on recruitment process. Please do not be misled by unauthenticated sources.
