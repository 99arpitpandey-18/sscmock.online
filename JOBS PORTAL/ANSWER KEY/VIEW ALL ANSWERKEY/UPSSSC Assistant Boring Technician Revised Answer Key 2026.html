❓ UPSSSC Assistant Boring Technician Recruitment 2026 – Important FAQs

Q1. What is the last date to apply for UPSSSC Assistant Boring Technician Recruitment 2026?

✅ The last date to submit the online application form for UPSSSC Assistant Boring Technician Recruitment 2026 is 05/05/2026. Candidates are advised to apply before the deadline to avoid server issues.

Q2. How many vacancies are available in UPSSSC Assistant Boring Technician Recruitment 2026?

✅ According to the notification issued by UPSSSC, there are 402 Post for Assistant Boring Technician job.

Q3. What is the Qualification for UPSSSC Assistant Boring Technician Recruitment 2026?

✅ Candidates must have passed Class 10th (High School) and possess an ITI Certificate in Machine Mechanic, Fitter, Wireman, or Turner. Additionally, a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card is required.

Q4. What is the Salary of UPSSSC Assistant Boring Technician in 2026?

✅ The salary for UPSSSC Assistant Boring Technician is Level-2 (₹19,900 – ₹63,200). The in-hand salary is approximately ₹25,000 – ₹30,000 per month, including DA, HRA, and other allowances.

Q5. How can I apply online for UPSSSC Assistant Boring Technician Recruitment 2026?

✅ To apply online, candidates need to: Visit the official UPSSSC website, Login using PET 2025 registration details, Fill in the application form, Upload required documents, Pay the application fee, and Submit and print the final form.

Q6. What is the selection process for UPSSSC Assistant Boring Technician Recruitment 2026?

✅ The selection will be made on the basis of Written Examination (Mains). Candidates should refer to the official notification for complete details.
