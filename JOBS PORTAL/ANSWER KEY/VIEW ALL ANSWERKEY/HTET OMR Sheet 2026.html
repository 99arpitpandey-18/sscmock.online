📝 How to Download HTET 2025 Result 2026

  1. Visit the official Haryana Teacher Eligibility Test website at https://htet.eapplynow.com/
  2. Click on the “Results” section available on the homepage.
  3. Find and open the “HTET 2025 Result 2026” link.
  4. Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth or Roll Number, as required.
  5. Complete the CAPTCHA code or other security verification, if prompted.
  6. Click on the Submit button to view your Haryana Teacher Eligibility Test Result 2026.
  7. Check your name, roll number, qualifying status, and marks displayed on the result page.
  8. Download the HTET Result 2026 PDF or scorecard to your device.
  9. Take a clear printout of the result for document verification and future recruitment stages.
  10. Keep your login credentials and downloaded result safe until the recruitment process is completed.

❓ HTET OMR Sheet 2026 – Important FAQs

Q1. What is the exam date for HTET 2025?

✅ The HTET 2025 exam was held on 04-05 July 2026.

Q2. When was the HTET Answer Key released?

✅ The HTET Answer Key was released on 06/07/2026.

Q3. What are the application fees for HTET 2025?

✅ General/OBC/Other State: Rs. 1000 (1 or 2), Rs. 1800 (Both), Rs. 2400 (Triple). SC/ST/PH: Rs. 500 (1 or 2), Rs. 900 (Both), Rs. 1200 (Triple).

Q4. What is the eligibility for Level 1 (Primary)?

✅ Intermediate with 50% Marks and Passed / Appearing 2 Year Diploma in Elementary Education / Special Education / B.E.Ed OR Intermediate with 45% Marks and Passed / Appearing 2 Year Diploma in Elementary Education / Special Education / B.E.Ed OR Graduation Degree in Any Stream and Passed / Appearing 2 Year Diploma in Elementary Education / Special Education / B.E.Ed.

Q5. What is the eligibility for Level 2 (TGT)?

✅ Degree in Any Stream with 50% Marks and 2 Year Diploma in Education / Elementary Education OR Graduation Degree with 50% Marks and B.Ed / Special B.Ed Degree OR Intermediate with 50% Marks and 4 Year BA B.Ed / B.Com B.Ed Degree.

Q6. How can I download the HTET OMR Sheet 2026?

✅ To download the HTET OMR Sheet 2026, candidates need to: Visit the official HTET website, Click on the "Results" or "OMR Sheet" section, Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth, Complete the CAPTCHA, Click on Submit to view the OMR Sheet, Download the PDF for future reference.
