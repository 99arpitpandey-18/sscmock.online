❓ UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains 2026 – Important FAQs

Q1. When was the UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains Exam conducted?

✅ The UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains Exam was conducted on 23/08/2026.

Q2. When will the UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains Answer Key be released?

✅ The UPSSSC Lower PCS Mains Answer Key will be released on 24/08/2026 on the official website.

Q3. How many vacancies are available in UPSSSC Lower PCS Recruitment 2026?

✅ According to the notification issued by UPSSSC, there are 2587 Post for UPSSSC Lower PCS job.

Q4. What is the Age Limit for UPSSSC Lower PCS in 2026?

✅ The minimum age is 18 Years and the maximum age is 40 Years (as on 01/07/2026). Age relaxation is applicable as per rules.

Q5. What is the Application Fee for UPSSSC Lower PCS 2026?

✅ The application fee is Rs. 25/- for all categories (General/OBC/EWS/SC/ST). Payment can be made through online mode.

Q6. What is the eligibility for UPSSSC Lower PCS Recruitment 2026?

✅ Candidates must have a UPSSSC PET 2025 Score Card and a Bachelor Degree in any Stream from a recognized university. For Executive Officer post, Graduation with 'O' Level is required. For post-wise eligibility, kindly read the official notification.
