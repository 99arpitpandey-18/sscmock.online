📝 How to Download UPSSSC ASO Result 2026

  1. Visit the official Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC)
  2. Click on the “Results” section available on the homepage.
  3. Find and open the “UPSSSC ASO Result 2026″
  4. The result can be checked either by logging in with your Application Number and Date of Birth/Roll Number, as required, or by downloading the result PDF, if provided.
  5. If the login option is available, enter your required credentials.
  6. Complete the CAPTCHA or other security verification, if prompted.
  7. Click on the Submit/Login button to view your UPSSSC ASO Assistant Statistical Officer & Assistant Research Officer Result 2026.
  8. If a Result PDF is provided instead, download and open the PDF and search for your Name or Roll Number.
  9. Check your Name, Roll Number, Qualifying Status, and Marks, if mentioned.
  10. Download and save the UPSSSC ASO Result 2026 PDF/Scorecard for future reference.
  11. Take a clear printout of the result for document verification and further recruitment stages.
  12. Keep your login credentials and downloaded result safe until the recruitment process is completed.
