📋 Eligibility Criteria (2026) — 4 Categories

🎓 Agniveer General Duty (GD)

Age: 17.5 – 22 years (as on 01 Jul 2026)

Education: Class 10 pass with 45% aggregate & 33% in each subject

Height: 170 cm (Western Plains) / 163 cm (Western Himalayan) / 160 cm (Eastern Himalayan)

Chest: 77 cm unexpanded + 5 cm expansion

🔧 Agniveer Technical

Age: 17.5 – 22 years (as on 01 Jul 2026)

Education: 12th pass in Science (PCM) with 50% aggregate & 40% in each subject OR 10th pass with 2-year ITI course

Height: 162 cm (minimum, all regions)

Chest: 77 cm + 5 cm expansion

📚 Agniveer Clerk / SKT

Age: 17.5 – 22 years (as on 01 Jul 2026)

Education: 12th pass in any stream with 60% aggregate & 50% in each subject (English & Maths/Accounts compulsory)

Height: 162 cm (all regions)

Chest: 77 cm + 5 cm expansion

🔨 Agniveer Tradesman

Age: 17.5 – 22 years (as on 01 Jul 2026)

Education: 10th pass with 33% each subject OR 8th pass with 33% each subject

Height: Region-wise (same as GD: 170 cm / 163 cm / 160 cm)

Chest: 77 cm + 5 cm expansion

Age relaxation (2026): Upper age limit increased from 21 to 22 years for all Agniveer posts. Age is calculated as on 01 July 2026 (born between 01 Jul 2004 and 01 Jul 2009).

🩺 Medical & Vision Standards

👁️ Vision

  • Distant Vision (GD): 6/6 in better eye, 6/9 in worse eye (without glasses).
  • Color Vision: CP II / CP III — must recognise white, red, green. Colour blindness is permanent rejection.
  • No squint or night blindness.

👂 Hearing & Dental

  • Hearing: Forced whisper from 610 cm in each ear.
  • Dental: Minimum 14 dental points, healthy gums.
  • No chronic ear infections or perforated eardrums.

🦵 Orthopaedic

  • No knock knees or flat foot.
  • Carry angle 15–18 degrees.
  • No spinal deformities (scoliosis).

❤️ General Health

  • Chest X‑ray: clear of TB.
  • ECG: normal heart rhythm.
  • No major surgical scars affecting function.

📝 Selection Process

Stage 1: Common Entrance Exam (CEE)

Computer‑Based Test covering General Knowledge, Science, Maths, Reasoning, and English. No negative marking.

Stage 2: Recruitment Rally

Includes Physical Fitness Test (PFT), Physical Measurement Test (PMT), and Medical Examination.

Stage 3: Document Verification

Original certificates, Aadhaar, domicile, category certificate (if applicable), and character certificate.
