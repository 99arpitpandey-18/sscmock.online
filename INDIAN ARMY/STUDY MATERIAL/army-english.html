📖 Chapter 1 — English Grammar

Grammar is the foundation of English. Competitive examinations such as SSC CGL, CHSL, CPO, GD, MTS, Banking and Railway exams regularly ask questions from Tenses, Articles, Prepositions, Subject-Verb Agreement, Voice, Narration, Pronouns, Conjunctions and Sentence Improvement.

Parts of Speech:
English has eight major parts of speech: Noun, Pronoun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb, Preposition, Conjunction and Interjection.
1. Noun

A noun is the name of a person, place, thing, animal or idea. हिंदी में इसे संज्ञा कहते हैं।

Examples:
Rahul is a student.
Delhi is a large city.
Honesty is a great quality.
  • Proper Noun — India, Rahul, Delhi
  • Common Noun — boy, city, country
  • Collective Noun — team, army, committee
  • Abstract Noun — honesty, bravery, wisdom
  • Material Noun — gold, silver, water
2. Pronoun

A pronoun is used instead of a noun to avoid repetition. Pronoun को संज्ञा के स्थान पर प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Example:
Rahul is a student. Rahul is intelligent. ❌
Rahul is a student. He is intelligent. ✅
  • Personal — I, we, you, he, she, they
  • Possessive — mine, yours, his, hers
  • Reflexive — myself, yourself, himself
  • Demonstrative — this, that, these, those
  • Relative — who, whom, which, that
  • Indefinite — someone, anybody, nobody
3. Verb

A verb shows an action, state or condition. Verb को क्रिया कहते हैं।

He runs every morning.
She is happy.
They played cricket.
  • Action Verb — run, write, eat, play
  • Linking Verb — is, am, are, seem, become
  • Helping Verb — is, have, do, will
  • Transitive Verb — takes an object
  • Intransitive Verb — does not require an object
4. Adjective

An adjective describes a noun or pronoun. यह noun या pronoun की विशेषता बताता है।

She is a beautiful girl.
He bought a new car.
It is a difficult question.
5. Adverb

An adverb modifies a verb, adjective or another adverb. यह बताता है कि कोई काम कैसे, कब या कहाँ हुआ।

He runs quickly.
She speaks softly.
He came yesterday.
6. Tenses

Tense tells us about the time of an action. Competitive exams में Tenses से बहुत अधिक questions पूछे जाते हैं।

Present Simple: Subject + V1 / V1+s/es
Example: He plays cricket.

Present Continuous: Subject + is/am/are + V-ing
Example: He is playing cricket.

Present Perfect: Subject + has/have + V3
Example: He has completed the work.

Present Perfect Continuous: has/have been + V-ing
Example: He has been studying for two hours.
Past Simple: Subject + V2
Example: She went home.

Past Continuous: was/were + V-ing
Example: She was reading.

Past Perfect: had + V3
Example: She had finished the work.

Future Simple: will + V1
Example: She will come tomorrow.

Future Perfect: will have + V3
Example: She will have completed the work.
7. Articles — A, An, The

Articles noun से पहले प्रयोग होते हैं। A और An indefinite articles हैं जबकि The definite article है।

A: consonant sound से पहले।
a boy, a university, a European

An: vowel sound से पहले।
an apple, an hour, an honest man

The: किसी specific व्यक्ति या वस्तु के लिए।
The sun, the Ganga, the Taj Mahal
8. Prepositions

Preposition noun या pronoun का संबंध दूसरे शब्द से बताता है।

Time:
at 5 PM
on Monday
in January

Place:
at the station
on the table
in the room
9. Subject-Verb Agreement

Subject और verb के number में agreement होना चाहिए। Singular subject के साथ singular verb और plural subject के साथ plural verb आता है।

He goes to school. ✅
They go to school. ✅

She doesn't like tea. ✅
They don't like tea. ✅
10. Active and Passive Voice

Active Voice में subject action करता है। Passive Voice में action पर emphasis होता है।

Active: Ram wrote a letter.
Passive: A letter was written by Ram.

Active: She is writing a book.
Passive: A book is being written by her.
11. Direct and Indirect Speech

Direct speech में speaker के exact words दिए जाते हैं। Indirect speech में speaker की बात को report किया जाता है।

Direct:
She said, "I am happy."

Indirect:
She said that she was happy.
12. Conjunction

Conjunction words, phrases या clauses को जोड़ता है।

Coordinating: and, but, or, so, yet, for, nor

Subordinating: because, although, since, if, unless, while

Correlative: either-or, neither-nor, both-and
13. Conditional Sentences
Zero Conditional:
If + Present, Present
Example: If you heat ice, it melts.

First Conditional:
If + Present, will + V1
Example: If you work hard, you will succeed.

Second Conditional:
If + Past, would + V1
Example: If I were rich, I would help others.

Third Conditional:
If + had + V3, would have + V3
Example: If he had worked hard, he would have succeeded.

📄 Chapter 5 — Reading Comprehension

Reading Comprehension में passage को ध्यान से पढ़कर उसके आधार पर questions solve किए जाते हैं। SSC level पर Main Idea, Inference, Vocabulary, Tone, Fact, Conclusion और Specific Detail पूछी जाती है।

Comprehension Strategy:
1. पहले passage को ध्यान से पढ़ें।
2. Main idea identify करें।
3. Important facts याद रखें।
4. Question को दोबारा पढ़ें।
5. Answer केवल passage के आधार पर दें।
Passage Practice:

Education plays an important role in the development of an individual and society. It does not simply mean acquiring information from books. True education develops the ability to think, analyse, communicate and make responsible decisions. An educated person is generally better prepared to understand social problems and contribute positively to society. In the modern world, education has become more accessible because of technology. Students can attend online classes, read digital books and use educational platforms from almost anywhere. However, technology alone cannot guarantee good education. Students must learn how to use digital resources properly and should develop the habit of checking whether information is reliable. Therefore, education should be considered a continuous process. Learning does not end when a person leaves school or college. People continue to learn through experience, reading, observation and interaction with others. A society that encourages lifelong learning is more capable of adapting to changing circumstances.

⚠️ Chapter 6 — Error Detection & Sentence Improvement

Error Detection में sentence के गलत part को identify करना होता है। सबसे common errors Subject-Verb Agreement, Tense, Article, Preposition, Pronoun, Conjunction और Comparison से आते हैं।

Common Errors:

He don't like tea. ❌
He doesn't like tea. ✅

She is a honest girl. ❌
She is an honest girl. ✅

He is senior than me. ❌
He is senior to me. ✅

She is more better than him. ❌
She is better than him. ✅

I have seen him yesterday. ❌
I saw him yesterday. ✅
